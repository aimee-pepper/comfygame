# Site-fragment corpus — current fifteen-site catalogue

**Status:** Authoritative first-slice prose for `found-writing-breadth-current.md`. Every line is safe
to encounter when the site first becomes visible and before its contents are searched.

## Authorship rule

These are anonymous traces from different people and periods after the sundering, plus older Binder
working fragments where the site supports them. They do not form one hidden diarist. A generated
record freezes the selected line ID, exact prose, site definition ID and site instance ID.

Lines describe visible construction, human use or immediate physical evidence only. They never name
hidden yields, an unseen guardian/species, analysis-only causes, search duration or a remote site.

## Exclusions and future lines

- The **Buried seam** remains a deep sign, not a generic writing host. Its pre-Grimmond and sounded
  observations stay in the Deep Works system rather than becoming anonymous site prose.
- Exhaustion-specific fragments may be added later, but only if their eligibility reads saved search
  state and the line describes visible depletion. The first slice needs no post-search pool.
- A future site must add at least two pre-search-safe lines before Site-fragment eligibility is
  enabled for its definition. Missing prose makes that site ineligible; it never borrows another
  family's line.
