# Traveller meeting voice revision packet — current

**Status:** exact-copy recommendation packet for Aimee's DEBUG atlas review; not promoted.  
**Scope:** the six highest-value corrections from
`traveller-meeting-voice-differentiation-audit-current.md`.  
**Source rule:** stable exchange IDs and scene facts remain unchanged. These candidates do not replace
the four authoritative review files until Aimee approves their exact text.

The goal is not to make the travellers less thoughtful. It is to stop every thoughtful person from
speaking in the same balanced thesis-and-correction cadence. Each revision leaves at least one
principle intact and moves the remaining weight into action, humour, interruption, memory or an
unresolved physical fact.
