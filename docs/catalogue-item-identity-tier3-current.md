## Semantic notes

- **Binder's Edge:** “a hand you almost recognise” is uncertainty, not a hidden identity reveal. Use
  authored-looking marks without letters, initials, portrait cues or a readable known script.
- **Vaultplate:** cold retention is material behavior. A separated inner layer may imply insulation;
  blue coloration, ice crystals or a temperature number cannot own the identity.
- **Someone's Ring:** emotional importance comes from scale, wear and the kept-object presentation.
  Do not use a heart, memorial portrait or ownership initial that invents a relationship.
- **Longstriders:** the item may suggest distance but cannot promise extra map movement unless rules
  actually grant it. Avoid a literal map, route arrow or speed-stat badge in the object art.
