## 2. SKY-01 · Bright and dark sky

**Goal:** Find the background light that will make entering a written world feel special.

Paint one bright treatment and one dark treatment, keeping clouds and landscape separate. Preserve a readable light-to-dark structure that can receive different colour palettes. Colour or grayscale studies are both welcome; a flat grey fill is unnecessary.

Think of bright and dark lighting rather than mandatory Earth day and night. Worlds can have constant or sourceless illumination, so leave out suns, moons, stars, and other celestial objects for now. Avoid a painted ground horizon that would force every landscape into the same shape.

**What to share:** your editable source and an original-size PNG preview of each treatment. Choose a comfortable study canvas and note its pixel dimensions. These are exploratory sketches, so there is no required final width or height yet. Keep the original rather than resizing it to an interim preview.

**Enough for a first review:** two treatments that let us discuss mood, shading, and how much attention the sky draws from the landscape.

## 3. SKY-02 · Separate cloud shapes

**Goal:** Change the weather and character of the sky without repainting its background.

Start with whichever shape sounds fun:

- **Wisps:** thin, open shapes with plenty of sky between them.
- **Scattered clouds:** distinct masses with clear silhouettes.
- **Overcast:** broad connected cover with enough shading to avoid a flat slab.
- **Storm clouds:** heavier masses with a stronger underside.

Keep each cloud separate from the sky, with transparent space around it. Preserve highlights and shadows so tint can change its colour without flattening its volume. Keep rain, snow, and haze out of these images; they will be separate treatments. Stormy-looking clouds do not automatically mean a damaging weather effect.

**What to share:** editable layers and an original-size transparent RGBA PNG for each shape. Choose your own study canvas and record its dimensions. Related layers should retain the same canvas and alignment. There is no tiling or animation requirement for these sketches.

**Enough for a first review:** one example of each family you choose. Additional variants can wait until we see which shapes the scene needs.

## 4. LIB-01 · Recolourable books and papers

Continue the Library files already being authored. Keep your existing names and originals; this list does not add another redraw or ask you to replace completed artwork.

For independently recolourable areas, keep separate layers and retain their shading. Related overlays should keep their matching component's canvas and alignment. Share the files when you are happy with them, or bring a work in progress if you want feedback.

## 5. Agreed colour direction

**Intended visual direction agreed with Aimee on 4 September 2026.**

- Grass belongs to the foliage colour family visually; this does not mean every decorative blade must be harvestable.
- Worlds use coordinated generated palettes with separate foliage, water, and sky colours.
- A specifically assigned shade takes precedence for that element.
- A world's colours stay consistent when returning to it or reopening the game.
- Tint preserves shading and recognizable material cues. Unusual water still reads as water through its reflections, shore edges, and surface patterns; colour alone does not declare a hazard.
- Material-led equipment keeps consistent silhouettes and shading, with every material component region receiving its selected tint.
- Equipment quality appears in the item-name highlight colour and the square thumbnail border, not in the item artwork.
- Potions, remedies, and similar supplies retain their recognizable authored colours.

## 6. What the final export brief will provide

Before **SKY-03** becomes ready, Asset Lead will add exact canvas dimensions, the visible crop, transparent margins, placement and alignment, colour-layer requirements, PNG format, and any movement or tiling requirements. You do not need to guess these or repaint sources to fit an arbitrary size.

We will review how the selected sky and clouds work together before requesting more variants. Tinting creates colour variety; additional silhouettes, shorelines, and terrain transitions will still need their own briefs.
