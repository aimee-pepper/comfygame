# Current Design — Perren and Nine Nine-Condition Signatures

**Status:** authored-direction profiles with playtest thresholds. These are interpretive endgame hunts
that deliberately use an established late vocabulary. Neither requires its owner's diary focus.

## Perren — a world that makes conflict look authored

Perren's location asks the player to write several genuine opposed pressures. This is not because he
is “the contradiction person”; it reflects his experience recognising instructions that make a
conclusion feel self-discovered. The world contains competing claims whose neat frame is itself
suspicious.

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Illumination opposed | ≥ 20 | “The light is being made and smothered at once; neither account explains the whole sky.” | Sun/Crystal against Cloud/Void |
| Thermal opposed | ≥ 20 | “Heat and cold press into the same interval instead of taking orderly turns.” | Sun/Magma against Ice/Void |
| Vitality opposed | ≥ 15 | “Growth and suppression are both active; the bare patches are not simply places life never reached.” | Root/Bloom against Miasma/Sulfur |
| Relief tag | `ruined` present | “The ground has been shaped, broken and used as evidence for more than one history.” | Edren's earlier Ruin |
| Relief openness | ≤ 35 | “Every route is framed tightly enough that the next choice can be mistaken for the only one.” | Great Granite/Ruin/Chasm |
| Substrate dispersion | ≥ 70 | “The worked material is spread so evenly that a chosen sample can be made to stand for the whole.” | Ruin plus Sand/Silt combinations |
| Atmosphere clarity | ≤ 40 | “Distance removes context before it removes the object being seen.” | Mist/Miasma |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 85 | “The same interval repeats until recurrence begins to resemble proof.” | Orrery |
| Hydrology salinity | ≥ 45 | “What withdraws leaves a visible edge, as though every boundary had always been there.” | Sea/Salt |

**Reward dependency:** never requires Mirror, Perren's diary focus.

**Risk boundary:** opposed pressure creates instability through the existing contradiction model. The
signature does not require Negate qualifiers, false clues or a special cult-world rule.

## Nine — continuity made from chosen repetitions

Nine's world has strong changes that do not arrive regularly, while several local conditions remain
stable enough to verify. It supports her present identity: continuity is a practice of checking what
holds, not recovering one perfect autobiographical sequence.

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Cycle regularity | ≤ 35 | “No interval arrives when the previous one taught me to expect it.” | Two earlier Drift marks offset Tide's regularity |
| Cycle amplitude | ≥ 65 | “When the change comes, it is large enough to divide one account from the next.” | Tide combined with Drift |
| Illumination tag | `sourceless` present | “A little light persists without revealing what is responsible for it.” | Crystal/Fungus |
| Thermal range | ≤ 12 | “Temperature is one of the few things that does not argue with the last entry.” | Sea buffering/even thermal writing |
| Hydrology dispersion | ≥ 70 | “Water reaches nearly every route, though never at the same point twice.” | Rain |
| Substrate dispersion | ≥ 70 | “The ground repeats its material without preserving the same surface.” | Sand/Ruin/Silt |
| Relief openness | 45–65 | “The horizon is present but bounded; I can check it without mistaking it for an escape.” | Balanced Granite/Wind/Ruin |
| Vitality produced | ≥ 40 | “New growth is visible beside what the last interval damaged.” | Root + Bloom |
| Atmosphere motion | ≤ 20 | “A loose marker remains where I left it even when my sense of duration does not.” | Hush |

**Reward dependency:** never requires Dream, Nine's diary focus.

## Validation notes

- Engineering tightened Nine's reversible Thermal range placeholder from ≤20 to ≤12 after the live
  distribution fixture found the former true in 87% of ordinary generated worlds; the authored
  stable-reference identity and Sea/even-thermal route are unchanged.

- Perren is allowed to be deliberately unstable because opposed pressures are the authored challenge,
  but his match must remain survivable enough to reach him. Instability belongs in the fixture audit.
- Perren's clues report simultaneous causes; they never lie or ask the Library to translate prose into
  pressure names.
- Nine uses Drift only because Tovin is earlier. This is a relationship dependency, not duplicated
  teaching ownership.
- Tide raises both amplitude and regularity, so the deliberate route uses two Drift marks with Tide;
  one Drift plus Tide does not satisfy the low-regularity clue.
- Nine's low Cycle regularity and high amplitude are compatible: changes are large and unpredictably
  timed. Her low thermal range supplies a deliberate stable reference rather than cancelling Cycle.
