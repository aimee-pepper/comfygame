# The Cast

**The problem:** *"half the game is supposed to be seeking these people out"* — and there are **six travellers**, two of whom unlock anything, against **thirteen specced buildings** and **twelve specced classes**. The maximum signature is four conditions when a late-game page holds twelve.

---

## 1. How many conditions a hand can write

A 6×6 page is 36 cells. A condition is a cluster — subject + focus + modifier, three sigils.

| Hand | Cells/sigil | Cells/condition | **Conditions** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Charcoal** | 4–6 | ~15 | **2** |
| **Pencil** | 2–3 | ~8 | **5** |
| **Fountain pen** | 1 | 3 | **12** |

**Compounds add more** — a three-focus compound saves ~3 cells at pencil, which is another condition on a full page.

**So signatures should span 2 → 10+.** Topping out at four means the late game has no hunt in it: nothing is hard enough to need the hand and the vocabulary you spent the whole game earning.

## 3. What a companion should give — four things

| | What | Built? |
|---|---|---|
| **1. A class** | Their pre-sundering trade: stat lean, combat role, starting skill | ❌ |
| **2. A building** | And the ability to build it (session 12) | ⚠️ 2 of 6 |
| **3. A diary rune** | The precise word they knew best — **exclusive**, unbuyable | ❌ |
| **4. A research branch** | Their building's own tree (Q40) | ❌ |

**The through-line: what they teach is what they knew.** A surveyor gives you words for land, a smith a word for metal, a binder a word for time. **That is why nobody else can sell it to you.**

**Fighter companions give 1, 3 and 4 without a building** — a branch of combat technique rather than a trade.

## 5. Phase and difficulty, together

| Phase | Hand | Conditions | Vocabulary they need |

| **Opening** | charcoal | **1–2** | starting only — Mara, Edren, Halloway |
| **Start of mid** | charcoal | **2** | **one non-starting focus** — Isolde, and the cap is absolute |

| **Late** | fountain pen | **6–9** | precise focuses, compounds, rare modifiers |

**The rule: a signature must fit the page the player would plausibly have when they reach that person** — and must be writable with vocabulary obtainable by then.

## 7. How they're earned — unchanged

**Named travellers cost nothing to recruit** (Q39) — the hunt was the price. **Random companions and animals cost a want.**

**With vocabulary as the gate, "the hunt was the price" is now literally true:** finding Isolde requires having learned Crystal; finding Tovin requires Fungus; finding the Emanant requires whatever writes an emanation. **The cost is vocabulary**, which is the currency the whole game runs on.

## 8. The cast order is the pity order

Because pity targets **the next rune for the next companion** (Aimee's correction), the system must know **who is next**. So the order in §4–5 has to be **authored**, not emergent from condition counts.

**[PROPOSAL]** an explicit ordering field per traveller, and a test asserting that every traveller's conditions are writable with the vocabulary obtainable by their position — the generalisation of the Isolde reachability test, which currently only guards her.

### 9.4 Sela's `openness ≥ 65` — verified reachable

**Openness baseline is 60.** She needs +5.

Wind gives **+10**, Sand **+25**, Sea **+30**, Marsh **+10** — all starting vocabulary. **Comfortably reachable, with margin.**

Her three conditions are all starter-writable; what places her at mid is the **page**, not the words — three conditions needs the pencil. **Which puts her correctly after Isolde.**

**Eight conditions, fountain pen, most of the late vocabulary.** That's a hunt.

### 9.6 No traveller is unrecruitable — [AIMEE]

**Every named traveller can be recruited.** The met-only endgame figure is withdrawn.

**Findable NPCs who aren't companions — merchants, residents of named places, people who only ever give you a clue — are a separate idea to revisit later**, and shouldn't be conflated with the cast.

### 9.7 How random companions differ

| | **Named** | **Random** |
|---|---|---|
| **How found** | Hunted — a signature deduced from diary pages | Stumbled on, in a world written for other reasons |
| **Cost** | **Nothing** — the hunt was the price | **A want** — bring, find, show or reach something |
| **Class** | Authored, unique | From a small pool of simple classes |
| **Building** | Often | **Never** |
| **Diary** | Yes — pages, a hint page, an exclusive rune | **No diary at all** |
| **Voice** | Written | Generated from templates |
| **Stats & levels** | Same system | Same system |

**The distinction that matters: named travellers have *diaries*, random ones don't.** A diary is the whole search apparatus — pages, clues, a hint page, an exclusive rune, cross-references to other people. **Random companions are people you meet; named ones are people you look for.**

**[PROPOSAL] Random companions fill out the party and the postings** — you'll want more than twenty-eight bodies across five party slots, home stations and assigned worlds — **while named ones carry the buildings, the vocabulary and the story.**
