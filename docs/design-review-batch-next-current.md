# Next Batched Design Review — Coherence Cleanup

**Status:** resolved coherence-cleanup batch; retained as historical rationale. These items are
grouped because each removes a legacy or premature subsystem rather than adding more machinery.

## Resolved decisions

### DRQ-044 — cut Traveller's Token

The recommendation in `travellers-token-audit-current.md` is accepted. A complete signature already
places its traveller with certainty. Making the item work on an incomplete signature would make
their recovered clues false; secretly biasing the unwritten world would turn a consumable into an
opaque signature solver. Aimee's later field-avoidance request supplies a genuine replacement need:
use the animal-only **Scent Mask** direction in `field-awareness-avoidance-current.md`. It enters
because play exposed a preparation gap, not to preserve a count.

### DRQ-069 — keep the first realm job shallow — settled

Keep one chosen discovered renewable yield and the single visible Worldwork aptitude. The first
production loop already asks the player to choose whom to remove from Party/Home work, cover realm
sustain, select a useful renewable output, and collect a capped delivery. Separate miner/forager/
keeper jobs would add assignment bookkeeping before play has shown a second meaningful decision.
Add another job family only if players repeatedly leave assignments untouched because all choices
feel equivalent—not merely because the system can support more fields.

### DRQ-075 — ship the Menagerie without a hard cap — settled

Approve the current no-cap first slice. Animals already compete for the five-member travel party,
while collection is a long-campaign pleasure and should not become discard administration. Solve
eventual list pressure with grouping, search and filters first. A habitat/kennel limit should be
considered only if the collection itself creates real performance or comprehension trouble, and
should never force-delete a bonded animal.

### DRQ-087 — use Chill, retire Frost — settled

Approve **Chill** for direct dry cold. “Frost” asserts condensed/frozen water that the focus does not
produce, colliding with Hydrology and the proposed Water form vocabulary. Art and copy may show dry
cold through value, stillness or brittle material response, but not frost crystals unless water is
actually present.

### DRQ-098 — retire the separate Quirk layer and reroll

Use `quirk-pattern-audit-current.md`: retain paired opportunity/consequence as a system-wide
design principle, but remove the obsolete independently rolled Quirk catalogue and veto/reroll
affordance. Current page authorship, pressure resolution, Danger runes, greed and contradictions
already create attributable bargains. A second random modifier deck would double-charge worlds and
blur which written choices caused them. Future named world conditions remain possible only as
derived descriptions of resolved facts with a real consumer.

## Why this batch is low risk

All five calls protect an existing readable loop:

- truthful traveller deduction;
- one understandable anchored-realm assignment;
- attachment-friendly animal collection;
- pressure names that state what they actually do;
- consequences attributable to the written book.

None removes currently indispensable live player agency. Traveller's Token and the separate Quirk
deck are held/legacy proposals, extra realm jobs and Menagerie capacity were deliberately deferred,
and Chill is already the implementation-ready focus name. Historical proposals remain in the archive
and source audits after resolution.

## Disposition

DRQ-044 and DRQ-098 are settled removals. Their audit documents remain as history; held Traveller's
Token promises are removed from current crafting/status summaries; old stable IDs remain only where
save migration requires them. Engineering cleanup is an ordinary later batch and does not interrupt
active P1 work.

DRQ-069 is settled by Decisions 36 and 118 plus the current anchored-production specification;
DRQ-075 by Decision 69; and DRQ-087 by Decision 76. Their exact rates, collection ergonomics and
focus magnitudes remain playtest scope, but those do not reopen the structural choices.
