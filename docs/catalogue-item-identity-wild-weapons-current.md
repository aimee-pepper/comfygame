# Catalogue item identity — wild/apex weapons

**Status:** handmade-art semantic identity brief for all eight wild-only weapon IDs.  
**Scope:** native 32px catalogue identity; instance growth/state remains separate.  
**Authority:** exact mechanics remain `apex-system-audit.md` and live item data. Environmental
affinities affect reward weights, not icon palette or anatomy.

## Shared wild grammar

Wild weapons should look physically exceptional because their construction/material does something
ordinary forging cannot reproduce. They must not become one visual family of glowing, thorn-covered
or creature-skull weapons.

- The eight core silhouettes are authored and stable by exact ID.
- Environmental affinity, source world palette, apex species and rarity do not recolor or regenerate
  the identity.
- A legitimately persisted instance rule/state may add a separately reviewed bounded layer; it may
  not replace the base silhouette.
- Bleed, concealment, reach, ward and growth use physical geometry plus anchored detail. Color-only
  status coding and generic magic aura are forbidden.

## Rule-specific boundaries

- **Two-Natured Blade:** the shape may express Pierce/Rend duality. It does not show which damage will
  be chosen against a target; matchup remains rules-owned preview.
- **Long Fang:** the icon shows extraordinary tooth length, not a fake long haft. Its far reach is an
  identified item rule explained in detail.
- **Ranked Spear:** player-facing mechanics are **Throughstroke**. Do not draw enemy ranks, multiple
  bodies or formation arrows, and do not resurrect a rank-dependent rule through art.
- **Barbed Edge:** internal save ID `rimed_edge` is invisible. No frost, ice, blue cold palette or
  frozen wound cue may survive from the retired name.
- **Living Hook:** encounter-win growth is persisted per instance. Base catalogue art owns the living
  hook identity; optional milestone changes may add one bounded growth ring/point only after a
  separate same-instance readability proof. Inventory state cannot reroll anatomy.
- **Quiet Knife:** preserved concealment is not invisibility. Do not erase/fade the item or show an
  unseen attacker.
- **Bloodletter:** legacy Bleed is a known weapon effect; show an open channel, not gore, a blood
  droplet or a status badge baked into the object.
- **Warded Haft:** old writing remains unreadable visual texture. It cannot reveal a focus, language,
  author or lore claim. Crush ward is external detail/state and must remain legible without a shield
  symbol embedded in every pixel.

## Collision proof

At native size compare at least:

1. Raking Edge ↔ Ripping Hook ↔ Barbed Edge ↔ Living Hook ↔ Bloodletter;
2. Keen Blade ↔ Hairsplitter ↔ Quiet Knife ↔ Two-Natured Blade;
3. Long Pick ↔ Warded Spear ↔ Kept Distance ↔ Ranked Spear ↔ Long Fang;
4. Banded Mace ↔ Anvilfall ↔ Settled Argument ↔ Warded Haft.

Render color, literal grayscale and silhouette/value-only. Within the rending group, backward
external thorns, one dominant hook, repeated recessed teeth, organic growth and a longitudinal wound
channel must remain five different dominant constructions.

## Required proof

1. All eight exact IDs render without SF Symbol fallback and remain distinct from all 44 ordinary
   equipment IDs.
2. Palette removal and palette swapping cannot confuse Barbed Edge with retired cold fiction or make
   the three piercing pole/fang families identical.
3. Same-ID pixels remain identical across loot, Storehouse, equipment and protected/non-recyclable
   contexts.
4. Living Hook instance growth, if shown, is deterministic from saved milestone state and cannot
   change the base exact-ID mapping.
5. No icon reveals affinity odds, source-world hidden facts, apex species or reward rarity.
6. Unknown/unidentified presentation never uses these silhouettes before the item itself is known.
