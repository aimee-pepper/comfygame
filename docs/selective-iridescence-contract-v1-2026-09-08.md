# Selective iridescence — accepted scope and production contract

8 September2026. **Accepted intended; visual effect not implemented.** PM relayed Aimee: “sure, iridesence can stay on like, some birds and some fish and some reptiles.” This narrows the earlier proposal. It does not approve all plants, chitin, shells or every candidate surface listed there. It also does not replace Finish with numerousness.

## Source eligibility and tuning

Use actual anatomy, not real-world taxonomic labels inferred from a name. The initial supported surface groups are (1) actual feathered wings with nonabsent wing state and positive count; (2) declared overlapping/armoured scales on axial, elongated or piscine source bodies. These cover the appropriate feather/scale examples in Aimee’s direction without adding Earth classes, inventing feathers on skin, or assigning flight from wings. No membrane, bare fin, horn, skin, fur, chitin, shell, plant or chemical surface receives the new effect in this first scope. A qualifying body does not spread its iridescence onto differently sourced appendages.

Design first-pass tuning: an independent deterministic25% selection for each eligible source **surface group**, not each mesh piece. One feather-wing-group draw keeps paired wings coherent; one exterior-scale draw covers its actual scaled region. Seed by stable source/world identity and group, using an isolated versioned stream. No render-time RNG, new costly budget or pressure bonus. A positive stored Schiller value alone is insufficient for eligibility; selected group with I=0 shows no added response. This percentage is revisable tuning, not individually dictated by Aimee.

Freeze explicit eligible/selected region facts in a new source/surface version. Nil and surface2 continue exact earlier appearance; do not reinterpret their raw Schiller values. Specimens inherit the source-group selection and retain their own legitimate inherited or overridden Finish intensity; explicit regional overrides retain ownership. Actual observed appearance/memory freezes the resolved result. Existing anatomy and family selection remain unchanged.

## Appearance

Reuse the bounded source-relative reflected-sheen direction from procedural-surfaces-and-markings-v2.md only on selected regions: with I=normalized Schiller, mu=clamp(abs(dot(normal,view)),0,1), amount=0.25I(1−mu)^2 and reflected hue offset12°I(2mu−1). These are first-pass stylized limits. Neutral sources receive neutral sheen. Source albedo/colour/Pattern, opaque alpha1, existing roughness/rough-surface floor, zero emission and zero metalness remain unchanged. No time-cycling rainbow or emissive substitute. Ordinary light reveals the finish; the finish adds no illumination or danger cue.

A production path needs an actual supported reflected-colour renderer, region metadata and cache/version integration. The existing URL Preview Surface control succeeded while the tested Standard Surface node was rejected on the recorded runtime; do not repeat the failed probe or claim all backend paths impossible. Engineering may resolve a different supported path independently of other work. Until then current opaque colour/shine/Pattern is truthful fallback, and iridescence remains explicitly undelivered.

## Economy, source custody and interface

Retain the normalized opacity/shine/Schiller triple and existing Lustre=shine+Schiller. Do not zero, rename, normalize differently or transfer Schiller into shine: those operations alter roughness, Chitin’s direct capability and other material consumers. Preserve existing flora material properties, legacy calculations, source quality, prices, reward quantities and earned stock. Visual selection cannot choose a material or increase yield. The earlier statement that stored Schiller was purely cosmetic is corrected: its new reflected effect is cosmetic, its stored value already contributes to materials.

No whole-game Finish migration or legacy2D/name rewrite is included. Current modular naming already avoids a Schiller-derived iridescent label; preserve that correct behavior. Any new iridescent descriptor may follow only the selected actual surface, never raw compatibility values. Material Lustre remains distinct from a visible finish description. Same subtype/quality stock grouping and expanded exact variants remain unchanged; selection is not a new stack key.

No plant numerousness scalar, new stems, flower heads, petals, harvest formula or ecological simulation is approved. Existing independent plant counts remain.

## Bounded readiness and handoff

Design scope is ready; renderer capability is the remaining named technical dependency. Engineering should preserve one eligible feathered source, one eligible scaled source and one ineligible source using existing actual source fixtures; no generated-world census. Focused checks cover group selection determinism, region exclusion/overrides, old reconstruction, specimen/memory custody and unchanged economic inputs. One actual supported angular-view comparison at the standard target demonstrates the reflected effect before any completion claim. Asset reviews supplied evidence and tunes only permitted appearance values; no new species texture pack, configuration matrix or duplicate Design capture. Backend failure goes to PM once with the exact evidence; it never stalls stems, discovery, water or other ready work.
