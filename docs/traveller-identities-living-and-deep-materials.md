# Traveller Identity Drafts — Corrin, Nessa and Grimmond

**Status:** Identity, focus, diary and shop-boundary directions approved  
**Scope:** Flexible living materials, prepared bodily effects, and deep extraction

> **Signature authority:** Nessa and Corrin's current four-condition profiles are in
> `traveller-signatures-mid-current.md`. Grimmond's seven-condition profile is in
> `traveller-signatures-seven-eight-current.md`.

These three travellers all transform world resources, but they operate at different boundaries:
Corrin works where material remains in contact with bodies; Nessa works where a preparation changes
a body; Grimmond works where removing material changes a place.

## Material contrast

| | **Corrin** | **Nessa** | **Grimmond** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reads | Flex, grain, stretch and repeated contact | Dose, reaction, timing and individual response | Load, pressure, void and disturbed depth |
| Makes possible | Capacity, bindings and light protection | Consumables, inks, coatings and cures | Deep sites, hard resources and better extraction |
| Central error | Calling a material neutral because its source is absent | Calling a substance cure or poison without a body and dose | Calling depth unclaimed because nobody is visible there |
| Recommended focus | **Chitin** | **Thorn** | **Mercury** |

# Corrin — Tanner

## Identity

| Field | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Pronouns** | she/her |
| **Calling** | a tanner and worker of flexible organic materials |
| **Building** | The Tannery |
| **Campaign phase** | Mid |
| **Signature complexity** | 4 conditions |
| **Teaching** | **Chitin**, diary-exclusive |
| **Starting lean** | Craft + Evasion, with practical Fortitude |

### One-line blurb

> Makes hard-wearing things for the parts of a body that refuse to stay still. Never calls material
> obedient when she means it has been forced.

### Voice anchors

- **Habit:** Flexes, warms and works a sample before judging it. She examines wear where straps meet
  shoulders, fingers, hips and joints rather than admiring an untouched surface.
- **Attitude:** Familiar, exact and unembarrassed by bodies or their practical needs. Corrin asks how
  something rubs, sweats, pinches or changes under repeated use.
- **Tension:** Good tanning makes an animal-derived material useful long after its source is absent.
  Skill can honour that cost through durability and little waste, but it can also make the source
  easier to forget by producing something clean, anonymous and convenient.

Corrin should not romanticise “using every part” as moral absolution. Avoiding waste matters; it does
not retroactively answer whether taking the animal was justified.

## Why the player seeks her

- satchel and storage-capacity upgrades as material variety expands;
- bindings, straps, flexible fittings and other shared crafting inputs;
- light armour whose identity is mobility, layering and close fit;
- a Tannery route for turning appropriate creature resources into durable usable forms.

Corrin owns flexible organic goods and foundational light armour. Bracken's Armoury owns higher-tier
armour recipes, resistance tuning and advanced defensive trade-offs across armour families. A Corrin
base may later be advanced or fitted through Bracken; the two shops should cooperate rather than sell
parallel “better leather” ladders.

### Coherence sentence

> Because Corrin understands **how living material changes under repeated contact with a moving
> body**, recruiting her opens **capacity goods, bindings and flexible light protection**.

## Chitin

Chitin productively challenges Corrin's preference for flex: it is jointed hardness grown by a living
body rather than rigid protection imposed afterward. Her teaching page should notice where a shell
must divide, overlap or remain thin so movement can continue.

Chitin is Corrin's diary-exclusive focus; its former ordinary wild/merchant route is superseded.

## Signature and diary shape

Her four-condition world should contain hard-covered life, strong organic yield, a condition that
requires flex or repeated movement, and a wet/dry or thermal cycle that changes material behaviour.

**Initial target: 8 pages**

| Pages | Purpose |
|---:|---|
| 4 | One location/material page per signature condition |
| 1 | Chitin teaching |
| 1 | Bracken disagreement over flexible layers and transferred harm |
| 1 | Vance or Kestrel page about provenance and animal cost |
| 1 | Failed binding, worn route or Corrin whereabouts page |

### Diary arc

- **First impression:** Corrin seems able to make every difficult material comfortable and useful.
- **Complication:** A beautifully finished batch concealed variation in its sources; reliability was
  achieved by discarding evidence that some material should never have been processed together.
- **After recruitment:** She keeps transforming remains into durable goods but preserves provenance
  and refuses “nothing wasted” as the end of the ethical question.

## Relationship anchors

- **Bracken:** Flexible contact layers versus advanced resistance structures.
- **Kestrel:** Efficient butchery supplies material; Corrin asks what knowledge and permission that
  efficiency cannot provide.
- **Vance:** Both keep objects in use, and both can erase provenance to make circulation easier.
- **Sabine:** Care for living creatures places limits on which materials should enter a workshop.

# Nessa — Apothecary

## Identity

| Field | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Pronouns** | she/her |
| **Calling** | an apothecary and preparer of situated effects |
| **Building** | The Apothecary |
| **Campaign phase** | Early–mid rather than late-mid |
| **Signature complexity** | 4 conditions |
| **Teaching** | **Thorn**, diary-exclusive |
| **Starting lean** | Craft + Ingenuity, with support access |

### One-line blurb

> Refuses to call anything a cure until she knows for whom, how much, and when.

### Voice anchors

- **Habit:** Labels preparations with body, dose, route and time rather than only a substance name.
  She asks what else a person has taken before recommending anything.
- **Attitude:** Direct, collaborative and comfortable saying “I do not know yet.” Nessa does not use
  detachment as proof of competence.
- **Tension:** Reading harm as transformable makes treatment possible. It can also turn another
  person's pain into an interesting problem and tempt the practitioner to value a measurable result
  above the person's experience of being treated.

Nessa is not a generic healer. Her domain includes coatings, inks, antidotes, prepared hazards and
escape tools as well as remedies. The same literacy serves care and harm; intent does not replace
dosage or consequence.

## Why the player seeks her

- the currently absent preparation verb: making consumables before difficult worlds and fights;
- cures, antidotes and emergency options;
- weapon/tool coatings and prepared reactive effects;
- specialist inks shared with the writing and crafting loops;
- the existing Waystone route if that item remains in the consumables design.

Nessa should arrive early enough that preparation matters across much of the campaign. Her shop adds
choices and inventory pressure already present in the crafting proposal; her character design does
not add another potency, toxicity or tolerance meter.

### Coherence sentence

> Because Nessa understands **effects as relationships among substance, dose, body and moment**, she
> can turn gathered materials into **consumables, cures, coatings and inks** without pretending any
> preparation has one universal meaning.

## Thorn

Thorn fits as a visible boundary between medicine and harm: tissue shaped to deliver force at a
particular point and discourage a particular contact. Nessa's page should study delivery and dose,
not reduce Thorn to “a poisonous plant.”

Thorn is Nessa's diary-exclusive focus; its former ordinary acquisition route is superseded.

## Signature and diary shape

Her four-condition world should contain chemically or metabolically distinct flora, one active
defence, accessible water or another carrier, and an environmental condition that changes reaction
or dosage.

**Initial target: 8 pages**

| Pages | Purpose |
|---:|---|
| 4 | One preparation or reaction page per signature condition |
| 1 | Thorn teaching |
| 1 | Auber relationship page: extraction judged by effect on a body |
| 1 | Sabine, Orsa or Perren page about treatment and consent |
| 1 | Mislabelled remedy, failed dose or Nessa whereabouts page |

### Diary arc

- **First impression:** Nessa's caution appears to make her preparations unusually safe.
- **Complication:** She once continued a technically successful treatment after the person receiving
  it withdrew cooperation, mistaking improvement in the measured condition for permission.
- **After recruitment:** She remains willing to intervene in emergencies but treats participation,
  refusal and lived effect as part of efficacy rather than obstacles to it.

## Relationship anchors

- **Auber:** Shared extraction practice; Nessa asks what the result does to a particular body.
- **Sabine:** Dosage and treatment across radically different bodies; neither assumes care generalises.
- **Orsa:** Informal reports at the Tavern reveal effects a workshop trial may never capture.
- **Perren:** A remedy offered through manipulative framing is not freely chosen merely because it works.

# Grimmond — Delver

## Identity

| Field | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Pronouns** | he/him |
| **Calling** | a delver who reads load, depth and the voids extraction leaves behind |
| **Building** | The Deep Works |
| **Campaign phase** | Late |
| **Signature complexity** | 7 conditions |
| **Teaching** | **Mercury**, diary-exclusive |
| **Starting lean** | Force + Ingenuity, with strong field utility |

### One-line blurb

> Maps what holds the ceiling after the valuable part is gone. Says an empty space is still made of
> consequences.

### Voice anchors

- **Habit:** Taps, listens and waits for delayed responses. He records removed volume and changed load
  as carefully as recovered material.
- **Attitude:** Patient underground, impatient with the word “untouched.” Grimmond assumes every depth
  has pressure, flow and history even where no person has left a visible claim.
- **Tension:** Refusing careless extraction protects places and workers. It can also become a form of
  romantic restraint in which Grimmond praises leaving material buried while other people bear the
  cost of scarcity above.

He is not a miner who became mystical about caves. His expertise is practical negative space: the
route, water, pressure and load that remain after removal. Depth is a place because actions continue
through it, not because it is sacred by default.

## Why the player seeks him

- access to deep sites and hard-substrate opportunities;
- improved mineral extraction and fewer wasteful or destabilising attempts;
- better interpretation of load, void, buried routes and deep hazards;
- a Deep Works progression distinct from Edren's ruins and Halloway's making.

Edren interprets constructed traces left by earlier people. Grimmond reaches and reads geological or
otherwise buried depth whether or not people built there. Halloway transforms material after recovery;
Grimmond decides how it can be removed and what must remain supported afterward.

### Coherence sentence

> Because Grimmond understands **extraction as a change to load, flow and empty space**, recruiting him
> opens **deep sites, hard-resource recovery and deliberate excavation** rather than merely larger ore
> numbers.

## Mercury

Mercury belongs to Grimmond because it refuses the ordinary visual grammar of mineral depth: heavy,
reflective material that moves and divides like liquid. His page should focus on how it finds low
places and joins separated spaces, supporting Mercury's existing chaining purpose rather than merely
naming a rare ore.

Mercury is Grimmond's diary-exclusive focus; its former ordinary late-wild/merchant route is
superseded.

## Signature and diary shape

His seven-condition world should describe connected depth rather than seven kinds of rock: hard
substrate, meaningful relief, buried water or flow, pressure, scarce access, a deep site and one
condition whose value makes careless removal tempting.

**Initial target: 11 pages**

| Pages | Purpose |
|---:|---|
| 7 | One depth, route or load page per signature condition |
| 1 | Mercury teaching |
| 1 | Edren or Halloway boundary-of-practice page |
| 1 | Auber relationship page about extracted material and residue |
| 1 | Collapse avoided/caused, sealed route or Grimmond whereabouts page |

### Diary arc

- **First impression:** Grimmond appears to know exactly what can be removed without consequence.
- **Complication:** He once chose the technically safest refusal while treating the people who needed
  the material as abstractions outside his site plan.
- **After recruitment:** He keeps limits on extraction but makes scarcity and alternatives part of
  the same accounting as collapse risk.

## Relationship anchors

- **Edren:** Built history and geological depth overlap without becoming the same expertise.
- **Halloway:** What can be recovered safely versus what can be made useful afterward.
- **Auber:** Separation begins at extraction; residue and altered sites exist before the Distillery.
- **Nessa:** Trace deep materials may be valuable or dangerous at doses a delver cannot judge alone.

# Complexity and ownership check

- Corrin uses the existing Tannery, capacity and light-armour plan. Bracken retains advanced armour.
- Nessa uses the existing Apothecary consumable catalogue. No toxicity or tolerance subsystem is added.
- Grimmond uses the proposed deep-site/extraction layer. His identity does not itself approve a new
  underground navigation mode.
- All three focuses already exist in the vocabulary; their approved acquisition routes change
  ownership without adding words.

# Approved batch decisions

1. **Corrin:** flexible-contact material identity, diary-exclusive Chitin and 8-page diary.
2. **Nessa:** situated-dose identity, diary-exclusive Thorn and 8-page diary; place her at
   early–mid so consumables matter across more of the campaign.
3. **Grimmond:** extraction-as-negative-space identity, diary-exclusive Mercury and 11-page
   diary.
4. Shop boundary: Corrin creates flexible goods and foundational light armour; Bracken
   owns advanced armour progression across families.
5. Grimmond's Deep Works improves access/extraction without independently approving a
   separate underground-navigation subsystem.
