# Creature discovery and material disclosure — player journey contract

**6 September2026 · Game Design first pass; implementation pending.** Closes the existing distant-sighting/material-disclosure design dependency as one bounded Field → Look → encounter/reward → Return → Library journey. It does not implement new anatomy, shop roles, harvesting, fees, XP rewards, ecology simulation or a broad creature rollout.

Authorities: [Bestiary knowledge/grouping](bestiary-knowledge-and-grouping-v1.md), [body/habitat](generated-creature-body-habitat-v1.md), [role coherence](creature-role-coherence-v1.md), [body/material rewards](creature-body-material-rewards-production-v1.md), settled Hide/Bone contracts, Aimee's colour/Pattern continuity and actual Apex status. Existing earned specimen measurements must not be hidden behind a new defeat, analysis or paid-library requirement.

## 1. Current owners and the specific missing distinction

Current `WorldRules` encounter admission calls `DiscoveryLog.recordSpecies` and `recordSpecimen`; `DiscoveryRecord.record` increments `timesEncountered`. First species XP is awarded from `initiallyUnrecordedSpecies`, determined by the absence of the species record. Combat Read/remember uses those existing knowledge owners. Bestiary entries and Library volumes currently require `species[key].firstSeenRunIndex != nil`. A raw generated cast is not discovery.

`WorldInspectionRules.inspect` is currently a pure read-only query over the run. `WorldRules.isCurrentlyVisible` is the exact conjunction of **full current tile visibility** and actual creature visibility, using the party-aware visibility profile. `isVisible` alone handles crypsis/Apex, not tile sight, and is insufficient. Revealed terrain, fringe and remembered tiles are not current creature sightings.

**Required separation:** a new sight-only record must not call `recordSpecies`/`recordSpecimen`, increment encounters, create a full specimen, award XP, consume the later first-encounter reward, or automatically unlock creature Writing projections that currently depend on the existing encounter registry. Introduce only the small additive sighting knowledge needed below, at the existing discovery/action owners. Do not redefine all historical `firstSeenRunIndex` records or delete source knowledge to make a linear level system.

## 2. One player journey

These are cumulative facts, not paid knowledge ranks. The player can encounter a concealed creature before ever having a sight-only note, and can retain an encounter record without winning.

| Event | What becomes known / visible | What does not happen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hidden, fringe, remembered-only terrain, distant sound/alert or special Apex marker outside full sight | Existing warning/marker presentation only, with whatever it already lawfully discloses | No new sight entry, body/habitat/colour sample, count or material prediction from a hidden actor |
| First actual full sight of a visible creature | **Seen** note: the existing lawful visible name/label, observed appearance, trustworthy explicit body shape, actual supported habitat chapter, visible appendage/covering description, source world and first observation | No specimen measurements, chemistry, skeleton deduction, reward quantity/band, XP, encounter count, specimen comparison or extra movement |
| Look at the currently visible creature | Read-only explanation of the saved/legitimately visible body and how its existing movement works; supported visible exterior parts may be named as **Possible materials**, explicitly unconfirmed | No turns, fee, harvest, combat, ingredient, new chemistry test, full specimen, extra XP or research requirement |
| Actual encounter admission, or existing lawful Read/remember | Existing full specimen/encounter knowledge and its measurements/comparisons; actual source-derived part identity may use those legitimately owned facts | No new victory/analysis toll and no bonus discovery reward caused by the earlier Seen note |
| Actual committed material reward | **Recovered materials**: exact real type/subtype, quantity, quality, source measurements/colour/Pattern/provenance from that specimen's awarded receipt | No extra drop roll or repeated loot; no claim that another specimen guarantees the same result |
| Return / defeat outcome | Existing recovered/lost stock partition; link material to its actual known source; previously learned facts remain knowledge even if that stock is later lost, sold or crafted | No second acquisition, material reveal from a merely proposed/unawarded drop, reward conversion or recipe unlock |
| Library / Bestiary | One entry for the same known identity with Seen, Encountered and Recovered facts where actually earned; source material history and existing specimen comparisons remain distinct | No missing-stage locks, hidden cast placeholders, automatic analysis or read-to-recover action |

**First-pass chapter disclosure:** a real full sight may record the creature's actual already-supported source habitat, so the new note can go directly to Sky/Water/Amphibious/Land. It does not infer habitat from standing on one water tile, wings, name or a climate. Missing/untrusted old habitat remains Unclassified; missing explicit body shape remains Shape not recorded. This is the explicit new learning event extending the old sorting contract, not a retroactive claim that all old visible actors were recorded.

## 3. First-sight evidence and save behavior

Use a proposed optional `creatureSightingKnowledgeVersion = 1` and sighting records keyed by the **same canonical discovery identity** that the actual encounter would use. An observation carries source world/run and actual creature identity, stable observed label, observation sequence, supported habitat/explicit shape, visible structural facts and the exact observed colour/Pattern source. Store only the permitted appearance/structural subset, not a full `CreatureTraits` specimen disguised as a sighting. A source with no canonical mapping may keep its current field label; do not fabricate a species identity from a name, icon or coordinates.

The real world-action/visibility owner records the sight after a successfully committed arrival, movement/visibility change or equivalent genuine current observation, with the party-aware **full** visibility and crypsis guards. The UI renderer and `inspect` remain read-only. Opening a menu, sorting the Library, reading saved world data or reloading an old hidden actor cannot record new biology. A resume may observe an actually currently visible actor through the same owner, but must not scan all saved/anchored worlds or backfill unseen history. No extra turn, RNG draw, animation wait or loot action is needed.

Repeated views of the same unchanged observation are idempotent. Update a latest observed appearance only from a new legitimate currently seen source/state, not from hidden movement or a changed source behind fog. Preserve the original first-observed reference. Do not add a new per-frame sight counter. Old saves decode the new collection as empty while retaining every existing species/specimen/Apex record and earned comparison. Existing known facts cannot be overwritten by an incomplete legacy default. Failed state persistence rolls back the sight update with its owning action; no UI-only knowledge is declared durable.

**Encounter preservation:** because sight-only knowledge is separate, a creature seen and then encountered for the first time still follows the existing first-species XP/encounter/Read path exactly once. After that encounter, union the same identity for display; the sight note does not become another species, encounter, specimen or award. Preserve existing Writing creature-knowledge eligibility until its existing qualifying encounter/Read event; a Seen-only Library note is not automatically that event. This is a compatibility boundary, not a new writing restriction on previously earned knowledge.

**No historical telemetry reconstruction:** do not convert old encounter counts into guessed first-sight events, generate missing source appearances, or mark old knowledge unread just because the new feature was installed. Encounter admission and Read may continue their existing counters; this packet does not fix or reinterpret their historical distinction.

## 4. Appearance, parts and hidden facts

A visible body description can say **furred covering**, **overlapping scales**, **feathered appendages**, **membrane wings**, **cranial horns** or another actually supported declared region. Use the body/material manifest and existing disclosed role names, not a colour-to-anatomy lookup. Qualitative colour/Pattern belongs to that actual observed specimen. Preserve its source data for truthful appearance; do not display numerical CMY, Patterning, material-quality score or hidden capability bars merely because the renderer stores them internally. Prior earned numerical fields remain accessible in their existing specimen view.

At Look, a **Possible materials** hint requires all of: the relevant exterior part is legitimately observed; the current source's actual versioned reward contract supports that material; and a real implemented collection/custody consumer exists. It is a conditional identity hint, not a promised drop, current quality band, yield, sale quote or recipe role. Do not show a planned new solid material as obtainable merely because its draft manifest is authored. Actual legacy rewards may be described with their true legacy names and supported uses; do not relabel a legacy Scale/Plate receipt as a new typed subtype.

First sight/Look cannot infer hidden internal Bone from a body silhouette, hollow bones from flight, Venom from warning colours, Oil from insulation, Ichor from glow, or a medicinal/caustic/pigment function from colour. Encounter/Read already records certain full source measurements; a part hint may then use a **complete supported source contract** that those actual known measurements establish, such as the settled skeleton-based Bone route when implemented. A high number is not a substitute for missing fluid, tooth, claw, tusk, down-layer or membrane anatomy. Generic legacy toxic/emanating projections are not proof of new typed chemistry.

Actual reward is the source of **confirmed recovered material** knowledge. Save the exact family/subtype/quality/count and complete actual colour/Pattern/part measurements in that recovery receipt. Display the quality and quantity that were really awarded. Preserve source detail behind grouped quantities; never transfer another member of a species stack's colour into the selected material. Any later crafting preview uses its real selected units and complete current recipe, not the Bestiary's generic historical list.

**Species uncertainty:** a pooled kind may contain different known specimens or versions. Say **Recovered from this kind before**, not **Always drops**. A failed70% Hide recovery does not prove the species lacks skin; no observed reward from an encounter does not fill all missing families with 'none'. Ineligibility can be named only for an actual known supported body/source contract, not inferred from one unlucky result or a held implementation.

## 5. Look, Return and Library presentation

Use existing native consumers: `WorldInspectionRules`/the current Look surface, `BestiaryView`, existing encounter result and Return material rows, and `LibraryRules`/the Bestiary shelf. No new research station, notebook, collection tool or mandatory tutorial.

**Look keeps the existing selection/interaction range**; this contract does not add remote inspection or movement. **Look order:** existing lawful creature name and actual Apex badge; one short body/habitat sentence; any known source-appropriate possible-material hint; existing danger/interaction information; a route to its current Bestiary note when the record exists. Keep explicit inspection visible under the delivered notice preferences even when optional monster/find popups are muted. Automatic knowledge may update quietly; it does not require a second mandatory popup. Existing optional notices keep their category controls, and muting presentation never prevents legitimate recording.

First-pass copy examples:

- Seen-only: **“Seen in [world]. No close encounter recorded.”**
- External body: **“Feathered wings and a scaled body. Suited to the sky.”** Only when both actual regions and habitat support it.
- Supported hint: **“Possible material: Fur Pelt. Recovery is not guaranteed.”** Only for an implemented actual source, without quantity or quality promises.
- Internal knowledge absent: **“Internal materials have not been recorded.”** Do not create a checklist of every hidden possible part.
- Confirmed receipt: **“Recovered: Rare Chitin Plate ×3, from this specimen.”** This is evidence from one successful reward, not a standing species quote.
- Lost stock after learning: **“Recorded during the excursion; these portions were lost on return.”** Only where the real outcome receipt establishes that partition.
- Previously encountered legacy entry: retain its normal specimen details; **“Earlier record; first sight not recorded”** is sufficient if needed. No repair chore.

**One Bestiary identity:** read the union of legitimate sight-only notes and existing encountered species, deduplicated by canonical identity. Existing full specimen details take precedence over a sight-only presentation without erasing a later actually observed appearance. Keep an observed appearance separate from the specimen used for numerical comparison; a newer Seen note must not silently replace the full traits behind an older comparison. The latest legitimately recorded **explicit** shape can govern the one current grouping, with the existing 'Latest recorded shape' explanation. If that representative has no full specimen, show the observed appearance/label, not fabricated measurement bars. Unknown observations never overwrite known classification.

Keep the independently saved habitat authority and its conflict/refusal behavior. Where a sight record and an existing full record disagree on supposedly immutable habitat, preserve the established record and isolate the invalid observation rather than silently moving chapters. Body shape may legitimately vary within an existing pooled kind under the established latest-record rule; it never creates a second identity.

Library volume construction and Bestiary counts/search must use the **same admitted union**. Fixed chapter labels remain navigation, not hidden population evidence. No all-species denominator, locked silhouette or new unseen body group is shown. Sight-only count means a known observed kind, not a full specimen count; encounters/individual comparisons continue using their existing real records. Use the existing per-identity Library content ID for attention, not a second unread entry on promotion; preserve read state and current attention semantics. Normal specimen-history pruning cannot delete known sight/classification/material-discovery facts, though exact physical ownership still belongs to inventory/outcome receipts.

Return names the actual carried/recovered/lost material with its source link where one exists. Reading that link only opens knowledge. It never creates a new acquisition, auto-packs retained stock, repairs an item, reruns the reward, reveals hidden relatives or grants a recipe. Selling/crafting/dismantling a recorded portion does not erase the fact that it was discovered, and the historical entry never becomes spendable inventory.

## 6. Apex, special actors and legacy preservation

Build319/320 actual Apex labels are delivered on the existing legitimate field/minimap/inspection/encounter surfaces. Keep the real `isApex` authority. An existing special range marker outside full sight remains a warning, not a sight-only specimen or source-chemistry reveal. Full current sight can record the same already lawful label and actual Apex fact; it cannot invent a proper name, trophy or guaranteed reward. Never infer Apex from stillness, size, rare material, an ordinary role-fit score or a bright effect.

Do not call the existing encounter-owned `recordApex` just to count a distant sight: its historic specimen/encounter semantics remain intact. A sight-only Apex fact stays in its sight record until its ordinary qualifying events add any further data. The display can say 'Apex observed' without claiming a battle, defeat or new tally in an old counter.

Sessile hostile flora keeps its existing flora/discovery path and actual harmful-growth warning. It does not become an ordinary animal material source or a fifth Bestiary habitat. Authored guardians/non-animal actors keep their actual permitted labels/rewards and existing records. Unsupported legacy shape/habitat stays accessible without guessed defaults. Existing saved rewards, source colours/Pattern, learned recipes, journal pages, full specimen stats, comparison counts and first-discovery rewards are never reset by this feature.

## 7. Exact remaining ecology and implementation boundary

This contract supplies the discovery journey and knowledge rules, not new source availability. The material contract still holds missing teeth/claws/tusks, a separate Down layer, fin/membrane measurements and recoverable Oil/Venom/Ichor anatomy/chemistry. Exact new solid-part equipment roles remain separate. All complete Resin/Toxic Sap/Dyer's Root shop routes remain independent.

Food requirements, edible flora/prey relationships, nest/den use and useful weather responses still need actual ecology design. A role label or territory-find story is not evidence those systems exist. The accepted body/habitat and cold-water-count corrections remain their own intended rules; this disclosure update does not implement them, change creature movement or create safe access to deep-water sources. Natural diversity, reward incidence, exploration decisions and crafting enjoyment need play of the completed flows. **All three Aimee creature goals remain incomplete**, with this marked as a finished bounded design contract awaiting implementation.

Bounded Engineering cases: full sight versus fringe/crypsis/remembered/remote Apex marker; Seen-only note without specimen/count/XP or Writing unlock; Look with no state spend/reward and no hidden chemistry; Seen→first encounter retains the existing single discovery reward and one identity; encounter without victory retains old full measurements; actual reward→Return loss→Library retains knowledge but no stock; existing known read entries/pruned specimens retain facts; source-specific colour/Pattern and same-kind variation stay separate; invalid source, stale action, failed save and repeated reopen never duplicate knowledge awards or loot. Extend current discovery/visibility/custody tests and one normal native journey when implemented. No new audit framework, speculative Asset work or Design native/phone rerun.
