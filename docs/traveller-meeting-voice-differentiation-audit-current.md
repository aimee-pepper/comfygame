## Finding

The meetings are physically coherent and mechanically well separated, but the corpus is more
uniform in **sentence logic** than in subject matter. Across the four drafts, abstract corrections
repeat heavily: 27 uses of “does not”, 18 of “is not”, 20 of “without”, 13 of “must” and 8 of
“becomes”. Nearly every traveller can currently end an observation with the same polished structure:

```text
X is real. It does not make Y true.
X preserves one thing. Y preserves another.
Doing X does not purchase / erase / appoint / prove Y.
```

Those distinctions are often thoughtful and correct. The problem is cumulative: after several
meetings, the player may hear one authorial ethic speaking through different professions. This is a
voice issue, not a request to make anyone foolish, less careful or less grounded.

## First revision candidates

These are atlas flags, not replacement copy:

1. **Rook:** keep one explicit boundary principle; ground the other two reply endings in moving the
   line, the creature's body/reach or the only dry crossing.
2. **Marrick:** preserve the excluded sixth position but replace one balanced protection/failure
   maxim with a remembered command, name-less incident or correction spoken to the player.
3. **Ashe:** retain every consent boundary while breaking the three equally polished answer shapes;
   one reply can be brief, one interrupted by managing heat, and one openly uncertain.
4. **Auber / Noll:** read side by side. If a sentence can move between them without changing nouns,
   revise it. Noll owns salvage decisions; Auber owns phase change, carriers and displaced remainder.
5. **Grimmond:** let the final reply end on the delayed sound or brace rather than another abstract
   `X is not Y` distinction.
6. **Nine:** add one small preference, irritation, pleasure or practical habit unrelated to lost
   biography while preserving the verified arrangement.

## Revision discipline

- Preserve every stable exchange ID and the order-independent meaning it owns.
- Preserve player-spoken offer / traveller-spoken terminal roles.
- Do not add lore answers, hidden signature facts, exact mechanics or profession exposition.
- Aim for at least two exchange endings per meeting that stop on a physical observation, action,
  joke, admission or unresolved feeling rather than a universal maxim.
- Do not enforce a corpus-wide quota mechanically. Perren and Lys may speak analytically; Talin may
  stay spare; Vance and Orsa may be funny. Difference is the goal.
- Any exact-copy change invalidates the prior hash and returns that unit to Draft until Aimee reviews
  it in the atlas.

This order makes interchangeability audible. A line that seems elegant alone but could belong to
the paired neighbour should be flagged for revision rather than approved from isolated reading.
