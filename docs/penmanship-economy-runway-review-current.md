# Penmanship economy runway — review batch

**Status:** reversible playtest comparison approved by Game Design; Aimee review remains open  
**Scope:** prices and world-resource counts only; Rough charcoal → Brush → Fountain pen and the
current fan/fork topology remain settled  
**Evidence baseline:** Recommended Raw Essence profile averages about 33 gross refined Essence plus
Spring return for a fully explored world, before paying the next book

## Finding

The migrated Brush retained the legacy Pencil's **150 Essence** price to preserve an earlier sense of
weight. That is not proportionate to its current campaign role or the newly measured continuation
economy:

- the Scriptorium already costs 60 Essence to build;
- Brush is the first purchased hand and parent of every core midgame writing practice;
- 150 Essence is 4.5 fully explored Recommended worlds of **gross** income by itself;
- building plus Brush is 210 Essence, or 6.4 gross worlds before any intervening bind cost;
- the current direct route through Chaining to Fountain Pen costs 1,440 Essence including the
  Scriptorium, more than 43 average gross worlds before book spending.

These are optimistic lower bounds. The player must keep writing worlds during that accumulation,
and partial exploration earns less. Calling Brush “the largest early capacity jump” does not justify
pricing the first usable ink hand like a late capstone—particularly when colored ink, compounds and
chaining all require it.

The result would not be meaningful scarcity. It would make the core writing progression compete with
continuation for too long, encourage hoarding, and delay the very mechanics that make later worlds
less repetitive.

## Recommended first comparison profile

Use the following reversible prices when the native Penmanship graph is implemented:

| Node | Essence | World resources | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Build Scriptorium | **60** *(unchanged)* | 14 Timber, 10 Clay *(unchanged)* | Meaningful arrival/build commitment |
| Brush | **45** | 2 Copper, 6 Fibre, 4 Timber | First ink-capable hand; opens the branch |
| A table that doesn't rock | **70** | 8 Timber, 6 Clay | Tier 1 infrastructure |
| Ink Mixing | **40** | 2 Copper, 2 Quartz, 4 Resin | Mixer/tooling unlock; pigments remain separately consumed |
| Compound Assembly | **55** | 4 Pulp, 4 Resin, 2 Quartz | Semantic composition tooling |
| Chaining | **90** | 2 Mercury, 1 Mote | Major world-structure capability |
| A ruling frame | **140** | 6 Quartz, 4 Silver | Tier 2 infrastructure |
| Fountain pen | **220** | 4 Quartz, 8 Resin, 6 Silver | Final one-cell hand |

This makes:

- station + Brush **105 Essence** (3.2 average gross worlds, before binds);
- station + Brush + table + Chaining **265 Essence** (8 average gross worlds);
- the full direct Fountain route **625 Essence** (18.9 average gross worlds), before optional Ink
  Mixing/Compound Assembly and before keeper-supplied tier substitutions.

The full route remains a long-campaign achievement; only the first essential step stops behaving
like one. World-resource identities preserve specific writing goals: Copper/Fibre/Timber make the
Brush, Mercury/Mote express Chaining, and Quartz/Silver gate precision. Ink Mixing's construction
cost does not include Ichor/Sulfur/Obsidian because those are recurring pigment bases, not proof the
mixer was built.

## Runway rule

Every Penmanship purchase preview shows:

- current Essence;
- Essence after purchase;
- the player's recent median ordinary authored bind cost; and
- **ordinary authored binds remaining** after purchase.

This remains advisory, not a hard reserve. A player may deliberately spend to zero, but the game may
not make continuation risk invisible. If fewer than two recent-median binds remain, use restrained
copy—**Low writing runway**—rather than blocking the purchase or invoking a tutorial.

For a fresh/teleported DEBUG fixture with no recent authored history, use the current authored-cost
preview itself, not the 10-Essence blank-world emergency minimum. The anti-lock subsidy is never the
price baseline for discretionary research.

Game Design promotes the complete comparison table below as one reversible playtest tuning profile.
This prevents native Penmanship work from preserving known-bad legacy prices merely because final
economy review has not happened. It does not claim Aimee's final approval.

## Acceptance for the playtest comparison

1. A forced Isolde-phase campaign with zero hoarded surplus can build toward Brush without using an
   anti-lock world as its expected funding plan.
2. Purchase previews and bug context use the same rules-owned recent-median bind runway.
3. Ten ordinary returns report gross Essence, bind spend, Penmanship spend, worlds-to-purchase and
   remaining authored-bind runway for each node.
4. No player-facing copy calls 10 Essence a healthy ordinary bind cost.
5. World-resource shortfall, stale purchase, cancel, keeper-supplied tier and legacy-node migration
   remain atomic and cannot double-charge a tier or capability.
6. Costs remain one tuning profile; changing them cannot alter graph topology, completed stable IDs
   or existing page geometry.
