# Contradiction and danger — current audit

**Status:** Current boundary and implementation audit. Catalogue-only contradiction and the
Danger↔time bargain are settled. Negation grammar remains held; one stale assertion should be
disabled until it can test what it claims.  
**Updated:** 9 Aug 2026

## What survives intact

Contradiction remains a small authored catalogue, never a formula over opposed pressures. A sunny
snowy world, hot rain or competing causes are ordinary worldbuilding unless an exact named entry
fires. This is the system's most important restraint.

The live effects should stay narrow and causal:

- each fired contradiction adds its disclosed Stability penalty;
- two or more add the separately displayed escalation term;
- a named contradiction may make a Tear site eligible;
- the description registers wrongness early and names it only at the earned attribution tier.

Danger runes remain a separate deliberate bargain. They add a specific hostility and buy Stability,
with the total Stability gift capped while all chosen hostility types still apply. Peace spends
Stability to reduce hostility. Neither system should be renamed into the other.

## Reachability audit

The catalogue contains nine entries, but its apparent breadth is misleading:

- **six negation entries** can fire only from a `Sigil.negatedTargets` value created in tests or
  legacy compound data;
- the current writing grammar has no player-placeable Negate/Without structural mark and
  `PageRules.chains` explicitly emits no negations;
- **three assertion entries** can fire from live focuses: Green in the dark, Rain without air and A
  day that never turns.

Do not report the six negation entries as reachable content. They are dormant authored candidates.

## Negation recommendation

Hold a universal Negate rune rather than adding it simply to wake six catalogue rows. Negating an
implicit secondary is a powerful and interesting later authorship tool, but it needs a complete
phone-readable statement grammar:

```text
Illumination <- Sun -> without Thermal
```

The `without` branch must attach to exactly one focus, name one actual secondary target of that
focus, survive the one-target-per-component rule, appear in written-chain readout and projection,
and never be randomly filled. A nonsensical denial is rejected rather than becoming a no-op.

Until that interaction is designed and tested, keep the stable contradiction IDs for saves/content
references but exclude their Negate mechanism from acquisition and progression promises. This is a
review recommendation, not a silent retirement of contradiction.

## Assertion corrections

### Keep: Rain without air

Rain explicitly asserts airborne water. When resolved Atmosphere peak is at or below 20, the named
contradiction is honest. It may arise from an unwritten Atmosphere result: the bound world, not the
player, supplied the impossible second half.

### Keep: A day that never turns

Sun plus Cycle peak at or below 8 remains honest once the Cycle clock actually stops. It should be a
visible fixed sun and a non-turning day, not merely a Stability charge beside a clock that continues.
The Cycle-clock implementation is therefore a prerequisite for treating this entry as complete.

### Disable for now: Green in the dark

The entry claims **photosynthetic** flora in darkness but tests only whether Root was written. Root
means production; the live flora system may satisfy dark growth with fungal or chemosynthetic
metabolism. Firing the contradiction would punish a world the ecology can honestly build.

Disable this catalogue row until an explicit photosynthetic assertion or generated-species fact can
be tested without revealing hidden flora before discovery. Do not replace it with “any life in the
dark”; lightless ecosystems are a settled feature.

## Unwritten-world participation

Keep assertion contradictions eligible after unwritten subjects resolve. An underspecified page is
a cheaper, broader gamble, and the resulting world may genuinely assert incompatible facts. This
does not apply to negation, which chance may never write.

Guardrails:

- the pre-bind Stability range includes possible assertion penalties without revealing the result;
- the bound preview registers wrongness at the player's current analysis tier;
- blank-page assertion frequency is telemetry, with **15% or less** as the provisional naturalness
  ceiling; above that, contradiction becomes a tax on uncertainty rather than a memorable event;
- only one authored contradiction entry is counted once, regardless of how many matching instances
  repeat it.

## Consequence boundary

Do not apply one generic “contradiction danger multiplier” to creature count, tier, flora and hazards.
That would make every impossible statement feel the same and overlap Danger runes. The first slice
is Stability + eligible Tear. Later entries may declare a specific visible consequence when it
belongs to their fiction.

Do not add contradiction-only bulk materials now. A universal impossible-matter reward turns tiny
contradictory books into a farming recipe. Tears may continue yielding modest reality currency,
while ordinary material grade remains a consequence of the actual world and its danger.

Tears do not apply their obsolete authored site `stabilityDelta`; contradiction has already been
charged. Site contents and world greed follow the ordinary site boundary.

- Track blank/underspecified assertion frequency separately from deliberate assertion frequency.
- Provide Test Setup forcing for each reachable contradiction without changing natural weights.
- Regression: gross opposition alone never fires or charges contradiction.
- Regression: chance never creates a negation.
- Regression: fungal and chemosynthetic dark growth never trigger Green in the dark.
- Regression: a stopped Sun world emits no day/night transition.
- Regression: one contradiction may make Tears eligible but never double-charges Stability through
