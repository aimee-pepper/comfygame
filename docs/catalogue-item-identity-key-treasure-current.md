# Catalogue item identity — keys, cores and progression treasure

**Status:** handmade-art semantic identity brief for seven uncovered live catalogue IDs.  
**Scope:** two carried progression keys and five Distillery/Channelworks treasure objects at native
32px.  
**Boundary:** these are authored objects, not recolored resources, generic diamonds or mini station
icons. Provenance and eligibility remain detail/state data.

## Core-family grammar

Heat, Caustic and Light cores share one authored **prepared core housing** so the player learns they
are compatible Channelworks inputs. Their inner geometry must remain distinguishable without color:

- Heat rises/circulates through curved bands;
- Caustic descends/pools and removes one edge;
- Light opens/radiates through straight channels.

Do not derive a core by recoloring one identical pixel body. Conversely, do not make the outer
housings unrelated: they are three attunements of the same Distillery output family. Quantity,
potency, sample provenance and conversion version are not encoded in the icon.

The Essence Crystal precedes attunement and therefore has a visibly different quartz-lattice body.
It is not the “white/default core,” and the three cores are not color variants of it.

## Progression/disclosure boundaries

- **Cache Key:** the icon may show that it is a key, not which cached world contains its lock. It
  cannot include a world color, site mark, route or lock contents.
- **Anchor Frame:** the open center signifies a reusable carried binding structure, not a preview of
  the world it will anchor. It reveals no world signature, natural anchor point or Atlas progress.
- **Conduit Fixture:** the installed Heat Core is known from the catalogue item and may be visible.
  The icon cannot display potency, exact sample or Oda/Auber portrait marks.
- Unique/progression protection is an external state. A lock/shield badge may disable destructive
  actions around the tile but cannot be baked into these object pixels.
