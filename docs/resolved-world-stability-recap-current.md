# Resolved World Stability and Expedition Recap — Current

**Status:** Settled and implementation checkpoint approved by Aimee, 10 August 2026.

## One resolved world, one stability truth

Anything left unwritten is rolled at binding and can affect the world's terrain, ecology, wealth,
danger **and stability**. The authored-only stability calculation may describe the contribution of
the marks while composing, but it is not the live world's score.

- Before binding, any unwritten subject keeps stability displayed as an honest possible range.
- Binding resolves the seeded unwritten pressures once and freezes the resulting stability score on
  the run.
- The live meter, decay rate, generation diagnostics, anchored revisits and relaunches use that same
  frozen score.
- Binding price remains based on authored marks. Accepting chance is cheaper, not secretly safer.
- An empty page must produce a deterministic but genuinely varied distribution across seeds; it is
  not a hidden stable-neutral preset.

Legacy in-progress runs that predate the frozen field derive it deterministically from their saved
book and map seed. Migration cannot reroll the world.

## The recap names every permanent discovery from this expedition

“Kept for good” contains:

- newly recovered diary pages;
- newly recovered field notes, route marks, site fragments and working scraps, with their text;
- travellers who actually agreed to come Home during this expedition.

Traveller presence, merely reaching a traveller, and declining recruitment do not count. Writing
and recruited people remain permanent on collapse, but the recap still distinguishes them from
ordinary haul and from discoveries made on earlier expeditions.

## Verification gates

- Empty-page seeds cross multiple meaningful stability bands and repeat exactly by seed.
- Save/reload and legacy decoding preserve the resolved score and decay.
- Portal and partial-haul/collapse outcomes include only newly recovered writing and newly recruited
  travellers.
- Older recap saves decode with empty new fields.
