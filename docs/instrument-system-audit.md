# Instrument system — historical decisions and current direction

**Historical review: 8 August 2026. Updated authority: 6 September 2026.** The [complete Survey Post production contract](survey-post-whole-system-production-v1.md) now owns all eight initial instruments and sixteen Good/Fine improvement recipes. The original audit's missing-field-loop table described an earlier build and must not be used as a current defect list. Its historical details remain in Git history. The field loop and Good/Fine precision services now exist under older ingredient rules; this is not a new delivery receipt.

## Accepted fieldwork design retained from 8 August

- One Survey uses all carried instruments for one world turn, outside encounters.
- Ownership is not calibration. An actual field reading makes the subject available to the page lens under its existing gates.
- Crude gives a qualitative band and broad interval, Good a narrower interval, Fine the rounded whole-number reading.
- Observations persist as count, range and best measured precision. Improving owned hardware at Home does not fabricate a more precise field observation.
- Page-lens tiers control explanation depth separately: prose; calibrated readings; written-focus contributions; derived instability explanation; detailed living-layer tendencies. Their existing subject gates and costs stay separate from instrument precision.
- Reading surfaces respect their actual knowledge/disclosure rules. No pressure measurement grants hidden map coordinates, resources or creatures.

## Superseded instructions

The old suggestion to replace permanent capabilities with graded material-derived inventory instances is retired for this batch. Instruments remain permanent Reality capabilities, with no equipment or consumable-slot toll. The generic property-35/65 sample recipes are old current behavior only; the complete named Good/Fine bills replace them without an extra hidden predicate. The new plan retains 20/50 Essence calibration fees as explicit first-pass tuning, not the old arbitrary ingredient test.

The distinct proposed Survey Needle retains its separate field-seeking scope. It is not a ninth pressure instrument and is not made playable by completing these sixteen improvements.
