# Current Design — Marrick's Six-Condition Signature

**Status:** authored-direction profile with playtest thresholds.

Marrick's world is not “a battlefield.” It is a place where a group can establish repeatable routines
under sustained environmental pressure: footing holds, space is neither exposed nor cramped, water
can reach everyone, time is reliable, and the strain continues long enough for coordination to matter.

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 50 | “The ground keeps the same answer beneath several sets of feet.” | Granite |
| Relief openness | 45–65 | “There is room to stand together without losing sight of the edges that contain the route.” | Granite/Wind balance or near-baseline relief |
| Atmosphere motion | ≥ 55 | “The pressure does not choose one person; everyone in the line has to lean into it.” | Wind |
| Thermal range | ≤ 25 | “The temperature changes slowly enough that fatigue, not surprise, becomes the problem.” | Sea buffering or deliberately even thermal sources |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 85 | “Intervals return precisely enough for several people to keep the same count.” | Orrery |
| Hydrology available | ≥ 60 | “There is enough usable water along the route that one person need not carry it for everyone.” | Rain, River or Sea |

## Dependency and clue audit

- All routes are starter/research or owned well before Marrick.
- The signature never requires `Ally: HP < x, any`, Marrick's diary teaching.
- No condition promises companions, formations, structures or a fight. The passages describe footing,
  shared exposure, cadence and supply that make Marrick's practices possible.
- The Relief condition is a band rather than a one-sided extreme: protection needs room for a group
  and boundaries that remain legible.
- Low thermal range is not the same as cold or warmth. The clue promises steadiness only.

## Diary allocation

Marrick retains the approved ten-page target:

- 6 location pages, one per condition;
- 1 `Ally: HP < x, any` teaching page;
- 1 Bryn relationship/formation-origin page;
- 1 account of a routine that protected only people already included;
- 1 long-duration world, site or group-practice page.
