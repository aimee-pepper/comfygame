# Selective Schiller and independent plant counts

8 September 2026. **PROPOSAL — not a settled trait replacement or implementation assignment.** PM relayed Aimee questioning universal Schiller and suggesting plant numerousness. Universal Schiller is no longer a ready implementation mandate. The separately accepted plant support/foliage revision proceeds unchanged, including matched examples. Phone-launch confirmation is never a production dependency.

## Superseding direct decision

Aimee subsequently accepted selective iridescence for suitable some-bird/fish/reptile surfaces. The broader plant/chitin/shell candidates below are not implementation authority. Follow [the narrowed accepted contract](selective-iridescence-contract-v1-2026-09-08.md); no numerousness swap or economy change is approved. The original comparison below remains proposal history.

## Recommendation

Use **selective, explicitly source-owned iridescence** for suitable surfaces. Keep the existing independent plant structure choices and describe their actual counts. Do not replace Schiller with a universal numerousness scalar. Preserve saved Finish and all existing crafting calculations during this bounded visual-scope change; removing their compatibility dependence would require a separately reviewed economy revision.

This keeps meaningful variation without making every plant and animal share every decorative feature. Its cost is explicit surface eligibility data and some retained legacy storage. It is a staged recommendation, not a claim that keeping the old field removes all conceptual debt.

## Current implementation and relevant distinctions

Source review uses frond-component at af172dd5e747950afd314144d015b8d7ec872bad (installed354); no new native verification was needed.

- Finish stores normalized opacity, shine and Schiller totaling100. Lustre is shine plus Schiller. Surface2 renders opaque source colour, shine-driven roughness and filtered Pattern; it does not render the intended reflected angular Schiller effect.
- Creature finish generation partly follows costly ornament. Flora Finish is a free appearance axis alongside colour and habit; its generation currently includes a Schiller bias under volatile/toxic conditions. That bias does not establish a biological reason every such plant should be iridescent.
- Newer creature material selection uses actual structure for Chitin eligibility. Its capability pair nevertheless includes covering hardness and Schiller. Feather and Ichor capability pairs include derived Lustre. Flora material properties include Lustre; legacy grade/calculation paths also consume it. These are existing dependencies, not newly approved quality formulas.
- Legacy Butchery can classify a hard covering using Schiller. Creature identity and combat description code also use it, and the2D renderer has a Schiller-related highlight hue shift. These older consumers must be inventoried before a storage migration; they are not authority to invent Chitin, shells or iridescent anatomy.

Thus my earlier description of Schiller as purely cosmetic was incomplete. The proposed reflected effect is visual; the stored input already has economic and descriptive consequences.

## Proposed selective eligibility

For a future independently versioned surface path, require an explicit iridescent-surface declaration on an existing source region. Default to no new iridescent effect when that declaration is absent. An eligible declaration permits the effect; it does not force maximum intensity. Resolve source inheritance and explicit regional overrides before rendering, then freeze the result with the source recipe/observed appearance.

The initial authoring scope should allow actual scale, chitin, shell and feather surfaces, plus specifically authored flora foliage or existing display surfaces. These are candidate surface categories, not a blanket grant to every member, an assertion all those producers are delivered, or new anatomy. Only a region already substantiated by the source may receive the declaration. A hard body, positive old Schiller value, toxic world, expensive ornament or Apex label alone is insufficient. Ordinary wood, fibre and other unselected tissue retain ordinary matte/glossy rendering in this first scope.

Selective plants are useful exceptions. Primary examples support the possibility without requiring a new ecology simulation: [Begonia leaf structures](https://www.nature.com/articles/nplants2016162) and [Pollia fruit structural colour](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3465391/). This proposal imports neither their photosynthetic mechanics nor new fruit gameplay into Bookbinder.

Retain source colour and Pattern. Do not implement the missing reflection as rainbow albedo, emission or metalness. The existing rejected shader-node probe is a technical limitation, not evidence to repeat that probe or silently substitute another effect. Exact eligible source fixtures and a supported renderer path must be named before a production assignment.

## Plant numerousness: reuse independent structure first

Current plants independently choose branching, growth type, arrangement and display. Branch counts are0/2/4/6 (dense6 is woody only). Growth repetition is3–8 per attachment group, or0 when absent. Total growth copies follow repetition times max(1, branch count). Those counts and their arrangement already supply useful sparse/dense variation without a new universal stat.

There is currently one central support. Branches are not multiple stems. The display builder attaches one display group to its existing owner; a blossom uses five component copies within that group, not five flower heads. The existing grammar therefore does **not** yet fulfill single-leaf, true multi-stem or multiple-flower-head generation.

Recommendation for this revision: reuse branching, repetition and arrangement as separate source facts; do not add a composite numerousness value or new organ counts. Where an inspection description needs them, say what is actually present—such as four branches—rather than displaying a speculative numerical trait. A later concrete need for single leaves, multiple stems or multiple flower heads should extend that specific saved structure field and its actual attachment grammar. It must not reinterpret a petal/component count as a head count. These missing capabilities remain explicit future scope, not secretly completed work or automatic new requirements.

Keep costly stature/tissue/defence allocations, map habit, family weighting and cast budgets unchanged. Repeated pieces distribute the existing plant's form; they do not create additional tissue budgets, source nodes, food, yields, occupied tiles or resource units. Harvest quantity continues through its own rules. No new pollination, growth lifecycle, abundance formula or world simulation follows.

## Saved data, economy and interface consequences

Do not zero Schiller and normalize: that changes shine and therefore both roughness and material values. Moving it into shine can preserve Lustre but still makes surfaces glossier, and it does not preserve Chitin's direct Schiller capability. Replacing it with a count breaks the meaning and units of the Finish allocation. None of these is an acceptable silent migration.

For the proposed visual stage, leave old books, source records, earned stock, material quality/capabilities, prices, exact variants and crafting colour unchanged. Preserve old2D and surface2 reconstruction. New selective surface metadata must use its own frozen revision; eligibility does not alter raw Finish or automatically recalculate outputs. This deliberately retains the existing economic coupling for compatibility. Any future removal must explicitly settle Chitin capability and other Lustre consumers across the crafting system before Engineering changes them.

Player-facing descriptions should distinguish material Lustre from visible iridescence. A new surface must not be called iridescent solely because its compatibility Schiller number is positive. Apply this correction only in the new approved source/identity version, preserving saved names and old appearances. Do not expose a universal Schiller bar or replace one with a numerousness bar as part of this proposal. Continue the established compact inventory grouping: same material subtype and quality share a stack, with exact source nuances expanded on tap. Surface eligibility/count metadata is not a new stack-splitting rule.
