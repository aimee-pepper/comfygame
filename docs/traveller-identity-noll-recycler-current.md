### Voice anchors

- **Habit:** tests joins with a thumbnail or the back of a tool, then arranges removed pieces in the
  order they came away.
- **Attitude:** patient with material, blunt with claims that sentiment excuses waste or that reuse
  erases provenance.
- **Tension:** unmaking can preserve usefulness while destroying the only whole form in which a
  history was legible.

Noll is not a trader, smith or generic scavenger. Vance judges circulation and price; Noll judges
what can truthfully be recovered; Halloway judges what should be repaired or made whole.

> Because Noll was a salvager who learned to read construction as a sequence of joins, they can
> recover exact useful material without inventing provenance—and teach the Binder when dismantling
> should travel into the field.

## Gameplay contribution

- Recruiting Noll unlocks construction of the independent Recycler.
- Recycler tier 0 immediately previews and recovers authored found-gear outputs; later crafted gear
  uses its exact cumulative construction receipt.
- Baseline preview, provenance truth, atomic commit and player selection are station correctness,
  never diary or research gates.
- Noll staffs the Recycler under the shared Home keeper/effective-tier rules.
- Their combat lean grants one authored point in **Precision** and one in **Protection**. Precision
  reads joins and failure lines; Protection decides what remains whole and who bears the cost. This
  is a reversible characterization value in the shared graph, not a private Salvager class.

## Exclusive teaching — Field Separation Kit

Noll's singular diary pattern is `field_separation_kit`. It unlocks one Recycler recipe for a
single-use kit that can dismantle **one ordinary Recycler-eligible piece of found or crafted gear
during an expedition**.

First-slice boundaries:

- the field action shows the same exact input/output preview and confirmation as the station;
- it uses Recycler tier-1 efficiency and the same receipt-versus-authored-profile route regardless
  of current station tier, so it is portable convenience rather than the best recovery yield;
- using it consumes one kit and one world turn atomically with the gear-to-output transaction;
- cancel, stale inventory, no output capacity or an ineligible item consumes nothing and no turn;
- apex, unique, narrative-key and Channelworks items remain ineligible;
- it creates no scrap currency, random substitute, bonus provenance or portable Recycler menu;
- the kit is crafted at the Recycler for a reversible **5 Essence + 2 Timber + 2 Fibre**. Exact cost
  and stack limit belong in DEBUG tuning.

This is deliberately a later convenience decision rather than required opening power. Noll and the
Recycler are fully useful before the page appears. If device play shows that field dismantling only
adds inventory administration, retire the pattern and preserve its history here rather than
weakening baseline Recycler truthfulness to justify it.

## Meeting — historical first draft

The text below records Noll's first complete authored draft. The canonical current review/generation
copy now lives in `traveller-meetings-early-hinge-review.md` beside Vance, so the paired opening-
economy batch has one active source. Any later wording change belongs there and makes this historical
copy stale by design; stable exchange IDs remain the same.

**Opening:** Noll has a cracked frame across one knee. Its removed fastenings lie in a neat line,
but one damaged corner remains deliberately joined.

| Stable exchange ID | Player choice | Noll's branch-specific reply |
|---|---|---|
| `noll.join_left` | “Why leave that corner together?” | “Because the join is evidence.” They press it once and stop. “Separated pieces tell you what may be used. A joined failure tells you what the maker expected to hold.” |
| `noll.repair` | “Could the frame be repaired?” | “Yes. That is not the same as saying it should be.” They indicate the bowed edge. “Repair preserves a whole. Salvage preserves uses. Sentiment becomes waste when it refuses to name which one it wants.” |
| `noll.vance` | “You know Vance?” | “He can price the story attached to a thing. I can tell him which part of the story will survive his buyer.” A brief look up. “He dislikes that distinction most when it saves him money.” |

**Offer:** “Come back with me. We have things that need a second honest use.”

**Accepted:** “Then do not call it rescue when we take something apart.” Noll folds the joined
corner into cloth and gathers the reusable pieces separately. “Give each result its own name.”

**Declined:** “Go carefully.” They return to the failed join. “Whole ground can hide worse breaks
than this.”

The atlas must store each choice/reply as an ordered stable exchange, but selection renders only its
own reply. Exact-text review is invalidated by any prose/hash change.

## Relationships and migration

- **Vance:** price versus recoverability; neither owns the other's station.
- **Halloway:** repair versus unmaking; mutual respect without interchangeable craft.
- **Corrin:** organic layers and exact material provenance.
- **Lys:** whether a whole object is a record that disassembly can destroy.

Migrating a future combined Trading Post/Recycler prototype must create two independent station records without
duplicating stock, items, keeper assignment or progression. Existing Vance ownership remains with
Trading Post. Recycler state transfers to Noll's station only when the campaign can legitimately know
Noll; otherwise preserve it as dormant migration state until recruitment rather than deleting or
granting the traveller. New campaigns use the authored order normally.

## Acceptance fixtures

1. Both signature conditions are deliberately writable with opening vocabulary and blank worlds do
   not satisfy the pair so often that authored order loses meaning.
2. Six pages validate, each carries exactly one purpose, and the pattern cannot appear through an
   ordinary research/drop route.
3. Every meeting choice renders its own reply without reordering the scene.
4. Recycler is useful before the pattern page; acquiring the pattern changes unknown to known once.
5. Field action uses the exact ordinary recovery route, consumes one turn/kit only on committed
   success, and cannot duplicate gear or outputs across relaunch.
6. Old combined-station saves neither lose possessions nor receive Noll for free.
