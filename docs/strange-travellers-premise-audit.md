# Strange Travellers Premise Audit — Perren, Nine and Tam

**Status:** Perren/Nine directions and Tam hold approved; detailed prose remains to develop  
**Scope:** Decide which strange travellers are ready for identity writing without inventing endgame
systems to justify them

> **Signature authority:** Perren and Nine's current nine-condition profiles are in
> `traveller-signatures-perren-nine-current.md`. Tam remains held until their endgame contribution is
> concrete.

## Core recommendation

The strange travellers should not follow the trade-traveller rule that recruitment must open a
building, nor the fighter rule that recruitment must add a gambit component. Their reasons to recruit
can combine:

- a powerful late world-writing focus;
- authored access to the Sundering or other endgame knowledge;
- an unusual but ordinary build within the shared combat trees;
- a diary structure and present relationship the player genuinely wants to resolve.

This is not permission for a traveller to contribute nothing. It is a safeguard against giving every
evocative person a private meter, menu or world-generation exception.

| Traveller | Readiness | Recommended mechanical scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Perren** | Approved for full identity development | Diary-exclusive **Mirror**, Sundering/cult interpretation, shared-tree companion build |
| **Nine** | Approved for full identity development | Diary-exclusive **Dream**, shared-tree companion build; no memory minigame |
| **Tam** | Hold | Do not define the unique act until anchoring and the Great Work are settled |

# Perren — approved direction

## What is already strong

Perren's diary records his commitment to and departure from a cult across pages found out of order.
The player may understand his arguments before knowing who wrote them or which direction his beliefs
were changing. That uses the existing page system exceptionally well and should remain the centre of
his design.

## Make the cult specific

Recommendation: the group taught that the Sundering's fundamental wrong was **difference**. It sought
correspondence between worlds—matching structures, lives and events—believing that sufficiently exact
reflection would restore one coherent reality.

This is more useful than a generic sinister cult because it grows from the game's actual verbs:
writing worlds, seeking repetitions, comparing records and deciding which differences count as
errors. Its danger is not theatrical evil. It is the willingness to erase lives and worlds that do
not fit the preferred original.

This direction is approved at premise level. The group's name, origin and exact relationship to the Sundering
should wait for the broader narrative pass.

## Present identity

> Perren recognises instructions that make obedience feel like independent discovery because he once
> helped write them.

He should not be defined solely by guilt or by whether the player forgives him. In the present, he is
good at noticing how choices are framed: the missing alternative, the cost moved outside the sentence,
the conclusion a sequence of questions was built to produce.

His tension is that suspicion can become another form of control. Having escaped one total account,
he may treat every shared belief as manipulation and refuse the vulnerability of trusting anything
he did not dismantle himself.

## Contribution without a new subsystem

- **Mirror** becomes Perren's diary-exclusive focus.
- His pages and dialogue interpret cult and Sundering sites once those exist; this is authored content,
  not a separate investigation interface.
- He joins as an unusual late companion built through the existing shared trees. Exact starting lean
  should follow encounter and cult design rather than creating a private class now.

Mirror fits because reflection is both the cult's promise and Perren's problem: a returned image can
look exact while reversing orientation and omitting everything outside its frame.

**Diary target:** 12 pages. His nine-condition signature can use nine separate clue pages, plus
Mirror, one relationship/whereabouts page and one unconditioned turn page. The order-independent arc
needs a dedicated narrative-structure pass before prose.

# Nine — approved direction

## Remove the familiar amnesia trap

Nine's story should not ask “who was she really?” as though recovering an old biography would replace
the person the player met. Her missing autobiographical memory is a condition of her present life,
not a locked box containing her true character.

## Present identity

> Nine keeps promises in forms that do not require remembering why she made them.

She builds continuity deliberately through repeated practices: marks she can verify, objects placed
where tomorrow's self will encounter them, agreements witnessed by other people, and habits whose
purpose is written plainly rather than mystified. She is observant, practical and occasionally funny
about the elaborate systems other people call instinct.

Her tension is not simply fear of forgetting. A promise designed to survive memory can become cruel
when circumstances change. Nine must decide when continuity is integrity and when the self who exists
now has the right to revise an obligation made by someone inaccessible.

This separates her from:

- **Isolde**, whose concern is memory as lived continuity;
- **Lys**, who preserves external records and their contradictions;
- **Perren**, whose past beliefs remain legible but morally difficult.

Nine's question is whether chosen repetition can make a life without becoming a prison built by an
absent self.

## Contribution without a new subsystem

- **Dream** becomes Nine's diary-exclusive focus rather than an end-of-research reward.
- Dream supplies a powerful late strategic reason to seek her and write stranger worlds.
- She joins as a capable companion using existing shared trees. Her exact starting lean should emerge
  from her eventual meeting scene, not from the amnesia premise.
- Her pages may use recurring marks and repeated promises, but the player does not manage a memory
  meter, reconstruct a timeline UI or continually remind her of facts.

Dream fits because it can write a world whose logic is felt before it is explainable. It must not mean
that Nine's missing memory was “only a dream” or that her present self is unreal.

**Diary target:** 13 pages. Nine currently has a nine-condition signature; use nine condition pages,
Dream, two present-relationship pages and one page whose meaning changes depending on when it is found.

# Tam — approved hold, not removal

## Why Tam is different

Tam's current promise is mechanical: an ordinary labourer becomes the only person alive who can do
one particular thing. Until that act is defined, personality work would decorate an empty slot. If
the act concerns world survival, alteration, transit or reset—as the premise suggests—it depends on
the unresolved anchoring and Great Work designs.

Assigning Tam one of these now would be premature:

- changing a generated world's parameters after binding;
- entering, preserving or recovering a collapsing world;
- carrying matter or people through a Reality reset;
- making or repairing a permanent anchor;
- moving pages, sites or properties between worlds.

Each is large enough to reshape the campaign. None should be introduced merely to make Tam sound
important.

## What can be preserved now

- Tam had an ordinary, specific working life; do not describe them as “a nobody.”
- Their former work should provide habits and relationships that remain useful after the change.
- The unique ability should create a difficult responsibility, not reveal that Tam was secretly
  exceptional all along.
- Ten conditions remains the maximum signature size, but page count may exceed ten.
- **Glass is not reserved for Tam yet.** It remains on its existing wild/research route until both the
  quartz-versus-Glass resource decision and Tam's actual capability are settled.

## Re-entry condition

Resume Tam's identity design after the project decides:

1. the complete anchor lifecycle;
2. what the Great Work requires from the player;
3. whether a Reality reset remains part of the campaign;
4. whether Glass is a focus, a physical resource distinct from quartz, both, or one concept with two
   representations.

At that point, choose a missing player action from the settled endgame and ask what ordinary work
could plausibly teach Tam the first half of it. The transformation supplies the impossible half.

# Approved batch decisions

1. Strange travellers may justify recruitment through late strategic
   vocabulary, narrative access and shared-tree growth rather than a private subsystem.
2. Perren's present identity and the cult premise of restoring reality by eliminating
   difference; make **Mirror** diary-exclusive to him.
3. Nine's promise-and-revision identity; **Dream** is diary-exclusive to her without adding
   a memory minigame.
4. Diary targets of 12 pages for Perren and 13 for Nine, with exact prose held for structural
   outlining.
5. Tam remains on the roster, with identity development formally held until anchoring, the Great Work,
   Reality reset and Glass/quartz are settled.
