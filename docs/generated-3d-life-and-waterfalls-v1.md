## Waterfall facts and admissibility

A waterfall is a directed connection in newly generated water geometry, not a decal chosen because two blue shapes overlap. Engineering owns the generation algorithm and exact schema. Minimum saved facts are: local surface-region IDs/cells and levels; each wet cell’s actual support/bed; liquid identity and frozen status; an explicit source-region outlet lip/edge; a lower receiving region and landing position; flow direction and continuous open route from source water through lip to receiving water. Depth is surface minus bed, never a third independent value. One support per cell; no stacked walkable floor is added.

A first-slice fall requires all of the following:

- Both source and receiver are actual unfrozen liquid water with compatible identity, strictly positive depth and valid independent local surfaces. The source surface is strictly above the receiving surface.
- An explicit open outlet reaches the source surface and connects to the receiver through a positive exposed drop. Containing banks close the rest of the upstream perimeter. A lower pool behind a dry ridge, blocked lip or closed bank is not connected.
- The downward water path reaches the receiver without passing through supporting rock or an unrelated bank. The outlet width fits the source edge and receiver; its visible curtain ends at the receiving surface. Opposed edges, uphill connections, cycles and missing endpoints are invalid.
- The generator records this connectivity before final mandatory route/source checks. Screen-space overlap, global Hydrology pressure, a channel count or a non-uphill channel route alone is insufficient. The renderer consumes the accepted connection without deciding where water flows.
- Apply the operative shared crossing policy in `generated-water-surfaces-and-outlets-v1.md`: preserve every existing enterable cardinal pair, including ordinary shallow margins. Final dry support is unchanged; candidate support differences of zero are level, one to three quarters are slopes, and four quarters are a bidirectional step at existing movement cost. A larger difference rejects measured geometry for that actual connected liquid component, never the old walking connection. Deep water remains non-walkable. A real waterfall can coexist with a permitted short step; tall drops require an already non-walkable edge. This replaces the initial deep-to-deep preference and whole-shore exclusion.

**First-pass scope:** finite authored/generated flow direction and a waterfall surface are sufficient; there is no volume transfer per tick, flooding, erosion, fluid physics, swimming, new wading cost, habitat expansion, damage aura or turbine/resource output. Separate pools keep independent levels. Flat connected water can join only when physical region identity permits it; equal-level water is not a waterfall. Frozen liquid, unknown legacy geometry, unlike liquids and unsupported cascades retain their existing honest presentation. No fall is promised in every world and the two old study pools remain disconnected unless explicit generation data connects them.

**Required generation dependency:** `generated-water-surfaces-and-outlets-v1.md` supplies the one operative region/receiver/DAG, quarter-unit surface/bed, final-bank and pair-preserving edge policy. Engineering confirmed its representation is feasible; implementation and actual generated/native evidence remain pending. Capture the pre-water scaffold explicitly, use final relaxed dry banks, and atomically admit a component only when all ordinary crossings and required source access remain truthful. The mixed-depth witness demonstrates a viable ordinary-shaped case, not observed waterfall frequency. Unknown old geometry retains current presentation. Flora/fauna can proceed independently through their own source-readiness gate; raw water topology is not waterfall delivery.
