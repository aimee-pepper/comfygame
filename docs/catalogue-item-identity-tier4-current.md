# Catalogue item identity — tier-4/mythic slice

**Status:** handmade-art semantic identity brief for the eleven ordinary-progression mythic items.  
**Scope:** exact live IDs at native 32px; no wild/apex weapons or treasure objects.  
**Principle:** mythic identity comes from singular authored construction and history, not glow,
rarity borders, larger pixels or universal ornament.

## Shared restraint

- These objects may be strange, exceptionally preserved or difficult to explain. Their icons cannot
  assert magic, sentience, named owners or undisclosed mechanics absent from the catalogue/rules.
- Damage, reach, protection, initiative and rarity remain detail/state data. The object silhouette
  may truthfully express physical form but never adds a stat badge.
- Wear, asymmetry and negative space must survive literal grayscale. A colored aura is not identity.
- Preserve family ancestry only where physical: a mythical boot remains wearable footwear, but it
  does not need “one more band” than the prior tier.

## Mythic fiction boundaries

- **The Long Grievance:** uncertainty about original use is the point. Do not settle it by making it
  unmistakably a sword, agricultural hook or torture implement.
- **Crown of Quiet:** “muffles more than sound” can remain suggestive. Art must not reveal memory,
  thought control, concealment or a combat status the item does not mechanically own.
- **The Sure Hands / Willing Edge:** catalogue prose uses personifying language. Construction may
  look unnervingly precise/adaptive; eyes, faces and independent motion would assert literal
  sentience and are not authorized.
- **The First Page:** lines show that something was written, never what. It cannot leak a focus,
  traveller, Sundering claim or collectible-page identity through icon pixels.
- **The Standing Wall:** age is visible through repair and wear, not a portrait, grave mark or named
  former wearer.
