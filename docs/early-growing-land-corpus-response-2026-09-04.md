## Concrete source observations

The existing unwritten sampler selects from the whole source pool for every silent target, including an intensity up to Overwhelming. Each selected physical source contributes to every target it actually affects. Thus a completely unwritten book can combine dark, cold, saline, dry or wet conditions. The conjunction of our early ordinary-plant requirements is more restrictive than any one requirement.

The intended first authored route supplies Sun after the introductory lesson; the blank corpus does not represent that authored input. Keep the blank result and original failed review criterion visible. Compare the supported opening before choosing a broader generation change; do not lower the target or silently declare a different test equivalent.

The current early root adapter applies water availability globally, adds 15 beside a fresh shore, and excludes moisture above 75 even on dry ground. This conflates total available water with retained soil moisture. It can also make an already-suitable shore-side soil fail merely by adding the shore amount. These are design-mapping concerns; they are not proof of the size of a correction.

## Bounded proposed correction: drainage on actual Loam

**Proposed, pending the dry-root breakdown; production JSON remains unchanged.** Keep all actual world pressures and source rolls. Resolve the existing saved surface before deriving root moisture. On explicitly dry, unsubmerged **Loam** without an explicit waterlogged/flooded root state, treat that surface as draining and derive root moisture as `min(75, clamp(ordinaryAvailableWater + freshShoreContribution, 0, 100))`. The shore contribution remains 15 only at the already-defined fresh shore. The dry cutoff remains 25.

Do not apply that cap to Clay soil, Mud, standing/deep water, ice, unknown ground, or a represented waterlogged root. Keep their present water rules. Never alter actual water depth, Hydrology, salinity, thermal readings, light, chemistry or the map to manufacture a compatible root. Ordinary-water identity, salinity <=25, light >=30, the existing thermal/frozen exclusions and the twelve-tile regional threshold remain unchanged. The proposal therefore cannot make saline, lightless, frozen or submerged roots eligible by itself.

This is a candidate correction to local soil semantics, not a promise to achieve 25%. Do not launch a second twenty-minute full corpus solely to evaluate this proposal before the pending dry-root evidence identifies the affected legal Loam area.

## Evidence needed to choose the next correction

Use the promised Engineering breakdown, or a small direct extension of the existing test if it lacks it: actual unshored water availability; dry Loam/Clay counts and regional sizes; joint root-pass incidence; eligible Resin/Softwood sites. If drainage affects only a small part of the deficit, the next design decision is about early Writing/teaching access or the distribution of unwritten climates, supported by these paired inputs. It is not permission to blanket-relax every habitat threshold, give all worlds an invisible safe forest, infer freshwater from appearance, or reduce already-plentiful minerals. No new Aimee decision is required merely to obtain this breakdown.
