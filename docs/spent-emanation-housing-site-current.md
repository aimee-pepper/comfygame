# Current Design — Spent Emanation Housing Site

**Status:** Live reversible site and diary lead. Exact weight, yields and authored-but-currently
uncharged stability value remain playtest tuning.

## Identity

**Player-facing name:** Spent Emanation Housing  
**Stable ID:** `spent_emanation_housing`  
**Category:** old ruin  
**Blurb:** “The housing is empty. The stone around it is not.”

This is a failed or exhausted **thermal** Channelworks predecessor whose intended release ended before
its environmental consequence did. It demonstrates Ashe's distinction between an instrument finishing
and a place/body finishing receiving it. It does not imply Oda built it or that every old housing was
unsafe.

## Eligibility and placement

| Condition | Working threshold | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Thermal peak | >=65 | Enough heat to charge or test the old housing |
| Substrate volatile form | >=35 | Responsive material capable of carrying the effect |
| Atmosphere motion | <=30 | Residue remains locally legible instead of dispersing immediately |

- Weight: **2**
- Maximum per world: **1**
- Placement: interior, minimum 5 tiles from entry
- Exclusions: none initially

The conditions are deliberately not identical to Ashe's signature. A diary site lead should open a
related writing problem, not become a ninth password for finding Ashe.

## Interaction payload

- Search time: **2 world turns**
- Rewards follow the current site-search rules. Raw Essence is not a site reward; the earlier 3 Raw Essence + 2 Ore specification is superseded and must not be restored.
- Items/teachings: none initially
- Authored stability delta: **-4**, retained for comparison but not currently charged by worldgen

The site's value is authored interpretation and a modest recovery, not a unique weapon or mandatory
schematic. Oda's housing schematic remains her diary reward; this site cannot teach it early.

## Safety and persistence

- Inspecting/searching does not apply an affliction or unavoidable damage in v1.
- The residual thermal consequence is communicated through art and prose. The authored stability delta is not charged; appearance does not introduce damage or an active discharge.
- It is intrinsically exhausted before search. In anchored realms its recognizable structure persists after search, with unique yields remaining taken; search state does not switch an active machine off.
- Ashe's diary page reveals the definition only. It never guarantees placement.

## Live acceptance gap

Generic site reachability, placement, persistence and search fixtures cover the shared machinery,
but this named site still needs one exact content fixture proving its three-condition eligibility,
two-turn search, rewards matching the current rules with no Raw Essence payout, and searched anchored revisit without changing the intrinsically spent machine into an active one.
That is a test-coverage gap, not permission to defer or rename the live page/site.
