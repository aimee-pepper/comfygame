# Solid creature materials in equipment — cohesive optional extension

**6 September2026 · Design-authored first-pass proposal; not implemented.** Closes the assigned equipment-role disposition for the existing Fur Pelt, Scales, Chitin, Shell, Protective Spines, Feathers and Horn source policies. Some parts receive narrow useful roles; others deliberately remain raw-sale-only. This is a separate later extension, not a replacement of any complete ordinary maker batch, an automatic creature rollout or a blocker for active Tannery implementation.

Authority: [body/material sources](creature-body-material-rewards-production-v1.md), [remaining anatomy](creature-anatomy-material-extensions-v1.md), the complete Blacksmith/Weaponsmith/Armoury contracts, and [shared improvement/recovery](equipment-improvement-recovery-production-v1.md). Aimee's actual-source colour/Pattern and four-band quality decisions remain unchanged. These socket choices and numbers are Design's first-pass authoring, not new personal approvals attributed to Aimee.

## 1. Source contract remains the owner

Only actual valid typed material from the opted-in frozen anatomical manifest can enter these new recipes. Existing generic legacy family names are not matching typed sources. Keep the source identity, actual part and measurements, quantity/Anatomy rule, colour/Pattern, four-band score, nominal unit value and once-only recovery established by the source contract. This packet creates no additional body part, drop roll, subtype, species, source frequency or material bonus.

Source covering protection remains actual hardness×coverage/100; insulation remains actual length×coverage/100. Preserve the source contract's unrounded75% pair-expression +25% saved Danger, final half-up rounding and Poor/Common/Rare/Exceptional thresholds. Do not regrade Shell by weapon power, Horn by imagined hardness, or Pelt by a newly invented insulation measurement. Specimen values remain per-source facts, never fabricated averages of a species or stock stack.

Scales/chitin/shell can form protective assemblies with the existing strapping, lining and fittings. These are authored construction uses of already-declared physical coverings, not evidence that an animal's percentage coverage is literal panel area or a measurement of tensile strength. Horn's source rule establishes a useful actual cranial part; its new support roles claim neither springiness nor a sharp edge. Fur is used as enclosed/exposed lining under the existing outer and binding, retaining its own material identity. No universal Pelt→Leather conversion or new tanning producer is added.

Do not silently mix Chitin Plate with Shell or Armoured Scales in one all-of-type outer bundle. Separately named groups may use different eligible materials. All ordinary world/mineral/textile routes remain available with their current costs and progression.

It is a prepared Collar, not a raw Horn accepted directly into a Collar socket. Only Weaponsmith prepares it; Forge still owns the ordinary Iron Collar and its existing Armoury/Weaponsmith knowledge route. Armoury is not newly gated on Maud: its Iron Collar remains sufficient.

These are all the new allowlist entries. In particular there is no Shell sword, Chitin cutting edge, scaled bow limb, feather string, spine arrow, Horn haft, Horn bow spring, Horn Maul or Horn head. Bowyer and Tannery receive no new recipes from this solid-part extension; their existing and separately proposed anatomy-specific routes retain their own owners.

## 3. Statistics and workmanship — one complete contribution

Use actual part covering protection for the new **working protective panels**, applying the same established role formula as other graded structural materials:

`contribution_i = ceiling × (.5 + actualProtection_i/200) × sourceBandMultiplier_i`

Average the contributions of all actual selected panel units in that working/outer group, then use the maker's one final quarter rounding `floor(4×value+.5)/4`. Poor/Common/Rare/Exceptional multipliers remain .75/1/1.25/1.5. Do not round a mean source into a fabricated material, multiply by the number of panels, add the target's old Protection or multiply by finished workmanship again.

| Exact working role | Contribution ceiling |
| --- | ---: |
| Forge Shell Shield face |2 |
| Armoury Balanced Overlapping Scales/Chitin Body |4 |
| Armoury Balanced Overlapping Scales/Chitin other allowed slots |2 |
| Armoury Rigid Armoured Scales/Chitin Plate/Shell Body |4.5 |
| Armoury Rigid Armoured Scales/Chitin Plate/Shell other allowed slots |2.5 |

**Supports add no Power or Protection:** Horn grip, Horn Collar and Fur Pelt lining use their actual source rank in the exact logical support group they replace. They do not turn the Horn source's Crush value into weapon Power or Pelt insulation into a second ward. The material's useful support/appearance contribution remains real even if a changed grade does not cross a workmanship rounding boundary.

- Forge Horn grip: one support-group rank, exactly like its one-piece Bone grip. The separately selected point/edge alone supplies Power. Shell face supplies the working-group rank (mean of2 actual units); its brace/wrap retains the normal support aggregation.
- Armoury panel outer: primary rank is the mean actual panel ranks. Rigid secondary is its binding assembly; Balanced secondary remains the equal mean of Cloth lining rank1, binding assembly and actual Collar rank.
- Armoury Fur Pelt lining: replace the Insulated lining rank1 with the mean selected Pelt ranks. Secondary is the equal mean of that lining and the existing binding assembly; the structural outer remains the70% primary. Whole Pelt lining versus whole Cloth/Down is an explicit choice, not a hidden half-mixture.
- Horn Collar: its preserved rank replaces only the existing fitting-Collar vote in Maud's or Bracken's current support calculation. One unit/ancestor does not vote twice. Maud's Balanced/Driving fitting effects are unchanged and remain the sole source of their existing +1 Initiative or+.75 Power.

Each maker uses its existing `roundHalfUp(.70×primaryRank + .30×secondaryRank)` workmanship rule. No ordinary recipe/refit grants Peerless. Animal-free recipes still produce their authored Fine baseline; no new quality requirement or sample is added.

Armoury Heat Ward remains the existing profile table: Rigid0; Balanced Offhand5/Head8/Body15/Hands5/Feet5; Insulated Head15/Body25/Hands10/Feet10. Apply it once through the existing ward owner, with the established equipment50-point and combined60% mitigation caps. New raw insulation is historical source data, not another combat channel. No weather/cold protection, reflected spikes, elemental status, Burn immunity, durability, weight penalty or extra attack is granted by any part in this extension.

## 4. Appearance, price and recovery

Actual selected full CMY/Depth/Pattern and source/part identity survive every step. Show each protective panel's source contribution, the chosen Horn grip/Collar and the Fur lining where the actual native component is exposed. An interior lining need not be painted over the whole outer. No automatic brown Pelt, ivory Horn, grey Shell, metallic Chitin, hidden global dye or average colour. Quality-label styling remains separate from material colour. Material recipes create no new stat because the parts match colours.

Mixed actual sources remain selectable beneath type/quality grouping. If a native consumer cannot yet represent a known source's full visual appearance, retain its facts and report the exact presentation dependency; do not replace them with a guessed RGB value or commission speculative new final art. Asset work follows the actual implemented named consumer and existing provenance rules.

All listed new raw portions retain the source policy's nominal sale2/4/8/16, with buy twice sale where actually offered. Horn Collar retains its own ancestor's frozen actual value. No automatic merchant stock, markup for colour/species or future catalogue repricing of existing lots. The complete new gear nominal sale **C = sum of its active recoverable components' frozen values**, buy2C. Fuel, Essence, history and raw ancestors of prepared materials are not counted a second time.

Craft/refit/remake remains0 Essence and follows the existing maker's access and recipe identity. Keep the exact owned instance, custody, supported inscriptions/name/history and valid slot/profile; incoming components must be reserved from actual stock. Replace one whole chosen group, return only its displaced recoverable units once, retain other groups and recompute the complete result. An explicit profile rebuild still requires the full destination bundle. Unsupported old paid/unknown receipts remain on their existing safe legacy service, never silently converted.

Shell/scales/chitin panels, a directly fitted Horn grip and Pelt lining recover as their exact raw component portions under this extension. Construction shapes/fits them but creates no second stock item or offcut grant. A fitted Horn Collar recovers **the Collar only**, never both Collar and raw Horn. Preserving its ancestral receipt does not preserve a second spendable material. Existing Cloth/Cord/Leather/Haft/metal recovery remains prepared-unit-only. A returned component is detached from the item's active construction; old history cannot refund it again. Cancel, stale allocation, repeated settlement and failed save keep the existing all-or-nothing custody rules.

## 5. Materials deliberately left raw-sale-only

**Protective Spines:** the existing source establishes long hard defensive covering, not a complete measured puncturing point, straight shaft, safe gripping region or projectile assembly. No Thorn damage, retaliation or Bowyer point is inferred. Keep its existing raw trade role; don't invent a new measurement just to force a weapon recipe.

**Flight and Contour Feathers:** the current maintained Longbow defines points, limbs and string, without a separate fletching component, arrow-shaft assembly or accuracy stat. Flight on the source creature does not confer projectile performance or movement. No optional feather fee, fake Power bonus, duplicate Down, universal Brush/Fountain requirement or ornamental equipment system is added to justify the drop. Both retain their raw trade role.

Raw-sale-only is a complete intentional disposition for this first pass, not an unassigned recipe promise. It does not grant blanket approval to increase incidental yield merely to sell it. When integrating actual creature sources, use the existing small material budget and observe whether ordinary rewards are useful. No recipe is required for every part, and unused whole families need not be enabled together. A future new role would need a named player-visible component and a concrete benefit before it is queued.

## 7. Remaining decisions/dependencies and implementation order

This closes the **Design role disposition** of the existing solid families. It does not close the three broader creature goals or claim native delivery. The following is a concise dependency handoff, not an instruction to interrupt the current maker lane:

1. **Finish the already-active ordinary maker/service batches.** Full Tannery garments/one-portion Leather/prices/refit and remaining ordinary makers stay on their accepted contracts. These optional animals introduce no new prerequisite. The delivered Apothecary remains complete.
2. **When creature integration is scheduled, establish truthful bodies and exact source custody.** Use the existing body/habitat/role and cold-water policies, existing narrow Hide, specified plain Bone, then a selected actual solid covering source with its named consumer. Preserve old species/saves/rewards; don't turn on all eighteen generic families. Source and crafting adapters, source colours and normal disclosure must work together. A concrete first solid route is Shell → Forge Shield with its retained ordinary brace alternatives.
3. **Add specialist panels, lining and supports as those actual maker consumers are ready.** This packet supplies complete Armoury scaled/chitin/shell outer and Pelt lining roles, plus Horn grip/Collar uses. Need no additional recipe-cost decision. Integrate actual source selection/value/refit/recovery together; keep Spines/Feathers deliberately raw-sale-only.
4. **Integrate separately proposed new anatomy groups only with real producers.** Jaw/claw/Down/sheet/reservoir fields and the previous narrow Fang/Claw/Tusk/Membrane/fluid uses remain explicit producer dependencies. No cast frequencies have been selected or new tissues inferred from old attacks. Choose coherent generated profiles under the fixed budget before advertising rewards.
5. **Complete knowledge and ecological relations on those actual sources.** Separate Seen/encounter/recovered-material knowledge; actual low-leaf/fungal/prey witnesses and optional observed-weather notes; truthful nest evidence. These existing proposals need implementation, not more generic audit packets. Particular unsupported feeding mechanisms remain Design work: aquatic/aerial/radial/amorphous intake and chemosynthetic consumers need actual food parts/chemistry/access rather than assumed diet.
6. **Resolve natural incidence and the combined player experience through ordinary play.** Unknowns are actual source prevalence, useful recovery frequency, grade distribution, repeated-material usefulness, reachable routes, how distinctly bodies/readable materials present and whether the resulting choices are enjoyable. No measured balance result is claimed here. Adjust first-pass tuning using that concrete experience without making Aimee price individual ingredients or complete a world-material checklist.

**Genuine owner decisions:** none newly required for these bounded creature contracts. The separate known Mote-on-miss/Peerless question remains outside this slice. Dynamic wetting, weather survival, migration, seasonal breeding, nest construction and additional feather/spine equipment systems remain **unselected proposals**, not overdue implementation requirements. Broad producer selection/prevalence and unsupported diets remain named Design dependencies; current shop work does not wait for them.

**Three-goal progress:** Body → materials now has a deliberate role or raw-sale-only decision for every existing solid family, with exact source/stat/colour/value/recovery rules. Ecological coherence retains source anatomy and supported habitats without inventing biological functions to fill a recipe. The player journey now has concrete optional part-to-equipment choices and safe services; actual generation, presentation and natural exploration/crafting play remain the required completion evidence. Keep all three broad homework boxes open.

Eventual implementation should extend its existing focused source/recipe/service checks with the arithmetic and custody cases above and one ordinary source-to-consumer route. No new evaluator, broad audit, Design native check, delivery polling, configuration matrix or speculative asset commission is requested.
