### Opening runway arithmetic

The current fresh save starts at 40 Essence. Let `Bᵢ` be each actual bind cost and `Yᵢ` the retained
refined-equivalent Raw plus Spring yield from that return. The real checkpoints are:

- after Trading Post: `40 - B₁ + Y₁ - 10`;
- after Recycler: `40 - B₁ - B₂ + Y₁ + Y₂ - 25`;
- after Blacksmith: `40 - B₁ - B₂ - B₃ + Y₁ + Y₂ + Y₃ - 55`.

Using a representative 14-Essence ordinary authored bind solely to expose the shape—not as a new
fixed price—the Recommended 33-return profile leaves 49 / 53 / 42 Essence after the three builds.
The Lean 28-return profile leaves 44 / 43 / 27. Trading Post and Recycler therefore retain a healthy
two-bind margin in both comparisons. Immediate Blacksmith construction after three Lean returns sits
one Essence below two representative authored binds. Its 12 Ore + 6 Fibre requirement will often
delay construction and create another return, but random material delay is not an economy guarantee.

Keep 10/15/30 Essence as the reversible comparison profile for now. Every station construction
preview must use the campaign's real recent-median nonblank bind cost and show:

- spendable Essence now and after construction, including currently refinable Raw;
- recent-median authored binds remaining;
- restrained **Low writing runway** below two;
- a stronger factual warning below one authored bind, without blocking the player's choice or
  invoking tutorial copy.

Telemetry records the actual formula terms at each build. Reduce a station cost only if fresh-save
play repeatedly reaches the warning under reasonably explored Recommended worlds; do not increase
Raw drops merely to subsidize one overpriced building.
