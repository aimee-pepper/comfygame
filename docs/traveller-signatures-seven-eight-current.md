# Current Design — Seven/Eight-Condition Traveller Signatures

**Status:** authored-direction profiles with playtest thresholds. This band covers Sabine, Grimmond
and Oda. The profiles assume late vocabulary and refined hand capacity but never require their
owner's reward.

## Sabine — an ecology that permits repeated care

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Vitality produced | ≥ 45 | “New growth appears quickly enough that feeding leaves change rather than absence.” | Root + Bloom |
| Vitality peak | ≥ 70 | “More lives here than can use the same shelter at once.” | Producers plus Herd/Swarm |
| Vitality trophic depth | ≥ 55 | “Feeding signs overlap in layers; nothing here lives outside every other appetite.” | Producer-supported Herd and Root |
| Vitality dispersion | ≤ 38 | “Life gathers around recurring places instead of spreading itself evenly.” | Bloom |
| Hydrology available | ≥ 60 | “Water can be reached repeatedly without every return using the same path.” | Rain/River/Sea |
| Relief openness | 45–70 | “There is room to approach without removing every boundary an animal might choose.” | Moderate/balanced relief sources |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 85 | “The same interval returns often enough for a routine to become recognisable.” | Orrery |

**Identity:** abundant producer-supported life gathers around repeatable resources in a landscape with
both room and boundaries. The signature supports observation, trust and routine but guarantees no
tame animal or particular species.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Coral, Sabine's diary focus.

## Grimmond — value under load

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Illumination peak | ≤ 25 | “Even the brightest interval fails to reach the lowest exposed surfaces.” | Cloud is starter vocabulary |
| Substrate peak | ≥ 60 | “There is more valuable ground here than empty material between it.” | Gold ore/Crystal and other prior rich sources |
| Substrate dispersion | ≤ 30 | “The richest material keeps to seams narrow enough to follow.” | Gold ore or Crystal |
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 30 | “The surrounding mass resists removal strongly enough to carry what lies above it.” | Multiple Granite marks alongside the rich source |
| Relief openness | ≤ 35 | “Every route closes behind the next wall, shelf or overhang.” | Great Granite plus Ruin/Chasm |
| Relief verticality | ≥ 35 | “The ground is arranged in loads above and voids below rather than one level surface.” | Great Granite plus Ruin/Chasm |
| Atmosphere peak | ≤ 30 | “Breath thins before the stone does.” | Thin Air research |

**Identity:** a dark, enclosed, layered and thin-aired world where concentrated value exists inside a
load-bearing place. It distinguishes extraction from merely finding expensive ore.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Mercury, Grimmond's diary focus.

## Oda — unlike forces held to one boundary

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 30 | “One part of the ground holds its shape when pressure reaches it.” | Granite mixed with a volatile source |
| Substrate volatile form | ≥ 50 | “Another part answers pressure by changing, venting or carrying it onward.” | Crystal/Sulfur/Meteor mixed with Granite |
| Relief openness | ≤ 40 | “The land contains each disturbance long enough for its path to be followed.” | Granite/Ruin |
| Atmosphere motion | ≤ 20 | “Nothing in the air carries an effect away before its boundary can be observed.” | Hush |
| Atmosphere tag | `toxic` present | “A harmful trace remains after its visible source is gone.” | Miasma |
| Thermal peak | ≥ 70 | “Heat gathers strongly enough to test every join intended to hold it.” | Sun/Magma/Volcano |
| Illumination floor | ≥ 18 | “Light persists without depending on the brightest interval.” | Crystal |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 85 | “Each interval returns predictably enough to distinguish containment from luck.” | Orrery |

**Identity:** hard and responsive materials, bounded space, still air and repeatable heat/light/toxic
effects create a containment problem rather than an elemental spectacle. Oda can observe where a
force goes and whether a housing truly held it.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Oda's emanation-housing schematic.

## Validation notes

- Sabine's four Vitality measures describe different guarantees: producer base, total life, food-web
  depth and spatial gathering. They are not four synonyms for “lush.”
- Grimmond's richness and concentration do not guarantee a named ore node or deep-site placement.
- Oda's mixed hard/volatile shares intentionally require multiple substrate sources. Neither share is
  a majority claim incompatible with the other.
- Oda uses existing toxic/thermal/light phenomena rather than adding electricity, Arc, freeze or
  shock.
