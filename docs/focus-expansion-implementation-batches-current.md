# Focus expansion — implementation batches and acceptance gates

**Status:** Implementation-facing sequencing for the current 62→85 scope. Names, purposes and
acquisition bands come from `focus-expansion-85-current.md`; contribution magnitudes remain
reversible playtest values.

## Why this is batched

The expansion should not land as twenty-three names followed by later mechanics. Each batch closes
its own loop: writable focus, pressure resolution, observable world consequence, acquisition route,
Library/analysis prose and deterministic fixtures. A batch may be smaller when a consumer is not
ready; it must never be larger merely to reach the headline count.

## Batch A — ordinary missing directions

**Seven reliable research focuses:** Chill, Spring, Clay, Copper, Mountain, Plain and Grass.

This is the safest first batch because every word supplies an ordinary request the player currently
lacks and every consequence already has a live consumer:

- Chill changes Thermal without implying water;
- Spring directs flowing Hydrology rather than generic wetness;
- Clay and Copper bias matching world resources without guaranteeing a node;
- Mountain and Plain create opposite Relief/open-route goals;
- Grass requests low producing life without promising timber or tall cover.

Do not gate these behind random world discovery. Their role is to broaden dependable authorship,
and the existing research architecture is their acquisition route.

## Batch B — early discovered material/terrain texture

**Three world discoveries:** Basalt, Limestone and Dune.

These should enter the world-drop pool only after their substrate/terrain distinctions survive
native map and History review. Basalt is not Obsidian, Limestone is not generic Stone, and Dune is
moving open relief rather than a Sand resource guarantee.

## Batch C — mid-campaign reliable goals

**Four reliable research focuses:** Hot Spring, Quartz, Terrace and Trees.

This batch connects authored worlds to developed Base demand. Quartz biases a resource with real
instrument/Distillery consumption but never guarantees it. Trees requests woody/tall producers but
does not force every growth tile to yield Timber. Hot Spring and Terrace must produce visibly
different map/reading outcomes from Spring/Geyser and Mountain respectively.

## Batch D — mid-campaign discovered specialisation

**Five world discoveries:** Waterfall, Obsidian, Silver, Canyon and Kelp.

These may use the existing rune-pity path when they are a known traveller target. Waterfall and
Canyon need navigation-visible vertical/enclosed consequences; Kelp needs aquatic producer support;
Obsidian and Silver need distinct resource bias and material prose without guaranteed nodes.

## Batch E — late identity

**One late research focus:** Weight.  
**Three late world discoveries:** Eclipse, Ring and Shattered Moon.

Weight means atmospheric density/pressure only, never gravity. The three celestial focuses land
together so their distinct Cycle/light rhythms can be compared against Sun, Moon and Void in one
fixture matrix. Void suppression applies to their entire celestial causes, including secondaries.

## Acceptance gate for every batch

Before a focus becomes obtainable, require:

1. one stable source ID, shape, subject attachment, palette group, acquisition and migration entry;
2. primary and secondary pressure contributions consumed by the current resolver;
3. one single-focus generated-world fixture and one relevant compound/contradiction fixture;
4. qualitative Desk/History prose plus calibrated numeric/attribution behavior at current lens tiers;
5. Count, Intensity and Scale behavior or an explicit honest non-applicability;
6. an observable map, clock, ecology, resource-distribution or description consequence;
7. deterministic acquisition validation with no diary-exclusive reward pre-emption;
8. before/after distribution telemetry for Stability, terrain, life, resources and accidental
   traveller matches.

If an entry fails item 6, hold it rather than shipping a decorative word. If a batch shifts
blank-page worlds or traveller match rates materially, tune contribution magnitude first; do not
erase the focus's authored direction.

## Explicit boundaries

- This sequencing does not add generic inventory Glass. Glass remains outside the 23-entry scope.
- A material-named focus biases matching world resources; it does not promise a node or fixed yield.
- No new shock, gravity, cave layer or hidden biome controller is implied by these names.
- Research costs are campaign tuning data, not part of focus identity.
