# Constellation — current first slice

**Status:** implementation-ready presentation/copy correction; future constellation expansion held
behind the Great Work decision.  
**Current content:** one node, **The Long Instruction**, maximum rank 1.  
**Owner:** Reality-layer progression; no keeper.

## Honest boundary

The Constellation is not currently a skill tree. It has one permanent purchase and no prerequisites,
branches or route choice. Do not render several empty stars, invented locked nodes or connecting lines
to make it look deeper than it is.

The current native footer—“These survive everything, including a future reset. Nothing else you buy
does.”—must be removed. The game has no implemented reset, and the Great Work/Reality outcome is
deliberately unresolved in `great-work-boundary-current.md`. Player-facing copy cannot promise one
ending or claim that every other purchase will be lost.

Current truthful language:

> **The Constellation changes Reality itself, rather than one building or one person.**

The Long Instruction detail says exactly:

> **Adds one Gambit rule slot to every current and future person in this campaign.**

It does not say companion singular, survive everything, prestige, reset, new cycle or forever beyond
the save. Animal action-kit rules remain separate and do not silently inherit a human Gambit slot
unless their eventual shared rule model explicitly consumes the same Reality effect.

## Screen

- Mote wallet remains a compact header fact.
- Present the one node as a centred 64-point star/glyph in a quiet constellation field, not a
  full-width `StationCard` list row.
- The node shows unbought/affordable/shortfall/bought through redundant shape and fill.
- Tap opens an anchored edge-clamped detail with exact effect, rank `0/1` or `1/1`, cost and **Fix in
  place** action.
- The rules-owned purchase remains atomic. Insufficient Motes changes no state; purchase updates the
  same node in place.
- VoiceOver reads wallet → node/name/state → effect/cost/action → Reality-layer explanation.

At accessibility sizes the detail may become a compact sheet. There is still no vertical catalogue
or placeholder star list.

## Future expansion boundary

When at least three approved Reality nodes with actual prerequisites exist, Game Design revisits the
Constellation as a spatial graph. New stars require:

- a live effect consumer and scenario test before appearing;
- settled Great Work/Reality fiction where relevant;
- explicit prerequisites if edges are drawn;
- migration and exact Mote cost review.

Do not restore the removed `extra_symbol_slot` or `essence_head_start` fossils merely to populate the
screen. A small truthful constellation is better than a decorative roadmap of unavailable systems.

## Acceptance

1. Release UI contains no “future reset,” “survive everything,” prestige or total-loss promise.
2. Only the one live catalogue node renders; no fake locked content or edge appears.
3. Buying The Long Instruction adds exactly one Gambit slot to every eligible current/future person,
   survives relaunch and cannot be bought twice.
4. The Mote cost remains catalogue-owned and insufficient/stale purchase is atomic.
5. 368×800, grayscale, large-text and VoiceOver proofs remain legible without a full-width list.
