## All four coatings — accepted excursion lifetime

Venom, Firebrand, Briar Oil and Flashsalt prepare one eligible physical weapon using the existing legal preparation action and its existing action cost. One bottle is spent on successful preparation. Do not add automatic preparation at craft, packing, departure or tool selection. This batch does not invent a new out-of-combat action or change Channelworks eligibility.

Persist the preparation on the exact weapon with the active excursion identity, outside encounter-only state. Hits, misses, target defeat, end of encounter, travel, permitted weapon custody changes, backgrounding and cold relaunch do not consume it. A successful eligible hit applies the existing corresponding affliction: Poison base 2 damage/4 boundaries, Burn 4/2, Bleed 2/3, Dazzle its existing 2-boundary effect. Existing actor modifiers, same-kind refresh, prevention, cures and target-death rules remain. Individual afflictions keep their own duration; excursion-long coating is not permanent Poison on a target.

A different coating requires one explicit legal preparation and replaces the old one; no hidden stacking or free return of the bottle. Unequipping does not move the preparation to the new weapon. If a permitted transfer removes the weapon from use, retain only the original weapon's receipt while the same excursion remains active. Ending that excursion through Return, actual defeat/forced exit or another existing completion clears it once; it never follows into a later world. Older active encounter coatings migrate to the same weapon for the remaining excursion, not to inventory or a new weapon. Mere inventory ownership never becomes a free preparation. An unknown or mismatched old excursion/weapon identity cannot be guessed; preserve the item and use an explicit unsupported legacy fallback rather than cross-world buff creation.

The longer duration is the accepted design and may need later effect/availability tuning after normal play. Do not revert to one-strike or charge new Essence to avoid balancing the four effects together.
