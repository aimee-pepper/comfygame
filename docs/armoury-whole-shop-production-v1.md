The following are **complete profile-role baselines**, not offsets added to the target's old Protection or six-band tier. Retain each source's own quality and measurements; never fabricate averaged Bone/Leather.

For each selected structural Bone i, contribution is `ceiling × (.5 + actualDensity_i/200) × bandMultiplier_i`. Average these contributions within the complete structural group, then round the finished Protection once with `floor(4 × Protection + .5)/4`. Keep quarter precision and the existing one-decimal preview.

Only the structural outer contributes to this Protection. Its quantity is construction cost, not another multiplier. Binding/lining/Collar does not add hidden Protection, Initiative, reactivity, ward, HP or old grade bonuses. Do not retain Tannery's separate garment+.25 closure contribution, Forge offsets, a current Armoury legacy offset, Reforge rank or old base-item Power underneath the new full profile result. ## 5. Workmanship and meaningful mixed loadouts

Four finished bands remain Rough/Fine/Superior/Exceptional, ranks0/1/2/3. Ungraded stock votes1; Bone/Leather/Bone Collar uses its exact preserved band. Primary group is the structural outer, averaging multiple creature-part ranks. Secondary groups are explicitly:

- Rigid: binding assembly only.
- Balanced: mean of Cloth lining1, binding assembly and fitting Collar.
- Insulated: mean of Cloth lining1 and binding assembly.

A binding assembly is Cord rank1 or the mean of its Leather ranks. An Iron Collar is1; Bone Collar keeps its actual band. Compute `roundHalfUp(.70 × primaryRank + .30 × secondaryRank)`. Multiple physical units in a group do not add extra group votes; raw ancestors do not vote twice. This explicitly designated structural strapping differs from Tannery's minor closure no-vote rule. Cloth lining has no fake Poor/Exceptional band. No creature inputs yields Fine, and no ordinary rebuild/refit yields Peerless.

Examples (Protection is equipment contribution before the existing wearer's combat calculation):

- Ingot Rigid Body:3.25 Protection/0 Heat Ward, Fine with Cord binding. Ingot Balanced Body:2.5/15, Fine with Cloth/Cord/Iron Collar. Cloth Insulated Body:2.0/25, Fine with Cord.
- Common Bone Density50 Rigid Body: `4.5 × .75 =3.375`→3.5 Protection. The same Common source as Balanced Body gives3.0/15. No old item stat is added.
- Common Leather coveringProtection50: Balanced Body3.0/15; Insulated Body2.25/25. A previous Tannery Leather Guard might have3.25 Protection; the preview shows the deliberate Protection-for-Heat-Ward trade, not an unconditional upgrade claim.
- Balanced Body with Poor Bone Density20 and Exceptional Density80: mean `(1.8+5.4)/2=3.6`→3.5 Protection and15 Heat Ward. Cloth/Cord/Iron Collar supports give rank1.35→Fine; real source parts remain distinct.
- Exceptional structural Bone with Exceptional Leather bindings can give Exceptional Rigid/ Balanced workmanship; an ungraded Cord binding can leave the same Rigid outer at Superior. This changes the honest workmanship label, not an extra hidden Protection multiplier.

At ordinary world-material baselines, five Rigid pieces total **11.25 Protection/0 Heat Ward**; five Balanced pieces total **8.5/38**. Insulated Body+Head+Hands with Rigid Feet+shield totals **8.0/50**. Wearing Insulated Feet as well would lower that set to7.0 Protection while its raw Heat Ward60 still caps at50; the comparison must disclose that wasted ten points. These examples deliberately make a mixed set useful rather than requiring all five slots to copy one profile. They are first-pass arithmetic, not claims of measured encounter balance or immunity.
