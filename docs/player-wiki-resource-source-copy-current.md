# Player Wiki resources — implemented source copy V1

**Publication status:** player-facing source copy for the 23 current resource records and their reachable
consumers at `a93077f1a13871809d61baf5d6510436e8ad449a`. No proposed sink, recipe substitution, purchase contract,
late-building signature or extraction gate appears here.

World conditions below describe where a resource is more likely to exist. They never guarantee an unseen
node, plant, creature or site in a particular world. Publish a resource's acquisition details only after
the player has legitimately learned that resource.

## Resource forms

Most pages describe **counted resources** kept in the expedition haul and Base resource stores. Physical
making may instead ask for an **exact material** with family, properties and provenance. Those forms are
not interchangeable. Recipe details always state which form is required.

Trading prices follow the current bands: staples sell for 1 Gold and buy for 3; uncommons sell for 2 and
buy for 6; rares sell for 5; precious resources sell for 12. Rare and precious resources do not appear in
ordinary rotating resource stock. Raw Essence and Motes are not trade goods.

**See also:** [Resources and Making], [Exact Materials], [Trading Post], [Resource Extraction].

## Rubble

**Finding it:** exposed Rank-0 deposits in hard, broken, high-relief ground. Rubble is staple Trading stock.

**Current uses:** counted Rubble has no current building, recipe or research cost. It can be sold. An exact
stone material obtained through a legitimate exact-material route may still fit a physical component whose
family and properties accept it.

**See also:** [Resource Extraction], [Trading Post], [Exact Materials].

## Iron Ore

**Finding it:** exposed Rank-0 deposits in hard, stony ground; ductile substrate favours useful ore. Iron
Ore is staple Trading stock, appears in current authored site rewards and can return through fixed legacy
Recycler profiles.

**Building material:** Blacksmith 12, Survey Post 8, Reliquary 12, Armoury 28, Weaponsmith 32,
Channelworks 50 and Anchorage 40.

**Other current uses:** counted Iron Ore pays current instruction, permanence, Bargain, Shelving and Satchel
research, including all nine Shelving tiers and the reinforced/deepened/strap/pannier Satchel tiers. It also
pays the current maker broaden/masterwork studies, Deepen the Spring, Write Your Own Hand and Peace.
Exact ore materials can serve compatible physical weapon, protection and fitting components.

**See also:** [Blacksmith], [Survey Post], [Reliquary], [Armoury], [Weaponsmith], [Channelworks],
[Anchorage], [Shelving], [Field Kit Capacity], [Recycler], [Exact Materials].

## Raw Essence

**Finding it:** direct loose pickups while exploring and the current rewards from certain searched sites.
Pressure affects direct-pickup frequency and yield. Raw Essence is not tradeable.

**Processing:** return it to Home and refine it at the Essence Spring. Each unit normally becomes 2 Essence
Crystals; Second Pass raises the rate to 3. Continuous Settling can automatically refine the Raw Essence
returned by its exact completed expedition when enabled.

**See also:** [Raw Essence Pickups], [Sites], [Expedition Return], [Essence Spring], [Essence Crystals].

## Mote

**Finding it:** permanent Reality-touched rewards from current authored discoveries, including particular
sites and cache outcomes. Motes bank into Reality rather than ordinary Base resources and are not tradeable.

**Current uses:** Constellation progression and one Mote in the Waystone recipe. No ordinary building uses
Motes.

**See also:** [Motes], [Constellation], [Waystone], [Sites], [Locked Caches].
