# Current Design Audit — Glass, Quartz, Rift-glass and the Waystone

**Status:** Current role audit with a reversible Waystone playtest placeholder; no Tam ownership is
assigned and the permanent recipe decision remains for Aimee's review.

## Three concepts currently exist

| Concept | Live state | Current purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Quartz** | Implemented world resource | Ordinary-to-late precision material: instruments, page lenses, pen work, Station costs and Farsight Draught |
| **Rift-glass** | Implemented rare world resource | Material from valuable unstable ground; used in Stillwater and the instability lens |
| **Glass** | Proposed world-writing focus only | “Ground fused”; listed as a Strange focus with a normal mid research/wild route, but no live pressure source or physical resource |

These should not be treated as spelling variants. Quartz is a natural crystalline precision material.
Rift-glass is reality-stressed material whose identity depends on unstable ground. Glass, if retained as
a focus, describes fused terrain and does not automatically add a generic inventory resource.

## Generic Glass resource conclusion

**Recommendation:** do not add a generic `glass` resource merely because old recipes use the noun.

- Current recipes already name Quartz when they need clear/lens-grade material.
- Rift-glass already serves rare reality-sensitive craft.
- Adding generic Glass would create a third hard/lustrous stock with no distinct recurring decision.
- A future recipe that specifically needs panes, vessels or ordinary fused silica can consume a
  property-qualified world resource or justify Glass then. No current system needs it.

This keeps the **Glass focus** available as world vocabulary without assuming that placing it yields
inventory Glass. Focuses describe worlds; resources still emerge through allocator requirements.

## Waystone material

The live repeatable Waystone recipe consumes one Adamant, one Mote and 30 essence. Anchor Frames no
longer use Adamant, but Adamant still gates permanent high-order instruments and analysis. A repeatable
emergency-return consumable competes badly with that progression role.

**Reversible playtest placeholder:** replace Waystone's Adamant with one Rift-glass while retaining
one Mote, 30 essence and the hardness-qualified world-resource sample.

- Rift-glass matches the fiction: the Waystone forces a path through an unstable boundary.
- It is rare and deliberately writable, so Waystones remain meaningful rather than routine escape spam.
- Consumption asks the player to spend interruption-sensitive material on interruption insurance.
- Adamant remains associated with enduring precision/permanent progress rather than being burned on
  every emergency return.

This remains DRQ-026 playtest, not a permanently settled recipe. Engineering may implement the
substitution now because Aimee explicitly authorized reversible placeholders where waiting would
block progress. Preserve the recipe as tuning data and compare Waystone crafting/use against both
Rift-glass acquisition and Adamant's permanent progression demand before final disposition.

## Tam boundary

- Tam owns none of Quartz, Rift-glass or Glass while held.
- The Glass focus remains on its ordinary proposed route and may be implemented independently if its
  pressure profile proves useful.
- Tam's eventual act should choose its material because the act needs it, not inherit Glass because an
  old roster table left the word in that row.

## If Glass focus enters implementation

Use a conservative Substrate/thermal identity: fused hard ground, low dispersion, a past high-heat
trace. Do not grant a Glass resource automatically, duplicate Mirror's reflection, or duplicate
Rift-glass's unstable-ground identity. Exact pressure numbers wait for the wider non-diary vocabulary
pass because Glass currently has no diary owner or progression dependency.
