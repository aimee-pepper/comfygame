### Why these parts fit

Fen cuts and laminates the two Hardwood Logs into the bow's two bending limbs during construction; Resin binds that assembly. A raw sample labelled Timber, a Softwood Log, or an ordinary Bone is not automatically a spring limb. **Hardwood is the declared first-pass physical allowlist**, not a claim that every real-world hardwood species is equally elastic. The prepared limb construction supplies the authored functional baseline; no hidden springiness measurement or real-time seasoning requirement is invented. Each limb retains the actual selected Log's source appearance.

Longbow's additional points are a deliberate construction change. Bone belongs in hard points, where its actual skeletal density can matter, rather than pretending all ordinary bones can bend into a bow. Projectile stock is maintained within the weapon, just as Sling's existing projectile component is; it is not a new ammunition inventory. The points and limb colours are distinct, and a coating is presented on the weapon's working projectile treatment, not as poison poured onto its string.

Clay shot is shaped and fired as part of Fen's single Sling craft, consuming the listed Coal. There is no intermediate fired-shot item or extra kiln/building requirement. The firing is a construction operation at the implemented Bowyer consumer, not a new field action. Ingot or Bone shot is shaped during that same craft and consumes no extra Coal. Fibre/Cord/Cloth is never the hard shot. Quartz is admitted for piercing Longbow points only in this batch, not a slicing Throwing edge.

The carrier/pouch is real functional construction, not a consumable satchel slot. Cloth and Leather are genuine alternatives; ties/cords still use prepared Plant Cord. No raw Hide string, generic Fibre pouch, finished equipment input, universal Reagent or fictitious material-measurement sample. Horn, Quill, Tusk, Shell, Chitin, Claw, Obsidian, Copper and Adamant remain their existing legacy/authored scope; this batch does not invent a typed producer simply because an old allowlist included them.

## 5. One final Power calculation and explicit workmanship

Far reach already changes tactical behavior. First-pass ranged working values deliberately sit below comparable Forge working values; do not also apply a new blanket ranged damage penalty in combat.

| Working choice | Contribution before final item rounding |
| --- | ---: |
| Iron Ingot point / shot / one throwing edge | 2.0 Power |
| Quartz Longbow points | 1.75 Power |
| Fired Clay Sling shot | 1.5 Power |
| Bone point / shot / one throwing edge | `3 × (.5 + actualDensity/200) × bandMultiplier` |

Poor/Common/Rare/Exceptional multipliers are .75/1/1.25/1.5. Longbow and Sling use their single working group's contribution. Throwing Set averages the two independent edge contributions; it does not add them as two hits. Quantity2 of Quartz or Clay is a construction cost, not twice the Power. Round the complete final Power once with `floor(4 × Power + .5)/4`, retaining quarter precision and the existing one-decimal display. No old quality-base, specialist offset, material-effect bonus or second quality multiplier is added afterward.

Support materials provide construction, source appearance and the specifically designated workmanship vote below. They add **no Power, Initiative, ward, HP, armour piercing beyond the existing Pierce rule, intrinsic Poison/Burn/Bleed/Dazzle, hidden reactivity effect or projectile count**. Resin and Coal never vote on quality; Coal is fuel, not a recoverable component. This packet does not turn ingredient properties into automatic weapon statuses.

Workmanship ranks are 0/1/2/3 for Rough/Fine/Superior/Exceptional. Ungraded material votes1; Bone or Leather votes its actual Poor/Common/Rare/Exceptional rank. Apply the accepted `roundHalfUp(.70 × primaryRank + .30 × secondaryRank)` to these exact logical groups:

| Family | Primary rank | Secondary rank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Longbow | Selected maintained-points group; Quartz/Ingot1, Bone's actual rank | Mean of limb assembly1 and string1 |
| Sling | Clay/Ingot1 or selected Bone rank | Mean of Cord assembly1 and pouch's actual rank (Cloth1 or Leather band) |
| Throwing Set | Mean of edge A and edge B ranks | Mean of carrier body rank and ties1 |

Here the string, sling cords, pouch/carrier and throwing ties are explicitly designated structural supports. This does not retroactively make every minor Cord tie a quality socket: Tannery's existing minor garment closure still has no vote. Resin bonding/fuel remains outside the calculation, and raw ancestor Fibre does not create extra votes. Longbow's updated primary group is deliberately its working projectile assembly, replacing the older limb-only quality grouping; there is no second old quality calculation underneath it.

All-world construction is Fine. Exceptional Bone with entirely ungraded supports yields rank2.4→Superior; exceptional material does not automatically mean every part of the completed weapon is exceptional. A Sling/Throwing Set with Exceptional Bone work and Exceptional Leather support has secondary mean2, total2.7→Exceptional. Ordinary crafting/refit never creates Peerless.

Worked examples:

- Ingot Longbow: Power2.0, Fine, nominal sale9/buy18.
- Common Bone Density60 Longbow: `3 × .8 = 2.4`→Power2.5, Fine, sale9/buy18. Its Power is not promised final HP loss.
- Rare Bone Density60 Sling with Cloth pouch: Power3.0, rank1.7→Superior, sale12/buy24.
- Throwing Set with Poor Bone Density20 and Exceptional Bone Density80: mean `(1.35 + 4.05)/2 = 2.7`→Power2.75. Cloth+Cord support gives rank1.35→Fine, sale21/buy42. Exceptional Leather carrier instead gives rank1.65→Superior and sale32/buy64. The two original Bone pieces remain intact in the receipt.
- At Density100 and Exceptional band, Bone Power4.5 compares with the Forge's same-source ceiling6.0. This is an authored equipment comparison, not an alteration of battle damage or a tested claim that every ranged build is balanced.
