# Current Design Audit — Adamant Demand and Anchor Frame Recipe

**Status:** Anchor Frame recipe resolved for implementation. Waystone substitution is a reversible
playtest placeholder; existing permanent Adamant progression remains current.

## Live demand

Adamant currently serves two different economic shapes:

| Use | Demand shape | Current amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Chronometer / late Cycle instrument | Permanent one-time progression | 2 |
| Attribution lens tier | Permanent one-time progression | 2 |
| Living-layer/Vivometer tier | Permanent one-time progression | 4 |
| Waystone | Repeatable emergency consumable | 1 each |
| Older Tether-iron / anchor proposal | Repeatable/portfolio expansion | proposed, not current |

The eight Adamant in permanent late analysis progression is coherent: reading Cycle and the living
layer are deliberately among the hardest measurement capabilities. The overload comes from also
making every escape and every manually anchored realm compete with those permanent upgrades.

## Decision: Anchor Frames do not require Adamant

Anchor Frames use property-matched **world resources**, not a named endgame ore:

| Requirement | Quantity | Minimum property |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rigid members | 2 | hardness 65 |
| Load-bearing members | 2 | density 65 |
| Binding/hinge material | 1 | flexibility 55 |
| Responsive key material | 1 | reactivity 65 |
| Binding work | — | 60 essence |

- Six distinct pieces are consumed; one piece cannot satisfy two recipe slots.
- Matching consumes the weakest qualifying pieces first under the shared crafting rule.
- The recipe is made at the Anchorage and produces one carried Anchor Frame.
- These are player-facing **world resources**, not “material samples.”
- Exact thresholds, quantities and essence are debug/playtest values. The property pattern is current.

This makes the crafted route expensive through breadth and quality of recovered worlds rather than
through one named bottleneck. It also supports the game's substitution/hoarding pillar: many strange
creatures or flora can supply a valid frame.

## Adamant's retained identity

Adamant remains:

- a precious, extremely rich world resource;
- a late precise focus and reward for difficult world-writing;
- a permanent analysis-progression material, especially for Cycle/living measurement;
- available for selected apex or exceptional authored recipes only when those recipes genuinely need
  its identity.

Do not use Adamant as the default synonym for “late recipe.” Advanced armour, weapons, Anchor Frames
and every final-tier station should not all converge on it.

## Reversible playtest placeholder: Waystone uses Rift-glass

The Waystone is interruption insurance: immediate return with the full haul. Charging one Adamant on
every use may make players hoard the safety tool until it stops serving that purpose.

Current playtest substitution:

- replace its one Adamant with **one Rift-glass**;
- retain one Mote and 30 essence;
- preserve its effect unchanged.

Rift-glass expresses crossing a threshold more directly and keeps Waystone costly without competing
with permanent analysis. Engineering may replace the implemented Adamant now as a reversible,
data-driven playtest change. This does not permanently settle DRQ-026; review the substitution after
both Rift-glass acquisition and repeated emergency-return use are playable.
