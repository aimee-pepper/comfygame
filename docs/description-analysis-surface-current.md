# World description and analysis — current disclosure boundary

**Status:** Current audit and implementation correction. The five-tier progression is settled; the
Tier-3 calibration leak and History copy mismatch are correctness fixes.  
**Updated:** 9 Aug 2026

## The surface is doing the right jobs

The Writing Desk correctly separates four kinds of knowledge:

1. **Prose:** always available for the subjects the page actually touches. This is the diary-clue
   deduction surface and never becomes a condition checklist.
2. **Uncertainty:** unwritten subjects produce Stability/turn ranges and an explicit count of
   subjects left to chance, without revealing their rolled identities.
3. **Measurement:** Tier 2 shows calibrated subjects only, at crude/good/fine precision.
4. **Explanation:** Tier 3 attributes focus effects, Tier 4 explains Stability/contradiction, and
   Tier 5 describes generated-life tendencies.

The pre-bind Stability headline remains available from the start. It is a binding-risk promise, not
an instrument reading. The exact cost is certain; Stability and turns are ranges while any subject
is unwritten.

The “What you wrote” readback also remains available at Tier 1. A player never needs an instrument
to be told which marks they joined or that a qualifier they placed says nothing; that is feedback
about the page interaction, not hidden world knowledge.

## Tier-3 calibration leak

The live Desk currently reveals every written focus's exact primary and secondary contribution once
Tier 3 is reached, even when the affected subject has never been surveyed. That bypasses the settled
rule that the page lens only reads subjects calibrated through field instruments.

Current correction:

- Tier 3 may attribute an effect only when its affected subject is calibrated in Reality;
- for a calibrated subject, show the focus → subject relationship and its deterministic scaled
  contribution; instrument precision still governs the resolved world reading, not the focus's
  authored/scaled contribution;
- do not show an “unknown secondary exists” placeholder for uncalibrated subjects, because that
  itself reveals the discovery;
- the primary page link remains visible at every tier, but its numeric effect remains hidden until
  that subject is calibrated;
- Tier 4 marginal Stability lines may still name the focus the player wrote, because the Stability
  contribution is the Tier-4 capability; they must not expose an uncalibrated secondary reading.

This preserves the Tier-3 revelation—“Sun has been warming my worlds”—while requiring the player to
have actually measured Thermal before the lens can teach it.

## World History consistency

History is permanently re-readable with current knowledge, which is correct. It must use the same
calibration and precision gate as the Desk:

- Tier 2 lists only calibrated subjects recorded in that world's saved readings;
- the pencil/die marker may reveal whether each readable subject was written or rolled;
- Tier 3 focus-attribution lines are filtered by calibrated affected subject just like the Desk;
- Tier 5 living analysis remains a distribution/tendency view, never exact undiscovered species
  identity.

The current Tier-3 History note says “You can read all of it,” while the actual list correctly hides
unmeasured subjects. Replace that promise with: **“You can trace the effects you have learned to
measure, including what the world decided for itself.”**

## Tier presentation

- **Tier 1:** qualitative prose; page-chain readback; inert-mark warning.
- **Tier 2:** calibrated resolved readings at instrument precision.
- **Tier 3:** calibrated focus attribution, including discovered secondaries; no Stability judgement.
- **Tier 4:** derived red/green Stability attribution, named contradictions, disclosed escalation
  and greed/contradiction/size/danger numerical breakdown.
- **Tier 5:** allocator-derived flora and creature tendencies and likely ecological roles.

Do not expose raw internal tags, catalogue IDs, exact spawn seeds or undiscovered species names on
these player-facing tiers. Those belong only in DEBUG diagnostics.

## Accessibility and phone readability

Red/green underlining is redundant visually because it adds a line as well as colour, but a screen
reader still needs the meaning. At Tier 4, each marked clause should expose “stabilising” or
“destabilising” in its accessibility value. The Stability range, wrongness warning and named
contradictions remain separately voiced text.

If eight prose clauses plus chains/readings overflow a phone screen, collapse **What you wrote** and
advanced analysis sections independently after their headings; never truncate the prose sentence or
hide the Stability headline. Remember expansion state only as a UI preference, not game state.

   not its warming secondary.
2. After one valid Thermal survey, the same saved page and historical world reveal that secondary.
3. Crude/good/fine changes resolved-reading precision but not deterministic focus-contribution math.
4. Tier 4 marks arise from current marginal Stability arithmetic, never authored clause polarity.
5. A blank page shows uncertainty without describing rolled subjects before bind.
6. Desk and History expose the same calibrated subjects at the same tier.
7. VoiceOver conveys stabilising/destabilising meaning without relying on colour or underline.
