# Creature natural prevalence — bounded acceptance plan

**6 September2026 · Design plan only; no new run performed or frequency chosen.** Follows the actual-source, feeding-support and equipment-role contracts. This describes how to answer the remaining natural-availability and player-experience questions using the existing ordinary world, encounter, reward, Return and crafting observations. It is not a new corpus, seeded census, telemetry system, evaluator or native assignment for Design.

## When it becomes meaningful

Use this plan **after Engineering integrates one named source with its actual consumer and lawful disclosure**, preserving source/part quality/colour/value and ordinary services. Before that, a failure to find an unimplemented material says nothing about generation balance. Continue the ordinary Tannery/maker lane without waiting for this creature work. Keep the actual implemented policy/receipt version attached to any eventual observation; never mix old generic projections with new typed sources in an availability claim.

A concrete first pair is the optional **actual Shell → Forge Shield face**, retaining the existing ordinary Shield alternatives and brace. Use whichever exact source/consumer pair PM actually schedules; this plan does not enable Shell or prescribe a special starting seed. Apply to food claims only after the three-profile source relation is actually implemented; don't pretend this plan learns an animal's diet from its attack or picture.

## One ordinary route, then only a justified follow-up

Observe the first normal excursion used for the integrated source/consumer's acceptance, with the player's real tools, unlocked makers and inventory. Use the chosen route and natural encounters; do not inspect hidden cast/source tables, inject resources, reroll until the wanted species appears, require a full map clear or make Aimee travel to an arbitrary quota of worlds.

Record a short plain-language account from **existing** sighting/encounter knowledge, actual committed reward/source receipts, Return outcomes and crafting quotes/history. The normal implementation check may already supply it; reuse that result. No new recording framework, screenshots, baseline configuration matrix or repeated phone installation is required. The account needs only the actual facts relevant to the questions below, not a transcript of every movement.

1. What the player was trying to make or explore, with current maker/tool access and starting relevant stock.
2. What actual sources/creatures were seen on the chosen route, which were legally reachable/contactable, what was actually encountered/defeated/harvested, and why the player continued or turned back.
3. What material was actually recovered, its named part/type/quality/source colour, what was kept or lost at Return, and what existing stock completed the recipe. Do not credit pre-supplied or older stock to this excursion.
4. What the actual quote offered, what changed in the preview, what was paid/returned, and whether the finished piece made the source choice understandable and worthwhile.

A second ordinary route is useful only if the first leaves a concrete unanswered question: for example the same reachable source repeatedly lacks a valid reward, one environment plausibly supports the new forage while another does not, or the optional material cannot be used despite the named shop being ready. Use a naturally encountered contrasting case or the next ordinary excursion. If it never occurs, record **not observed** and leave that claim unresolved. Do not launch a seed corpus to manufacture an answer or claim that a small sample estimates world frequency.

## Concrete questions and evidence

| Question | Existing observation that can answer it | What a concern means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the player recognize a potentially useful material without knowing hidden anatomy? | Actual full-sight/Look/encounter explanation, then the committed source part | Contradictory or fabricated anatomy/disclosure is a source/knowledge defect; a hidden internal part need not be obvious at long distance |
| Is a seen source actually obtainable through the accepted movement/tool rules? | Chosen route, actual passability/contact and tool response | Deep-water sightings do not count as reachable reward opportunities. No remote kill/harvest is added to improve the numbers |
| Can optional creature parts improve a chosen construction without becoming mandatory progression? | Exact new-material quote alongside the already-complete ordinary recipe | A new animal-only tool/foundation/material prerequisite is a contract violation, not a reason to inflate creature spawns |
| Is the recovery useful relative to the current optional bill? | Exact reward quantity/Anatomy and kept stock versus the actual component count | One successful defeat need not finish an optional item. Repeated unusably small yields are a tuning question, with the real route/effort recorded |
| Are materials repeatedly interesting rather than an automatic sale routine? | Which observed source choices changed a component, appearance, statistic or useful stock; what was sold and why | Repeated low-use rewards can justify reducing/enabling a family selectively. They do not require a new recipe for every Feather/Spine |
| Does quality do what the player expects? | Actual selected source measurement/band, resulting stat and workmanship preview | A rare support may leave rounded stats/label unchanged; false preview promises are defects. One trip cannot establish a representative quality distribution |
| Do colour and Pattern survive the useful journey? | Observed source, actual harvested/reward lot, selected component and supported return/refit receipt | Lost/averaged/guessed colour or duplicate recovery is a defect in that slice, not a new palette or asset assignment |
| Does the creature's claimed food relation have real support? | Existing generated relation's permitted evidence and actual reachable source facts inspected through the established implementation path | A false/absent witness invalidates that claimed link; an intentionally unspecified diet is an honest supported outcome, not proof of starvation |
| Does the world invite exploration without making completion the objective? | Why the player chose a branch or Return, what the next optional goal was, and whether ordinary progression forced clearing the map | A forced full-clear dependency needs recipe/source/progression adjustment. Not seeing every species or material is expected and is not a failure |
| Do encountered creatures feel distinct and coherent? | A concrete comparison among actually encountered bodies, movement, material possibilities and supported behavior | Identify the specific confusing sameness or contradiction; don't infer that every world must contain all seven shapes or four habitats |

## How to interpret the result

Keep **seen**, **contactable**, **encountered**, **defeated** and **recovered** separate. If a compact count helps, its denominator is only the actually observed opportunities on that route and under those tools/policies. It is not the entire generated cast, total world population, implied spawn chance or a completion percentage. A missed Hide recovery remains subject to its accepted70% rule; it is not proof that the species has no covering. Other typed sources keep their own exact reward rules.

An excursion with no encounter of the selected optional material establishes **no observation**, not zero prevalence or a broken generator. Losing a real reward before Return establishes custody outcome, not failure to spawn. Having the material but lacking an intentionally later maker establishes progression context, not an unusable body. Missing source knowledge and missing source stock are also different issues. Do not change frequencies from a misclassified observation.

Hard source, custody, recipe or disclosure contradictions should be corrected in the exact implementation slice before expanding it. Keep other delivered shop work available. Soft balance concerns need a concrete example and one named proposed adjustment—such as component quantity, useful family inclusion or later source tuning—with the accepted ordinary alternatives intact. Do not choose percentages, cast quotas, drop guarantees or target grade frequencies before there is actual evidence to justify them.

The smallest useful handoff is: **what was observed; what was not established; the specific defect or balance question; the next bounded change or observation that would resolve it.** No new audit packet series or repeated unchanged status report. Engineering owns its focused implementation/native acceptance; Design uses that evidence to make the actual mechanic/tuning choice, and the Wiki changes current behavior only when delivery is reported.

## What this can and cannot close

This plan can establish that an implemented source/consumer journey works and provides an intelligible optional choice on a real route. It cannot certify global spawn rates, an entire food web, sufficient calories, universal all-world availability or complete creature variety. Broad source prevalence, unsupported diet mechanisms and actual long-term play appeal remain honestly unresolved until relevant evidence or a selected design choice exists.

All three creature goals keep their existing boxes open. Body/material role disposition is specified; exact producer/consumer integration still needs completion. Food support has the finite supported/unspecified boundary; no hunger, breeding, migration or chemical-animal simulation is assumed. The complete player experience requires actual coherent exploration/crafting, not a checkbox for finding every part. No new Aimee decision or recipe-pricing chore is created by this plan.
