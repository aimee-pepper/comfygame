# Sigil Purpose Audit — which focuses have a reason to exist

> *"why would someone choose this if they need a lit world vs something like sun? this doesn't make sense. audit for any other sigils that don't have real purpose."*

**The test applied to every focus:** *is there a situation where a player would deliberately choose this one over its neighbours?* If the answer is "it's a smaller version of something else," it has no purpose.

**Result: 6 built focuses fail, and 9 of my proposed additions fail.**

---

## 1. The failure I made, and what it revealed

**Gleam** — my proposal for Isolde's diary rune — was *"constant illumination like Crystal but weaker and cheaper in greed."* Aimee: **why would anyone choose it over Sun?**

They wouldn't. **Sun is +55 and, since the greed fix, costs about −2.** There is no "cheap light" niche because light is already cheap. I invented a gap that the greed fix had closed and never re-checked.

**The general lesson: a focus needs a reason that isn't "a smaller number."**

---

## 2. Illumination — the crowded subject

Fourteen focuses touch it. Ranked by what they actually *do*:

| Focus | Distinct? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun** | ✅ | Strong cyclic light **and heat**. The default |
| **Moon** | ✅ | The only thing lifting the **night floor** while cyclic |
| **Crystal** | ✅ | **Constant** light — no day/night at all — plus substrate value |
| **Fungus** | ✅ | Constant light **that also feeds a lightless world** |
| **Magma** | ✅ | Constant light with **enormous heat** |
| **Void** | ✅ | The only thing that **removes** sky |
| **Canopy** | ✅ | Occludes **and** adds life and cover |
| **Cloud** | ✅ | Occludes **and** retains heat |
| **Ash** | ✅ | Occludes **and** poisons ground |
| **Miasma** | ✅ | Occludes **and** kills life |
| **Smoke** | ⚠️ **weak** | Occludes, adds a little atmosphere. **Ash does this and more** |
| **Stars** | ⚠️ **weak** | +4 cyclic. Below noise. What is it *for*? |
| **Aurora** | ✅ **(Aimee)** | Kept as built — cyclic, no heat |
| **Comet** | ❌ **fails as a light** | +15 cyclic, erratic. A slightly odd Moon — **becomes Meteor, a hazard** (below) |

### Fixes (Aimee)

**Aurora stays as it is.** Cyclic, no heat, no change.

**Comet becomes Meteor, and it's a hazard, not a light.** Its purpose is *impact*: falling rock that damages, cracks ground, and **leaves material behind**. That makes it the only sky focus that changes the ground, and it belongs with hazard sites rather than in the light list. Its glow was never the point.

**Ring becomes a light source** — and it fills the one genuine gap in the subject.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | A band across the sky, always there, catching light the world itself isn't getting |
| **Does** | **Lifts the night floor substantially.** A ringed world is never fully dark |
| **Why it has purpose** | Moon lifts the floor by **+4** — barely. **Nothing else meaningfully lights a night.** Crystal and Fungus light the ground; a Ring lights the *sky* |
| **Distinct from Moon** | Moon is small, cyclic and weak. A Ring is enormous, permanent, and turns night into dusk |

*I had cut Ring as decoration. It wasn't decoration — it had no job, and now it has the best one available.*

**Stars — keep as the cheapest possible "there is a sky at all."** Its purpose is to be nearly free, a marker that a world isn't void, not to light anything.

**Smoke — make it the occluder that also *harms***, distinct from Ash's ground-poisoning: it should hurt to breathe, tying it to Atmosphere rather than duplicating Ash.

## 3. Isolde's diary rune — **Stillness**, not a light word

**Her original clue was:** *"The air is thin and terribly still. Nothing here disturbs a page."*

**A calligrapher's real need isn't light. It's air that doesn't move.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rune** | **Stillness** — Atmosphere |
| **What it does** | **Lowers atmospheric motion.** The only focus that does |
| **Why it's hers** | *Nothing here disturbs a page.* It was in her own writing from the start |
| **Why it has purpose** | It's a **capability rune** — nothing else can still the air. Not a weaker version of anything |

**This is a much better fit than any light word**, and it fixes a second thing: `sigil-vocabulary.md` proposed Stillness for Atmosphere *and* a Cycle *Stillness* meaning "nothing turns." **The Cycle one should be renamed — [PROPOSAL] *Hush*, or *Frozen Hour*.**

| Focus | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| **Gleam** | A weaker Crystal. Cut, replaced by Stillness (§3) |
| **Frost** | "Cold that isn't Ice or Snow." **Ice already is that** |
| **Dune** | Sand already flattens and shifts land. Same word twice |
| **Basalt** | *"Dark stone"* — how does it play differently from Granite? |
| **Chalk** | A softer Limestone. Colour is not a purpose |
| **Cinder** | A third burnt thing after Ash and Smoke |

## 5. Scarp — replace it

**A scarp is a cliff face formed by erosion or faulting.** Real word, genuinely obscure, and vocabulary the player writes with should be legible on sight.

**Superseded 9 Aug 2026:** Mara uses diary-exclusive **Scarp**, the word already present in the
vocabulary, rather than adding the near-synonym Bluff. See `traveller-identities-existing-six-current.md`.

**Worth a general rule:** *if a focus's name needs explaining, it's the wrong name.* The vocabulary is meant to be readable, and half of reading it is knowing what the words mean.

## 6. On backfilling — the principle I broke

> *"we do not need to come up with things to spend made up resources on. we can recalibrate resources that make sense for what we're actively choosing to create. do not backfill to make things make sense."*

**I invented Reed the papermaker to give Pulp a purpose.** That's backfilling: a resource existed with no use, so I built a person and a building and a mechanic around it rather than asking whether the resource should exist.

**Consequences:**
- **The Paper Mill and page quality are cut.** Page quality was separately bad — a second randomness axis on a page that already rolls what you don't write.
- **Pulp and paper may not need to exist at all**, since essence already binds books. **[AIMEE]** floated keeping paper for **anchor books** specifically, which is a real use for a real thing — that's the direction, if any.
- **The sixteenth trade companion is an open slot again.** It should be filled by *a building the game needs*, not by a resource looking for a home.

**The rule going forward: content follows need. Never the reverse.**
