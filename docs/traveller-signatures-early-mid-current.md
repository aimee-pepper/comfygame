# Current Design — Early–Mid Traveller Signatures

**Status:** authored-direction profiles with playtest thresholds. These signatures are deliberately
writable from vocabulary available before each traveller and never require the traveller's own
teaching.

This document now covers three early–mid signatures plus Vance's reordered opening signature. Bryn,
Orsa and Talin retain three conditions/three location pages. Vance has one opening condition; his
former two additional conditions survive as diary world observations, not recruitment gates. Exact
match rates must be measured before thresholds become balance-final.

## Bryn — sheltered weight

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relief openness | ≤ 40 | “The land closes around every path, leaving only narrow approaches to watch.” | Granite/other enclosing relief |
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 35 | “Even loose pieces strike like blocks; there is little here that yields underfoot.” | Granite and Iron are starter vocabulary |
| Atmosphere peak | ≥ 62 | “The air has weight enough to press at the ears before the path begins to climb.” | Cloud is starter vocabulary |

**Identity:** a naturally defensible world with narrow approaches, dependable footing and heavy air.
It expresses protection through position rather than danger or ownership.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Bryn's `Ally: rank = back` teaching.

## Orsa — a usable shelter

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Vitality peak | ≥ 62 | “There is enough living here that every cleared place is used again by morning.” | Bloom/Herd are starter vocabulary |
| Relief openness | ≤ 45 | “The ground makes rooms of itself: close enough for shelter, with more than one way out.” | Granite and other enclosing relief |
| Atmosphere motion | ≤ 20 | “Smoke climbs without being torn sideways, and loose cloth stays where it is put.” | Hush is learned from earlier Isolde |

**Identity:** a lively but calm world where temporary shelter can be made without pretending it is
permanent or private by default.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Hive, Orsa's diary focus.

## Vance — an open route (opening order 1)

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relief openness | ≥ 68 | “A load can cross the horizon here without the land inventing a toll.” | Sand/Sea/Wind and other opening relief routes |

**Identity:** an unobstructed route that makes circulation possible without claiming that movement,
price and worth are identical. Concentrated seams and pockets remain important observations in his
diary, but no longer delay the first economy keeper behind a three-condition midgame hunt.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Amber, Vance's diary focus.

## Talin — the visible opening

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 40 | “The ground breaks into plates with edges clean enough to catch the light.” | Granite/Iron are starter vocabulary |
| Relief openness | ≥ 70 | “There is nowhere for an approach to hide; distance announces everything.” | Sand/Sea/Wind are starter vocabulary |
| Illumination peak | ≥ 65 | “At its brightest, every edge separates sharply from the surface behind it.” | Sun is starter vocabulary |

**Identity:** hard, plated ground under open, decisive light. It supports Talin's attention to armour,
gaps and openings without requiring literal armoured creatures to generate.

**Reward dependency:** never requires `Foe: armour > x`, Talin's diary teaching.

## Cross-batch validation

- Bryn and Talin deliberately share hard/open terrain language in opposite spatial configurations:
  Bryn encloses and holds; Talin exposes and commits.
- Orsa's Hush dependency is allowed because Isolde is earlier and required. It also makes authored
  people influence one another without duplicating teachings.
- Vance's one-condition opening match needs fresh accidental-match testing. Do not make it rarer by
  restoring hidden extra conditions, moving Amber into the route or requiring a random drop.
- No passage claims a site, creature, building, resource name or social history that pressure
  conditions cannot guarantee.
