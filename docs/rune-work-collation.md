# Collating the Rune Work — his five words against my eighty-five

**He authored five focuses out of necessity while fixing the greed model** (Q45–Q47). I proposed an expansion of 46 in parallel. This compares them, and one of his findings exposes a blind spot that runs through my whole proposal.

---

## 1. What he did, and why

| Focus | Subject | Why he needed it |
|---|---|---|
| **Chasm** | Substrate **↓30** | *"if no source subtracts then we've been building this wrong from the start"* |
| **Silt** | Substrate **↓18** | Same |
| **Stillness** | Cycle **↓35** | Cycle had no focuses at all |
| **Tide** | Cycle ↑ | Same |
| **Orrery** | Cycle, regularity ↑↑ | Same |

**The finding underneath:** once the floor moved to *ordinary* (Q45), he measured which subjects could be written **downward** — and **Substrate and Cycle could not.** Every focus touching them raised them. So *Sparse Ore* was secretly a **greedy** choice, and *"reliable as a clock"* was a sentence nothing could trigger.

## 2. Where we agree — Cycle

**Three of his five are Cycle focuses, and all three are on my list**, drawn from `cycle-sources-draft.md`:

| | Mine | His | Match |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tide** | proposed | **built** | ✅ |
| **Orrery** | proposed | **built** | ✅ |
| **Stillness** | proposed | **built** | ✅ |
| **Drift** | proposed | not built — *"the obvious fourth"* | pending |
| **Time** *(broad)* | proposed | not built | pending |

**He took exactly the three Aimee called tightest** and left the rest alone, which is the right restraint.

**One collision to resolve:** I proposed **Stillness** as the *Atmosphere* focus — the only thing that stills air — and as **Isolde's diary rune**. **He built Stillness as the *Cycle* focus.** Both are good words for their subject and they can't both have it.

**[PROPOSAL]** His Cycle *Stillness* keeps the name (it's built, and *"nothing turns"* is the stronger claim). **The Atmosphere one becomes *Hush*** — and that's Isolde's rune. *Nothing here disturbs a page* is a hush, not a stillness.

*Note his Cycle Stillness already carries `atmosphere motion −25` as a secondary — so the two ideas are partly fused already, which is fine and probably better than two separate words doing the same thing.*

## 3. Where he found something I missed — **the subtractive blind spot**

**I proposed 13 additions to Substrate. Every single one adds.** Limestone, Clay, Quartz, Silver, Lead, Copper, Mercury, Adamant, Obsidian — materials, all of them, all raising richness.

**Not one lowers it.** I audited the vocabulary for *redundancy* and never asked whether each subject could be written in **both directions**. He did, and it's the more fundamental question — a subject you can only push one way is half a subject, and *"barren"* becomes unwritable.

**His §3 note is the important one and it applies to my expansion too:**

> *"Relief, Hydrology and Atmosphere each have exactly one or two subtractive words. They work, but they're thin — a subject you can only really write upward is the fault we just fixed, in slower motion."*

**My expansion should be re-cut with this in mind.** Additions that only ever *add* are half the work.

## 4. Sparse Ore and the ladder — a good fix worth noting

**Sparse Ore now expands to Silt**, so it means what its name says, and **Common Ore is faint iron rather than moderate**, so the ladder has a genuine middle: **below ordinary, at ordinary, above.**

**This vindicates the compounds reclassification.** `sparse_ore = silt` and `common_ore = iron` are compounds pointing at single focuses — nearly synonyms, which is exactly what a *teaching* compound should be. They're the rung that shows a player what compounds are before the interesting ones arrive.

## 5. Isolde — his fix and mine, reconciled

**He fixed her spawn rate** (Q47): her thresholds were `relief ≥ 18` and `substrate ≥ 30` against *ordinary* values of 35 and 30 — **so both were true of almost any world**, and she stood in **67% of blank books**. Raised to `relief ≥ 55` and `substrate ≥ 55`, she lands at 26%.

**That's a defect fix and it stands.** But note what it doesn't solve: her conditions are still **on subjects the starting vocabulary can only push upward**, so she's found by writing *more of the ordinary* rather than by knowing a word. **My proposal was that her gate should be vocabulary** — and the two are compatible:

**[PROPOSAL] Keep his raised thresholds, and make one condition need a word.** `relief ≥ 55` stays; the second becomes **Hush** — atmosphere motion, which nothing in the starting set can write.

**That gets both properties:** rare by threshold *and* gated by vocabulary, which is what a start-of-mid-game required character should be.

## 6. His three questions

**1. The rest of the Cycle list.** **Drift** is the obvious fourth and I agree. **Time** as the broad word is the other — a subject needs a coarse word before precise ones, and Cycle currently has three precise words and no way to say *"this world has a rhythm"* without specifying which.

**2. Are Chasm and Silt the right words for poor ground?** **Yes.** *Chasm* is absence of ground;
*Silt* is ground with nothing in it. Two genuinely different kinds of poor. **Later correction,
9 Aug 2026:** Mara uses **Scarp**, already present in the vocabulary, rather than adding Bluff as a
near-synonym; see `traveller-identities-existing-six-current.md`.

**3. The thin subjects.** **Agreed, and §3 above is the list.** Atmosphere with one downward word is the worst of them.
