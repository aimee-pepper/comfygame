# Terrain height, reachable high ground and scenery

7 September 2026. **New direct Aimee decision; intended, not delivered in333.** This version changes structural generation and movement together. It supersedes the old instruction to preserve unrestricted cardinal walking in NEW worlds only. Existing saved books/worlds/opening continuations retain their own movement, water policy, sources and creatures. Read with generated-water-surfaces-and-outlets-v1.md and the modular flora/creature contracts. Engineering source review is required before enabling this structural version; independent appearance work continues.

## One level and one movement authority

One elevation level is exactly4 integer quarter units, using the existing dry terrain level and its existing visual scale. It is not one metre, one screen pixel, one water-depth class or a new skill level. Retain the current0–3 whole-level relief range for this first pass; Aimee's decision does not require increasing maximum height.

Freeze a versioned structural support Q for EVERY new-policy tile before ecological/source reservation. Dry Q=4×final structural Tile.elevation. Wet fallback Q preserves the authored structural support before the legacy water paint overwrites Tile.elevation; do not interpret that overwritten zero as the height of an elevated lake. An admitted measured-water bed becomes that wet tile's final support. Water surface and bed remain distinct. New fallback scenery must render at its declared structural support, without claiming measured depth or displaying an unproved waterfall. Never have movement read prepaint height while the visible floor reads overwritten zero. Unknown deep beds remain undisclosed.

For an ordinary cardinal step with both endpoints otherwise permitted, delta=abs(Qdestination−Qorigin):0 is level,1–3 quarters a slope,4 quarters a one-level step,>4 quarters a cliff/refusal. Apply in BOTH directions. A two-level descent is blocked just like ascent; there is no jump, drop shortcut, climbing, damage payment or one-way fall. A0→1→2 path is legal;0→2 directly is not. Existing ground movement costs remain, with no extra height surcharge. Deep water/chasm/blocked sources keep their own restrictions even when delta is small. A water surface is not party footing.

WorldRules.MovementEdge must own from/to support and refusal for manual steps, paths, generation flood fills, previews and source approaches. Destination-only canEnter remains a cell predicate, not proof of an edge. The current manual step and Worldgen private passability-only reachablePoints must use the same new edge policy. No renderer-only cliff rule or diagonal bypass. Keep existing sightline rules and knowledge limits; a path refusal may describe a visible cliff without revealing hidden terrain or sources.

## Generate routes first; preserve cliffs deliberately

Build coherent landforms and an actual entry-connected route network before resources/animals. Reserve entry, portal, protected introduction route and mandatory source approach space under the same movement policy. Reachability is the full cardinal graph from the ACTUAL entry through ordinary permitted tiles and ≤4-quarter edges, not straight-line proximity, an imagined climbing route or every point with the same elevation.

Replace blanket relaxCardinalElevation for the new version with route construction/repair that preserves deliberate cliff faces. Form stepped approaches alongside/between high areas; changing every adjacent difference to≤1 erases the desired cliffs. Keep water/chasm categories and written-world promises under their owners; do not fill a lake or delete promised material to make a staircase. Resolve structural route choices deterministically before appearance or rewards, then finalize water and recompute entry reachability.

**First-pass distribution tuning:** on maps with elevated walkable ground, at least90% of those tiles must be entry-reachable. Elevated means dry ground whose structural level exceeds the map's lowest dry structural level, not necessarily above the entry. Count terrain before blocking content placement; mandatory routes must survive the final content pass. Ordinary generation may reserve at most ONE disconnected elevated scenery district per map, at25% initial chance and no more than10% of elevated dry tiles. Zero scenery districts is valid; no minimum inaccessible quota. A district is a cardinal connected elevated dry area plus its local contained water/vegetation. Reject a scenery selection that exceeds the cap or intersects entry/portal/mandatory reservations; do not divide it into artificial districts. Other disconnected high patches must receive a valid stepped route or be removed from the proposed terrain layout before admission. These numbers are revisable Design tuning, not Aimee-supplied values.

The generator must satisfy actual required sources and routes before a world is committed/charged, using its existing preparation/refusal owner if it cannot. No new paid retry, resource deletion, already-admitted world reroll or forced expedition reset. A reachability annotation is internal generation data, not a map reveal or promise the player can afford to explore every tile.

## Scenery, vegetation and aquatic animals

Freeze a scenery exclusion mask from final terrain/water and entry reachability BEFORE source and animal placement. Inaccessible high dry cells belong to scenery even if their surface would otherwise be passable. For a local lake/channel reach, distinguish access to its shore from party entry into deep water:

- A measured local water region is shore-accessible when it borders an entry-reachable ordinary bank/shallow approach, the bank/approach is not separated by a blocking ridge, and its support is within4 quarters of that region's surface. This is a fauna placement classification only; it grants no party swimming or remote encounter.
- For unmeasured categorical water use its saved structural shoreline/support adjacency in place of a guessed surface; require the entry-reachable approach and ≤4-quarter structural difference. Do not use legacy overwritten wet zero.
- Actual same-level connected water regions may share that shore-accessible status. A waterfall or higher/lower connection NEVER propagates accessibility upstream or into an isolated high lake. Classify each height separately. Frozen passable water follows its ordinary ground access.
- Deep water in a shore-accessible lake retains its lawful aquatic fauna habitat, including locations outside party reach. An isolated elevated lake with no such shore access has NO animals. Merely having a lower reachable waterfall receiver does not make its upper lake accessible.

All animals, including aerial creatures, ordinary cast placements, hostile flora actors and Apex, must spawn and remain outside the scenery mask. Existing creature habitat/capability gates still apply. Ground/shore walkers use compatible height steps; lawful flight or swimming does not grant permission to occupy excluded scenery. Do not introduce a new ability to swim up waterfalls. Ordinary lowland remote aquatic habitat is not blanket-banned by a party canEnter test; this is specifically high-scenery exclusion plus existing habitat rules. Use the mask in later wandering/placement too, not just initial spawn. No actor may be hidden on a scenic peak and later pulled into an encounter.

Scenery may show trees, foliage and water. Its vegetation must be explicitly decorative/non-source: no resource/source ID, harvest plan, timber or medicine producer link, stock/yield, collection highlight, resource map entry or mandatory-source credit. Existing Flora kind identity/assembly may supply appearance and legitimate visual knowledge, but cannot automatically turn the tile into a gatherable plant. A visible tree shape alone does not grant Logs. This is distinct from the approved no-eligible-timber-kind fallback on ACCESSIBLE ground, which remains an actual obtainable source with its old tree presentation. Never convert a promised/earned source into decoration.

## Exact resources and final admission

Place actual nodes only in accessible terrain districts and respect one source per tile. A blocking node's root need not be a standable tile, but must connect to the entry component when that node ALONE is treated as clear; other blocking content stays in effect. At least one actual legal work position must be in final entry reachability, satisfy the existing tool/target owner and have a support difference≤4 quarters to the source root. Use existing action reach/adjacency, not a new diagonal or ranged harvest permission. A reachable low tile next to a two-level-high tree is not a legal approach. Preserve required tool/source ordering and yields; do not assume an unavailable tool clears the approach.

After all blocking sources/content are placed, revalidate mandatory entry/portal/lesson/source positions and the scenery/actor restrictions. Reserved approaches cannot be occupied by a later node. A scenic Flora object must not leak into resource passes. Resolve conflicts at placement before admission, never by losing obtainable guarantees or counting scenery as inventory. Source placement must not sever the only stepped access to a required plateau.

## Water-policy integration and bounded acceptance

Retain the existing canonical water regions, containment, receivers, depths and positive-drop tests. Before candidate beds, baseline edges already obey the NEW structural≤4-quarter rule. Measured-water admission preserves those baseline legal pairs; it is not required to preserve formerly unrestricted pre-version walking. A candidate mismatch still rejects only that liquid component's measured presentation. Its fallback retains structural support and the new cliff restriction. Geometry cannot revive a forbidden edge, erase a required route or treat flow as stairs. Recompute the actual final entry set before habitat/source reservations.

Existing focused tests need only direct cases:0→1→2 versus0↔2; a preserved tall cliff with a route around it; scenery quota zero/small-map bounds; inaccessible decorated plateau without nodes/animals; inaccessible upper lake connected by a fall to a reachable receiver; accessible shoreline retaining deep-water fauna; source root/work-position and late blocking-node route protection; measured-water rejection retaining the structural cliff; old-world nil-version unchanged. Engineering uses its existing fixed-target native consumer for the resulting route; no new trial, world-search corpus or Design native rerun. The previous333 controlled waterfall remains water evidence, not proof of this new terrain policy.
