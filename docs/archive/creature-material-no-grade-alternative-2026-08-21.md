# Rejected creature-material no-grade alternative — historical record

**Disposition:** rejected when the hybrid material-family + six-band quality model was settled.
**Date archived:** 21 August 2026
**Current authority:** `../creature-ecology-and-materials-overhaul-current.md` and
`../crafting-components-and-schematics-current.md`

## What this alternative proposed

This branch would have removed material grade entirely and retained one exact property-bearing unit for
every harvested part. Recipes would have selected exact units by property floors and least surplus; maker
state would have imposed output tier; the Trading Post would have valued two family-specific properties;
Distillery potency would have used exact property formulas; Scent Mask would have accepted certain tissue
families; and Recycler would have returned only exact construction-receipt parts.

It correctly identified several facts that survived the decision:

- Creature materials and World resources must be separate, slot-free domains.
- Material family/provenance must remain truthful.
- An ingredient cannot satisfy a physically incompatible socket because another number is high.
- Recycler cannot invent a creature source for catalogue gear.
- Migration must preserve every committed holding and crafted item's effective result.

## Why it was rejected

The property-only model required players and every live consumer to compare six continuous bars, while the
rest of the loot game was moving to an intuitive Grey/White/Green/Blue/Purple/Orange ladder. It also made
stacking difficult, made repeated loot unnecessarily bespoke, and would have replaced one confusing grade
with several hidden synthetic scoring rules.

The settled model keeps physical family eligibility and named material effects, but freezes one six-band
quality for stack/value/output weighting. Schematics own sockets; material family owns what it can do;
quality owns how strong that valid component is; no station imposes a hard quality cap. The exact current
tables and 70/30 output formula live in the current authorities linked above.
