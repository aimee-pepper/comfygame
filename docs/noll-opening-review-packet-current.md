## Field Separation Kit disposition

The kit is not part of opening Recycler correctness. Its review question is narrower:

> Is converting one carried unwanted gear piece into exact outputs during a world worth one packed
> item slot, one turn and a confirmation interaction?

Current working answer remains **possibly**, with these safeguards:

- one ordinary eligible piece only;
- one exact preview and atomic commit;
- fixed Recycler tier-1 recovery, never best available tier;
- consumes the kit and one world turn only on success;
- unique/apex/narrative/Channelworks gear excluded;
- no scrap currency or invented provenance.

Do not implement a portable Recycler browser merely to justify the page. If phone play shows the
decision is always “use immediately on the first unwanted object” or “never pack,” replace the diary
reward later and preserve the rejected kit in history. Until the reward is live, its page remains
review-only and cannot enter ordinary recovery.

## Economy gate before promotion

Noll's station should relieve inventory pressure, not compete with continuation. Test a fresh
campaign from the same save boundary with:

1. one first world and Vance's 10-Essence Trading Post built;
2. enough remaining/next-world Essence to bind again normally;
3. Noll found through the authored clue route;
4. 15 Essence paid for the Recycler;
5. at least one ordinary found gear piece previewed and recycled;
6. another world bound without Spring emergency aid being the expected route.

Record generated/collected Raw Essence, both station costs, bind costs and remaining runway. If the
sequence fails only because 15 is too high, compare 10 and 5 before changing drop rates again. The
station price is reversible; the separation of gold sale versus world-resource recovery is not.
