# Current Design — Five-Condition Diary Packets

**Status:** Authoritative authored packets; thresholds inherit
`traveller-signatures-five-condition-current.md`. Reward and relationship pages enter the live catalog
only when their singular reward/target IDs validate.

These books sit at the transition from midgame literacy to specialist play. Each location page names
one condition only. Later pages complicate the traveller's craft instead of advertising a better shop.

## Implementation and forward-reference order

- Bracken can land after Corrin; defer no page if Bracken and current earlier IDs validate together.
- Fen may land with `rook`; defer `oda` until Oda's traveller ID exists.
- Wren may land with Sela and Dagg; defer Marrick until his ID exists.
- Kestrel may land with Corrin and Fen; defer Sabine until her ID exists.
- Maud may land with Halloway, Bracken and Dagg. `maud_fitting_pattern` is a singular authored recipe
  teaching, not a world focus and not an equipment-state feature.
- Relationship pages are always story redundancy. They never gate finding the named traveller.

## Validation rules

1. Location conditions reproduce the signature thresholds exactly and each page evaluates one condition.
2. Gambit, focus and pattern pages grant exactly one known ID.
3. Unknown targets/rewards fail authoring validation and remain deferred; they never silently no-op.
4. Completion rewards remain separate from recruitment and are outside these packets.
