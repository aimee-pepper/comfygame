# Orsa — Keeper

## Identity

| Field | Current design |
|---|---|
| **Pronouns** | she/her |
| **Calling** | a keeper of temporary shelter and negotiated company |
| **Building** | The Firepit upgrades into **The Tavern** |
| **Campaign phase** | Early–mid |
| **Signature complexity** | 3 conditions |
| **Teaching** | **Hive**, diary-exclusive |
| **Starting lean** | Ingenuity + Fortitude, with social/field utility |

### One-line blurb

> Makes a room where strangers can stay without pretending they already trust one another.

### Voice anchors

- **Habit:** Arranges seats, light, exits and shared surfaces before food. She notices who needs to see
  the door, who wants company without conversation, and which questions should not be asked publicly.
- **Attitude:** Warm but not indiscriminately intimate. Orsa treats welcome as a practical arrangement
  people continually consent to, not a performance of instant family.
- **Tension:** A keeper learns a great deal because people lower their guard nearby. Using that
  knowledge can help someone find what they need; it can also turn hospitality into surveillance,
  leverage or an unspoken debt.

Orsa is not the maternal caretaker of the cast. Her expertise is coexistence among people who have
different needs, incomplete trust and reasons to leave again.

## Campaign staging — no premature Tavern subsystem

Recruiting Orsa in Band 3 does not silently unlock a miniature Tavern, visitor roll, relationship
currency or generic clue bonus. Her immediate honest payoff is Orsa herself as a party/Home person,
her authored combat lean, her diary route and eventual Hive teaching. The existing Firepit may
acknowledge that she is using it, but flavour presentation cannot claim visitors, rumours, wants or
improved rest before those transactions exist.

The Firepit → Tavern upgrade is one later, complete social-loop checkpoint. It lands only when at
least the first visitor, one authored want, one permission-safe rumour/clue exchange and the rest
presentation work together. Until then **Tavern not yet built** is more coherent than granting Orsa
an unrelated numerical perk merely so every traveller owns a room immediately.

## Why the player seeks her

The Tavern makes the starting Firepit into the social centre of the search loop:

- visitors and travelling companions appear there;
- rumours and whereabouts clues accumulate there;
- wants can be learned, discussed and satisfied;
- resting gains a stable social fiction and later upgrades;
- cast relationships become visible without requiring everyone to be permanently posted together.

The Tavern should organize existing visitors, wants, clues and rest rather than introduce a separate
relationship currency. Hospitality changes access and information through authored interactions and
building progression, not a generic affection meter.

### Coherence sentence

> Because Orsa knows how to make **temporary shelter usable by people who do not yet trust one
> another**, recruiting her turns the Firepit into a **Tavern where visitors, clues, wants and rest
> become part of one social loop**.

## Hive

Hive does not mean that togetherness is naturally harmonious. It means many lives shaping one place
through repeated routes, labour, exchange, accumulation and defence. Orsa's teaching page should
notice the negotiated traffic and maintenance that let density persist, not praise everyone for
working toward one mind.

Hive is Orsa's diary-exclusive focus. Its former ordinary acquisition route is superseded.

## Signature concept

Orsa's three conditions should create a world where different lives repeatedly converge without
becoming identical:

- high enough life or resource density to sustain traffic;
- a traversable bottleneck, shelter or meeting terrain;
- a cycle, atmosphere or hazard pattern that makes temporary congregation useful.

The signature should describe a meeting place, not simply “a cozy world.” The world may be difficult;
what matters is that several routes can share one pause.

## Diary shape — 7 pages

| Pages | Purpose |
|---:|---|
| 3 | One convergence, shelter or route page per signature condition |
| 1 | Hive teaching |
| 1 | Sela or Vance relationship/whereabouts page |
| 1 | A confidence Orsa used helpfully but without permission |
| 1 | Firepit precursor, abandoned shelter or Orsa whereabouts page |

### Diary arc

- **First impression:** Orsa seems able to make almost anyone feel known and accommodated.
- **Complication:** She once passed on private information because it solved an urgent problem, then
  treated the good outcome as proof that the breach was justified.
- **After recruitment:** She keeps connecting people and clues but becomes explicit about what may be
  repeated, to whom and for what purpose.

## Meeting-scene direction

Orsa has made a temporary shelter functional for several travellers who do not like or trust one
another. Nobody is fighting, but one person has moved their seat outside the light because Orsa
solved a dispute by revealing something they told her privately. The player meets her while she is
deciding whether a peaceful room is enough evidence that she handled it well.

### Offer

> The fire at your base already gathers people. I can make the gathering useful—and make sure useful
> does not become the only reason anyone is welcome.

## Relationship anchors

- **Vance:** Trading Post sale as a completed price versus hospitality as an ongoing relationship; both can
  use private provenance as leverage.
- **Sela:** Sela brings news from routes; Orsa decides how and whether news becomes communal knowledge.
- **Nessa:** Informal conversation reveals lived effects and needs that controlled preparation misses.
- **Nine:** A witnessed promise can preserve continuity, but the witness gains power over how it is
  remembered.

## Complexity check

- The Tavern upgrades an existing Firepit rather than adding another base door.
- Visitors, clues, wants and resting are already planned functions.
- No relationship meter, social-stat economy or hospitality minigame is added.
- Hive already exists in the vocabulary; only its acquisition route changes.
