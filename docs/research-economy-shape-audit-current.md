# Research Economy Shape Audit — Current

Status: **Design audit; no live prices changed**  
Date: 11 August 2026

## Question

Does the Penmanship runway problem indicate that the whole research economy is inflated?

## Conclusion

No blanket rescale is justified. Penmanship is the immediate structural outlier because a short chain
of expensive purchases gates the game's core expressive activity. Other expensive branches generally
buy optional breadth, prediction, or deliberately long-term capacity in smaller increments.

This does not certify every other placeholder price as balanced. It means they need telemetry rather
than an indiscriminate percentage reduction.

## Evidence from the current catalogue

- Penmanship's five legacy nodes total 1,380 Essence. Its 150-Essence first hand follows a
  60-Essence Scriptorium and gates every colored-ink and compact-writing practice.
- Instruments range from 30–150 Essence across eight independent subject tools. A player can gain
  one new capability without completing the whole branch.
- Lens upgrades range from 70–260 Essence across four progressively stronger reading capabilities.
- Hold begins at 15–45 Essence and grows into intentionally optional, very-late capacity purchases;
  its 550/890/1,440-Essence shelves are not prerequisites for writing another world.

## Cost roles

Research prices should be judged by what a purchase does to continuation, not by a single global
curve.

1. **Core expressive unlocks** — writing hands, basic ink access, and other mechanics required to
   engage the game's central authorship loop. Price against recent *net continuation runway* after
   ordinary binding, and never assume an emergency anti-lock subsidy as income.
2. **Optional capability unlocks** — instruments, lenses, specialist recipes, and tactical breadth.
   These may be multi-world goals when each purchase adds a usable capability on its own.
3. **Long-horizon capacity** — late Storehouse/satchel expansion and similar hoarding relief. A steep
   or exponential tail is acceptable when visibly optional and never required to continue writing.

## Current disposition

- Keep DRQ-170 as the only immediate price review. Do not freeze the legacy 150-Essence Brush.
- Do not bulk-rescale Hold, Instruments, Lens, or specialist branches from this audit.
- Extend the proposed **Low writing runway** preview to any Essence purchase: compare the remaining
  balance with the recent median ordinary authored bind cost. It is advisory, never a purchase lock.
- Collect purchase timing, skipped-purchase, remaining-Essence, and next-bind data during playtesting.
  Revisit individual optional branches only when that evidence shows they are functionally absent or
  competing destructively with continuation.

## Invariant

The player may choose an ambitious upgrade over immediate safety. The interface must make that
tradeoff legible; the economy must not disguise a mandatory creative unlock as optional luxury.
