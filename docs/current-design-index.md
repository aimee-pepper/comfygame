## Authority order

When two documents disagree:

1. A clearly labelled current decision in the relevant living system document wins.
2. `decisions-session-18.md` wins over earlier session logs for work covered by the current lead pass.
3. `design-review-queue.md` identifies placeholders, reviews and blocks; a placeholder is usable but
   not final.
4. `roster-coherence-audit-current.md` controls cross-roster ownership and distribution.
5. Files under `docs/archive/` are historical only.
6. An older proposal never overrides a newer current document because engineering references it.

## Current handoff discipline

- Normal design work updates Markdown and sends engineering a concise handoff; engineering owns code.
- Engineering may use a labelled placeholder to stay unblocked and reports the assumption to design.
- Asset work remains separate from game code; engineering owns integration.
- Progress summaries cite current documents rather than requiring Aimee to reconstruct chats.
