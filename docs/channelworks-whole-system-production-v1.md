# Channelworks — complete first-pass production contract

**6 September 2026 · Game Design.** Whole-system implementation packet, paired with [Distillery](distillery-whole-system-production-v1.md). Accepted identity is retained; all new bills, output arithmetic and service tuning below are **Design-authored first pass**, not further personal Aimee approvals. Implementation is pending. The current stored Heat Fixture is not yet an equipped combat housing.

## 1. Retained identity and corrected comparison

Oda makes a weapon-slot emanation housing; Auber prepares its Core. There are **three attunements × three reaches**, exactly nine configurations. All use ordinary attacks and existing cooldown actions, with no mana, charges, ammunition, durability or extra damage kind. The attunement persists through attacks, encounters, Return and reopening until explicitly retuned or the item is actually destroyed/lost under normal custody. It never lights the world: combat Light/Dazzle is separate from Lantern/Light Inscription and its unresolved activation contract.

A physical weapon retains full physical output/matchup and may use one of the four Apothecary coatings on that exact weapon for **the whole excursion**. A Channelworks weapon trades raw output for direct emanation that bypasses ordinary armour and is countered by matching Ward/resistance. It cannot accept a coating and supplies only its own weak Burn, Poison or Dazzle; no Bleed, Freeze, Shock or Arc. Neither the old one-strike comparison nor a generic reactive-material Poison fallback is valid. Existing rules that explicitly expand armour to emanation, such as Immovable's supported effect, still apply; ordinary armour bypass is not true damage.

Do not turn an arbitrary physical/Apex weapon into a housing. No fabricated Pierce/Crush/Rend kind, Apex inheritance, physical coating, physical matchup or bare-handed fallback attaches to a housing profile. Existing unsupported physical special/paid-credit items retain their real services and data.

No attending-Oda gate, second restoration bill, paid root Research, new travel prerequisite, Auber recruitment prerequisite or Distillery prerequisite for this first usable weapon. Foundation materials build the shop; do not pretend they are nine refundable components inside that starter. The shop grants the restored weapon to safe Storehouse/Waiting once, without auto-equipping or changing a live excursion's gear. Retain the durable existing restoration entitlement and exact fixture identity.

Oda's existing diary schematic `emanation_housing` opens **Contact and Projection** recipes after her shop is built. It is not a world-writing rune, station prerequisite, second starter requirement or item/Core reward. Keep its learned identity and existing acquisition path. Tier placement was placeholder: the first pass uses this one real schematic gate, with no second paid level or hidden rating gate. Basic Conduits, including Caustic/Light when an actual Core is available, do not require the schematic.

Keep the verbs distinct: **Oda's restored conduit** names the historical one-time entitlement/location; **Build a housing** makes a separately paid new item; **Retune** replaces an existing item's Core. Moving, equipping, trading, losing or dismantling the starter never resets the entitlement. For an exact surviving current stored fixture, the new weapon adapter preserves that exact instance ID and Core; it does not grant another item. An existing receipt whose item is gone stays fulfilled. A receipt with unknown historical identity stays unknown. Older ambiguous missing-receipt saves retain their current supported migration behavior; this batch must not add a new grant-on-every-load rule or infer unclaimed entitlement from an empty Storehouse. Only a proven unused construction entitlement may grant the authored starter, atomically once.

## 4. Complete first-pass combat arithmetic

Let **P** be this exact Core's saved valid potency in0…100. New Distillery Cores are60; Oda's starter is40. The housing's weapon-power contribution is **1 + P/40**, rounded to the normal quarter-point boundary half-up when necessary. Thus starter40 supplies2; new60 supplies2.5; legacy80 supplies3. This replaces any catalogue placeholder weapon power, not an addition on top of it. Casing, Quartz and binding add no second physical power.

Use the existing actor's pre-matchup attack calculation with this power once, normal weapon-power conversion, and nil physical kind. Keep the existing nil-kind character-stat rule (better of Might/Finesse); do not impersonate Pierce to get Finesse or add both character bonuses. Use existing unconditional/emanation-eligible actor bonuses once; physical-kind-only nodes and Apex/weapon-specific effects do not become universal. Snapshot the actual housing/Core in the normal encounter gear owner. Roll the normal attack once, then apply the family factor to that rolled pre-defence power:

| Family | Raw emanation factor | Burn damage per boundary | Poison damage per boundary | Existing durations |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Contact | .80 | max(1, round-half-up(P × .80 /30)) | max(1, round-half-up(P × .80 /60)) | Burn2; Poison4; Dazzle2 |
| Conduit | .70 | max(1, round-half-up(P × .70 /30)) | max(1, round-half-up(P × .70 /60)) | Burn2; Poison4; Dazzle2 |
| Projection | .60 | max(1, round-half-up(P × .60 /30)) | max(1, round-half-up(P × .60 /60)) | Burn2; Poison4; Dazzle2 |

Heat applies only Burn; Caustic only Poison; Light only Dazzle (no invented Light damage-over-time or second strength-based miss probability). At P60 the three Heat strengths are **2/1/1**, all Caustic strengths are **1**, and all Light applications use the existing two-boundary Dazzle. At P40 all Heat/Caustic strengths are1. These replace the old .22/.18/.14 × potency prototype status coefficients, whose large damage was not appropriate for a weak persistent affliction. Potency bands label the exact saved number; no housing-tier cap silently wastes a better old Core.

Send the resulting unrounded direct power through the existing **`Harm.emanation`** path for exactly Heat/Caustic/Light. Matching Ward, worn Heat insulation/Heat Ward, chosen attunement resistance and explicit emanation-armour exceptions use their shared owner once. Keep that owner's existing final rounding/minimum-damage policy (including the existing matching-resistance floor); do not force a new universal rounding mode or physical back-rank damage penalty onto emanation. Physical covering/matchup and ordinary armour alone cannot change the result. Preserve existing reach, rank legality, interception/Ground, accuracy/Dazzle, retaliation, critical eligibility and saved RNG behavior. Route consequences to the actual recipient after redirection, not the originally selected target. Existing typed blocking/prevention rules remain authoritative.

An ordinary legal landed direct housing hit proposes one corresponding status application on its surviving actual recipient. A miss, rejected target, mere equip, Aim or inspection applies none. Shared prevention, resilience, same-kind stronger/refresh behavior, cure, tick timing and death resolve that application. Ward on the direct hit is not an invented second multiplicative reduction to each later status tick; existing status defenses own those ticks. A carried, reflected, retaliation or incidental packet does not create a second Core application unless an existing explicitly typed action owns that effect. No future multi-target/chain release is added.

Existing actions remain governed by their actual eligibility: kind-specific physical techniques require a matching physical weapon; independently authored spells/actions retain their own harm; an action that explicitly performs an ordinary weapon strike uses this housing route. Do not convert every cooldown skill into emanation by equipped-item name. Existing Snuff removes its intended active emanation effect, not the saved Core, and never consumes a weapon. Coating selection rejects a housing before spending anything. Light has no field illumination, terrain, fog or Lantern activation side effect.

Arithmetic example with an already-rolled pre-defence power10 and no critical/resistance: raw Contact8, Conduit7, Projection6. Existing matching Insulation at.65 gives the shared floor-once results5/4/3. Increasing ordinary physical armour alone leaves those values unchanged. Integer rounding can make two lower-power profiles tie; the unrounded factors remain ordered. These examples are not promises of every actor's damage or a substitute for the shared real combat calculation.

## 5. Retuning, rebuilding and recovery

All ordinary Oda services occur at Home, cost **0 service Essence**, and need only the built shop plus the target family's actual schematic gate. No repair timer, random improvement, durability or generic Reforge rank.

- **Retune:** one selected supported housing + one selected valid Core of any of the three attunements. Replace its Core and recalculate power/status/nominal new-policy value; keep exact item ID, family/reach, active hardware, custody and unrelated lawful saved properties. The previous Core is destroyed in containment work and is not returned. Same-attunement replacement is allowed when potency or actual selected Core provenance changes; identical no-op selection is not charged. Preview the exact before/after and irreversible old-Core loss. No missing hardware or fixture bill is retroactively added.
- **Rebuild/change reach:** one selected supported housing + the complete structural bill of its chosen target family, with its **existing Core retained once**. Contact/Projection require the existing schematic. Return old active hardware once; explicitly reuse an eligible existing component in the quote to net its incoming/return allocation once. Show changed reach, damage, status, appearance and value. Same family with changed materials is a supported rebuild for appearance. Same construction is a free no-op. This is Oda's specific housing service, not arbitrary conversion of physical weapons.
- **Dismantle:** the existing Recycler owner destroys one eligible unequipped housing and returns its recorded active physical casing/Haft/Quartz/Cord components once. The installed Core is non-recoverable; explicitly preview its loss and no Essence/catalyst refund. Prepared Hafts/Cord/Ingots return as prepared units, never their Logs/Fibre/ore/fuel as well. History is not inventory. Unsupported older items keep their explicit lawful legacy salvage until an actual adapter proves exact ownership. No generic partial recovery layered onto the new complete hardware recovery.

A valid older stored fixture made by the current Core-only conversion has a real paid Core but no fabricated bill for new hardware. Adopt it as the same Mid housing with that Core/potency and an empty old hardware receipt, preserving its frozen old value. Retuning can replace its Core without inventing earlier hardware; a later explicit rebuild pays the actual new hardware bill and previews the new-policy value. Oda's starter similarly has no recoverable foundation materials or free Core. A missing/malformed Core receipt cannot be repaired by catalogue-name inference: keep the owned item safe and explain the unsupported conversion, without minting a valid new Core or deleting paid stock.

For newly constructed housing, nominal sale value **C = the selected Core's frozen nominal sale value + the sum of active structural components' frozen nominal sale values**; buy2C. Do not count Essence twice, nested raw ancestors, the old destroyed Core or service history. Retuning/rebuilding an already new-policy housing updates this exact active composition. Existing item values remain preserved until an explicitly quoted supported service changes their active composition. Sale/dismantle are choices with different receipts; selling never also returns components.

Peerless, future containment choices (raw output versus affliction emphasis), advanced infusion and final family specials are outside this complete ordinary batch. They have no paid or misleadingly usable button. No new request to Aimee is needed; they await an actual bounded later design/play result and cannot block the nine ordinary configurations. Existing special ownership, if encountered, must be preserved rather than silently reset by a generic adapter.
