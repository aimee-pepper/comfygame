# Current Design — Midgame Four-Condition Traveller Signatures

**Status:** authored-direction profiles with playtest thresholds. This batch covers Nessa, Corrin,
Dagg, Rook and Lys. Every condition has a deliberate pre-recruitment route and must receive one
location page.

## Nessa — dangerous ingredients, usable conditions

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Atmosphere tag | `toxic` present | “The first breath leaves a taste behind long after the air itself is gone.” | Miasma research |
| Vitality peak | ≥ 58 | “Enough survives here that poison is a relationship, not an empty-world warning.” | Great Bloom or Bloom + Root |
| Hydrology available | ≥ 35 | “There is water to work with, though nothing about it promises it is safe.” | Lake, Rain or Sea are starter vocabulary |
| Substrate volatile form | ≥ 35 | “The exposed ground changes colour where moisture touches it.” | Sulfur or Crystal research |

**Identity:** a biologically active world where harmful substances coexist with carriers, remedies
and living responses. It teaches dose and context without making toxicity a new player meter.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Thorn, Nessa's diary focus.

## Corrin — repeated movement through living material

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Vitality produced | ≥ 40 | “Growth replaces what is stripped away quickly enough to leave fresh edges beside old ones.” | Root + Bloom are starter vocabulary |
| Vitality trophic depth | ≥ 45 | “Every patch of growth shows signs that something else has fed there.” | Herd over producer-supported vitality |
| Atmosphere motion | ≥ 60 | “Nothing broad stays flat for long; every loose surface bends and returns.” | Wind is starter vocabulary |
| Hydrology dispersion | ≥ 70 | “Water reaches the whole route in films, drops and shallow crossings.” | Rain is starter vocabulary |

**Identity:** productive, wet and moving conditions that make flexibility and repeated contact
legible without promising a particular creature, hide or harvested material.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Chitin, Corrin's diary focus.

## Dagg — commitment followed by release

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 50 | “The ground answers a misplaced step instead of forgiving it.” | Granite is starter vocabulary |
| Relief verticality | ≥ 32 | “Every ascent commits the body before the next landing can be seen.” | Great Granite or Chasm |
| Cycle amplitude | ≥ 65 | “Long quiet intervals end in changes large enough to demand preparation.” | Tide research |
| Atmosphere motion | ≥ 58 | “The air arrives in force, spends itself, and leaves a space to move afterward.” | Wind is starter vocabulary |

**Identity:** hard footing and strong cycles create visible preparation, commitment and recovery.
The world does not guarantee slow creatures or a combat tutorial.

**Reward dependency:** never requires `Self: recovery complete`, Dagg's diary teaching.

## Rook — an approach that can be read

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relief openness | ≥ 78 | “An approach begins at the horizon; nothing nearby can pretend it started later.” | Sea or Sand are starter vocabulary |
| Relief verticality | ≤ 15 | “The ground offers no rise high enough to erase what is coming.” | Flat baseline plus Sea/Sand |
| Hydrology standing form | ≥ 60 | “The water holds to one broad body and makes every crossing a chosen line.” | Sea or Lake are starter vocabulary |
| Atmosphere motion | ≤ 20 | “Grass tips and loose threads remain still enough to show the smallest disturbance.” | Earlier Hush |

**Identity:** a flat, open boundary where distance, crossings and warnings are readable. Standing water
funnels approach without claiming that every map will contain one bridge or choke point.

**Reward dependency:** never requires `Foe: cannot reach me`, Rook's diary teaching.

## Lys — records that persist in relation

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relief tag | `ruined` present | “The ground has been shaped, broken and shaped again; no layer is the first account.” | Earlier Edren's Ruin focus |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 85 | “The same interval returns reliably enough to compare one change with the next.” | Orrery research |
| Illumination floor | ≥ 18 | “The light never leaves completely, even when no bright source remains overhead.” | Crystal research |
| Atmosphere motion | ≤ 20 | “A loose page stays open to the same line until someone chooses to turn it.” | Earlier Hush |

**Identity:** repeated time, persistent low light, still air and worked layers make comparison possible.
Ruin marks altered terrain but never guarantees a discrete ruin site or truthful single account.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Echo, Lys's diary focus.

## Validation notes

- Nessa deliberately combines toxic tags with sufficient life. This is ecological coexistence, not a
  contradiction unless the authored page explicitly negates one source.
- Corrin's `produced` condition counts actual producers rather than herds alone; this prevents a rich
  food-web clue from being satisfied by consumers with nothing beneath them.
- Dagg's amplitude condition describes the size of changes, not irregularity. Drift remains Tovin's
  later teaching.
- Rook uses two Relief measures because openness and flatness communicate different guaranteed facts.
- Lys's location uses Edren and Isolde's earlier teachings, strengthening relationships without
  duplicating ownership.
