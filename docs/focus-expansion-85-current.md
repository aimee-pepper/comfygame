# Focus expansion — 62 to 85

**Status:** current content direction; names, magnitudes and acquisition bands are reversible until
single-focus fixtures pass. The 23-entry scope replaces the older unreconciled proposal to reach 95.

## Selection rule

Add a focus only when it supplies an ordinary concept the player cannot directly request, a missing
directional capability, a goal with a real downstream consumer, or a late identity not expressible
with an existing focus plus modifier.

The cut excludes redundant surface variants and words whose implied mechanics do not exist. Comet
overlaps live Meteor; Cinder overlaps Ash/Wildfire; Chalk overlaps Limestone; Lead has no current
resource/recipe demand; Gale is Great Wind through Intensity; Thunder implies the deliberately absent
shock/electricity system; Moss and Vine overlap existing low-growth/canopy language at this scope.
Glass remains outside this expansion while Quartz, Rift-glass and Tam's held role are reviewed.

World-discovery entries use current rune pity when they are the authored target. None is
merchant-only; the Exchange may later circulate already eligible non-diary vocabulary without
becoming its sole source.

## Required metadata and fixtures

Every addition needs explicit palette group; Count grammar; primary/secondary prose and analysis
labels; one single-focus and one relevant contradiction fixture; reachability/resource/site
distribution comparison; and stable-ID/acquisition validation.

No focus ships merely as a name and numbers. If visible consequences cannot yet be expressed, hold
that entry rather than adding an inert word.

## Scope result

The live 62 plus these 23 produces **85 focuses**. Future additions remain possible, but 85 is now a
coherent milestone rather than an invitation to fill every old proposal row.
