# Full Named-Traveller Roster — Structural Audit

**Status:** First comprehensive pass; recommendations pending Aimee’s review  
**Scope:** The six implemented travellers plus every proposed traveller in `cast-roster.md`  
**Rule:** No individual traveller is finalized until the roster works as a whole

## Why this pass comes before character completion

A traveller sheet can be internally coherent while the cast around it is not. Before finalizing
names, voices, signatures or diary packets, the roster needs to answer four whole-game questions:

1. Does every major progression route belong to the right person?
2. Are there enough different kinds of people, rather than a list made entirely from buildings?
3. Does each exclusive teaching have one owner and a reason to be theirs?
4. Can the authored order sustain a month-plus campaign without a crowded middle or empty endgame?

The existing-six audit remains useful evidence, but its recommendations are held until this pass and
the later character sheets are reviewed together.

The initial core comparison model, authored order, structural sheets, teaching ledger and proposed
relationship graph are in `full-roster-framework-proposal.md`.

## Cross-roster conflicts

### 1. Exclusive teachings are not exclusive yet

| Teaching | Current proposed owners | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Amber** | Vance, Auber, Kestrel | Reassign all but one. None currently has an airtight claim; write each person first, then choose |
| **Echo** | Lys, Ashe | Prefer Lys provisionally because an archive is literally repetition preserved; design a more elemental word for Ashe |
| **Hush / Stillness / Gleam** | Isolde’s documents disagree | Current decision is **Hush** for still air; clean the older labels after the roster pass |
| **Verdigris Bloom / Drift** | Tovin live data vs current roster | Current decision is **Drift**; verify the focus before replacing implementation data |

Every traveller teaching should pass two tests: no ordinary acquisition route gives the same thing,
and the word or technique feels inevitable in that person’s hands.

### 2. Animal ownership — settled

- **Sabine** is the Menagerist and Beast-handler. The Menagerie owns taming, keeping, trait inspection,
  animal commands and fighting as a pair.
- **Kestrel** remains distinct as the Hunter: tracking and butchery are not keeping or handling.
- **Oda** no longer belongs to the animal system and is repurposed as the owner of a ranged-magic-
  weapons shop.

### 3. Making-equipment roles — tiered specialisation

Halloway’s Blacksmith establishes ordinary equipment work. Advanced equipment then separates into
four specialist routes: Bracken’s **Armoury** for armour, a new traveller’s **Weaponsmith** for
physical melee weapons, Fen’s **Bowyer** for physical ranged weapons, and Oda’s **Channelworks** for
all magical weapons. Corrin remains distinct through flexible organic goods, capacity and light
protection.

The intended division is:

- Halloway establishes basic equipment work, transformation and reforging.
- Corrin makes flexible storage and light protection from organic materials.
- Bracken advances armour through higher-tier recipes, upgrades and resistance-oriented protection.
- The Weaponsmith advances non-magical melee weapons through higher-tier recipes and upgrades.
- Fen advances all non-magical ranged weapons through the Bowyer.
- Oda advances magical weapons at every reach through The Channelworks.

**Casting decision:** Fen and the Weaponsmith are separate. **Maud** is the developed Weaponsmith
proposal: fitted melee craft, leverage and chosen proximity distinguish her from Halloway rather
than treating “higher tier” as a personality. See `traveller-identities-equipment-specialists.md`.

### 4. The middle is crowded

The current roster places most trades and fighters at three-to-five conditions. That creates a large
undifferentiated middle rather than an authored sequence. Phase labels are insufficient because pity
and vocabulary acquisition require a specific next person.

**Recommendation:** after roles settle, author one complete order for all travellers. Use signature
conditions, vocabulary, hand upgrades, building dependencies and diary cross-links together. Do not
sort solely by condition count.

### 5. The cast needs relationship architecture

The implemented diaries already hint at a network—Mara names Sela, Sela names Halloway, Tovin names
Edren and Isolde—but most proposed people are isolated job descriptions.

**Recommendation:** every named traveller should have at least two cast connections:

- one person who knew, mentions or misreads them;
- one person they themselves point toward, remember, disagree with or depend on.

This is a target for the whole network, not a forced pair of exposition pages for every person.

## Diary implications

With a high-twenties-or-larger cast at an ordinary 5–10 pages each, plus longer late books, the full
game will contain **well over 200 named diary pages**. That is appropriate for a long campaign, but it changes
placement and authoring needs:

- one writing per world can support the corpus only if the campaign contains many worlds and page
  eligibility does not stall;
- diary weighting must prevent the same few books from monopolising early worlds;
- the one-page/one-condition rule means late books are intentionally longer;
- cross-character pages count in the author’s book, not the subject’s;
- page types need a roster-level distribution so “assortment” is real rather than every diary using
  the same formula.

Do not assign exact page counts yet. First settle roles, order and relationships; then lay out each
book beside the others and check the global mix of location, teaching, site, whereabouts, worthwhile
world, research and character-bearing pages.

## Recommended comprehensive workflow

1. Resolve the roster-wide calls in the review batch below.
2. Correct the roster count and numbering, but do not finalize names yet.
3. Establish the full authored progression order and broad relationship graph.
4. Complete template sheets for **all** travellers at structural level: identity premise, reason to
   recruit, contribution, phase, signature concept, teaching and diary shape.
5. Review that full matrix together.
6. Only then finalize the existing six and draft detailed voices, meetings and page prose in related
   batches.
