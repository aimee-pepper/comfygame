# Current Design — Auber and Ashe Eight-Condition Signatures

**Status:** authored-direction profiles with playtest thresholds. Both are late eight-condition hunts;
neither requires its owner's exclusive reward.

## Auber — separating a thing from what carries it

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hydrology salinity | ≥ 45 | “What dries here leaves enough behind to change the next water poured across it.” | Sea or Salt; never requires Brine |
| Hydrology dispersion | ≤ 30 | “Water gathers into distinct bodies instead of hiding in every surface.” | Sea/Lake |
| Hydrology standing form | ≥ 60 | “Most of what is wet remains long enough for one layer to settle from another.” | Sea/Lake |
| Thermal range | ≥ 45 | “Heat and cold take turns strongly enough to separate what either alone would carry.” | Sun + Ice or other wide-range combination |
| Substrate volatile form | ≥ 50 | “Part of the ground changes readily when heated, wetted or exposed.” | Crystal/Sulfur/Meteor |
| Substrate dispersion | ≤ 35 | “The responsive material keeps to seams that can be worked without taking the whole ground.” | Crystal/Gold ore |
| Atmosphere clarity | ≤ 45 | “Vapour holds in the air and makes every farther edge uncertain.” | Mist or Miasma |
| Cycle amplitude | ≥ 65 | “Each returning interval changes the mixture enough to begin another separation.” | Tide |

**Identity:** concentrated standing saline water, responsive seams, retained vapour and strong repeated
thermal/cycle changes make separation observable. The signature describes processes and carriers,
not a guaranteed still, reagent or crafted output.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Brine, Auber's diary focus.

## Ashe — several emanations felt in one body

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Thermal tag | `geothermal` present | “Heat reaches upward through the ground without waiting for the brightest part of the sky.” | Geyser or Volcano |
| Thermal range | ≥ 45 | “The change between intervals is large enough to be felt in joints before it is read on an instrument.” | Heat source plus Ice/Void |
| Illumination tag | `sourceless` present | “Some light remains where no visible sky-source accounts for it.” | Crystal/Fungus |
| Substrate volatile form | ≥ 50 | “The ground does not merely hold pressure; part of it answers.” | Crystal/Sulfur/Meteor |
| Atmosphere tag | `toxic` present | “A harmful trace persists in the breath after its visible cause has passed.” | Miasma |
| Atmosphere motion | ≥ 55 | “Whatever the air carries reaches the body from more than one direction.” | Wind combined with Miasma |
| Vitality produced | ≥ 35 | “Living growth continues beneath effects that instruments would call hostile.” | Root + Bloom |
| Cycle amplitude | ≥ 65 | “Each interval returns the same forces at a different bodily intensity.” | Tide |

**Identity:** geothermal heat, sourceless light, reactive ground, toxic moving air and persistent life
interact across a strong cycle. It is not “all elements at once,” and it adds no electricity, freeze
or shock taxonomy. The clues remain bodily and observable rather than turning Ashe into an instrument.

**Reward dependency:** never requires `Foe: emanating`; Ground is Ashe's starting technique rather
than a location key.

## Validation notes

- Auber reaches high salinity through pre-existing Sea/Salt, preserving Brine as a reward rather than
  a key.
- Auber's three Hydrology conditions state amount arrangement, form and dissolved content separately.
- Ashe's tags require actual authored phenomena, while numeric conditions ensure those phenomena
  materially shape the world rather than appearing as decorative labels.
- Both profiles need refined-hand deliberate fixtures before thresholds become balance-final.
