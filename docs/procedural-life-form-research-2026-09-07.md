# Procedural life: coherent form within a practical scope

7 September 2026. Bounded Design research requested directly by Aimee through PM. Her clarified ambition is at least halfway towards Spore qualitatively, while remaining well below full Spore complexity. This is not a percentage metric, a parity promise or permission for its editor/simulation scope. Engineering continues the approved release order. The present primitive assemblies establish functional coverage; that receipt alone does not establish the desired visual richness.

## What is already accepted

Bookbinder generates and freezes species/kind structure when writing a world, using six creature layouts and five flora construction families, independent compatible part groups, source-owned colour/Pattern/finish and existing world pressures. Species remain stable, flora has no per-tile biological reroll, resource occupancy/access remains rules-owned, and mesh anatomy cannot invent rewards or movement. Dragon DNA supplies the modular inspiration. No new taxonomy, breeding, editor, food chain or plant lifecycle is proposed here.

## Recommended improvement order and concrete examples

**1. Make the existing anatomy read as a whole.** Existing contract requirement: every declared group is present, attached and within logical/display limits. Recommended refinement: coordinate trunk curvature/taper, support placement and attachment thickness, keeping purposeful gaps between adjacent groups. A six-legged axial animal with vestigial wings, horns and a tail should read as one body with three supporting pairs and separate wings, not a sphere with identical radial rods. A serpentine animal retains its continuous elongated form with wings attached to appropriate body zones. These are possible combinations within accepted anatomy, not new species or guaranteed encounters.

**2. Make variation change shape, not only count or colour.** Recommended refinement: two legal four-legged animals may differ through slender versus broad trunk, limb proportions and rear-body/tail profile without changing source count, stats or rewards. Use bounded correlated profile controls; selecting a profile must not quietly add an organ or overwrite saved anatomy. Avoid adding a new random parameter when existing source build, size or saved proportions can own the effect. New shape choices, if needed, belong to a versioned frozen recipe.

**3. Give flora a repeated structural logic.** A woody form can have a readable trunk, narrowing branches and graded foliage within its chosen crown. Fibrous stalks should visibly carry blades/upper growth; a fleshy rosette should repeat leaves around its actual support. Fungal shelves/caps and chemical tubes/crusts retain their own support and growth grammar rather than inheriting tree branches or flower rules. Leaf taper/curvature and position-based scaling are recommended presentation refinements; botanical studies of ordinary plants do not authorize new fungal biology.

**4. Preserve meaningful pressure and material differences.** Already accepted: habitat eligibility and actual tissue/stature/build/covering come first. A woody-dominant tall kind and a fleshy low kind should visibly express their actual source traits. Cold bias only affects the approved lawful water groups; pressure cannot add invented fur, fins, feeding behavior or growth permissions. Bark/wood and foliage retain separate source colours; harvested portions keep their true assignment. Pattern and finish should reinforce the form rather than conceal missing structure.

## Quality criteria and remaining tradeoffs

These are concise criteria for the already planned fixed-target native acceptance, not a new test matrix or automated evaluator:

- **Whole-form readability:** the displayed creature/plant has a clear overall shape; differences are perceptible at normal viewing scale without depending solely on colour.
- **Structural truth:** correct counts and roles, attached supports, no accidental tissue bridges, misleading flight pose or source-changing geometry. Keep intentional radial/amorphous forms; do not impose bilateral Earth anatomy on all families.
- **Controlled variation:** related parts share a coherent profile while different saved kinds retain recognizable differences. Repetition follows attachment/branch order instead of uniform visual noise.
- **Continuity:** the same saved kind/specimen stays recognizable after movement/reopen and in permitted memory; source-specific materials do not get averaged or recoloured for aesthetic convenience.

Open technical tradeoff: local join/profile refinement versus more general surface meshing. Recommend bounded profiles and local joins first; Engineering and Asset choose against the actual visible defect and existing runtime consumer. No measured performance advantage is claimed. Exact profile curves, branch envelopes and material treatment need implementation-specific choices; they are recommendations, not newly accepted numerical tuning. Full gait retargeting, editor tools, continuous plant growth and global space-colonization simulation remain outside this work.

No structural flaw requiring the approved implementation to stop was established by this research. Fix an observed contradiction in the existing native route if one appears; otherwise proceed with the release dependencies and apply agreed form refinements to that consumer. Do not relabel functional primitive coverage as finished Spore-like richness, and do not postpone all useful phone delivery until final art.
