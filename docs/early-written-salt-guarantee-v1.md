## 1. Exactly which statement makes the promise

Expand the normal canonical **authored** book grammar, including authored compound components, before
adding unwritten sources. A component qualifies only when its canonical source ID is `salt`, its explicit
target is `substrate`, intensity is not Absent, and it does not negate its own Substrate contribution.
Salt merely contributing incidentally to ground while bound to another target does not qualify. Salt on
Hydrology or Vitality retains its current pressure meaning without this resource guarantee. Unwritten Salt
also remains ordinary. Do not change any pressure coefficient or existing denial effect to implement this.

Local denial/Absent excludes that component's promise; it does not cancel a separate positive qualifying
component. Direct and compound forms share one canonical Salt identity. One or more qualifying components
produce exactly **one promised Salt Crust per world**, regardless of repeated words, Count, Scale or
Intensity. Those modifiers retain their other current pressure, size and Writing-cost effects.

| Authored case | Salt promise |
|---|---|
| Salt explicitly on Substrate, positive/non-Absent | One deposit |
| Same component through a compound | Same one-deposit promise |
| Several qualifying Salt components | One deposit total |
| Salt on Hydrology or Vitality, or unwritten Salt | None; existing pressure/ordinary rules |
| Only Absent or locally Substrate-negated Salt | None |
| Separate positive Salt alongside a locally denied/Absent component | Positive component still qualifies |

## 2. The Salt-specific physical permission

Prefer an existing valid exposed Salt Crust site that meets the final route constraints. Otherwise the
promise may establish **one authored salt-crust surface deposit**, at its one final anchor, over empty,
dry, unfrozen, unsubmerged ordinary soil (Loam/Clay-soil) or sand. Require known ordinary-water chemistry,
a passable entry-connected site and no conflicting authored/protected surface. This is the named resource
itself on a small exposed patch; it is not a 20%-of-ground declaration or a salt-water request.

This authored deposit **does not require ambient salinity >=60, root water <25, shoreline adjacency or a
successful ordinary 50%/35% roll**. Those remain the unchanged conditions for ordinary Salt. Writing the
material into the ground supplies this one local physical Salt occurrence; making the entire world saline
is unnecessary. Do not counterfeit an ordinary regional host receipt to make a fresh region pass its old
salinity predicate. Preserve a distinct `written_salt_deposit` proof with the qualifying component IDs,
canonical promise identity, parent region/base substrate, exact anchor and frozen generation policy/plan.
An already-resolved eligible Salt source is reused and credited once, not copied into an extra source.

The new proof permits exactly this typed Salt deposit through source/custody validation independently of
the ordinary salinity threshold. It contains no invented recession event, ordinary random success, animal
source or derived inventory stock. Retain the underlying bedrock, base ground, elevation, actual water and
regional chemistry. The selected surface is exposed salt crust, so it cannot simultaneously count as an
ordinary growing root or a harvestable plant. Update the affected site/final host and habitat facts using
the actual crust surface; keep neighbouring roots and unrelated random region/formation rolls unchanged.
Preserve these facts through downstream region adapters, just as the source's saved parent geology is kept.

Do not drain/freeze/thaw water, change elevation, fill chasms, create access corridors, expose submerged
crust, replace stone/snow/ice/mud/ash/broken/unknown substrate, erase an existing plant/source/object, or
consume an arrival, return, teaching, traveller or other protected/promised site. Optional flora/population
comes later and cannot occupy the selected anchor or invalidate its route. No whole-region repainting,
climate rewrite, extra formation roll or source-per-colour variation is permitted.

Use the existing passable underfoot hand-gather producer: one successful world action gives **2 ungraded
Salt**, then the source is depleted. No tool purchase, harvesting tier, extra hit, regrowth or free stock
comes from the guarantee. The player must reach the source and gather normally.

Reserve exactly **one existing loose-earth slot** for this written source before ordinary Clay and Salt
allocation, without enlarging either loose-earth or mineral budgets. It satisfies the ordinary one-Salt
reservation when that reservation could otherwise participate; do not add a second Salt after crediting it.
Other authored guarantees remain jointly protected. Ordinary Clay and other reservations use remaining

counts and reasons. A greedy allocation miss is not proof that no joint assignment exists. Additional
ordinary placements follow only the existing allocator and remaining budget, never a new Salt bonus cache.

The source's **own standable anchor** is 3–8 shortest-path movement steps from entry in the final movement
graph, with all retained blockers present. It is not an adjacent mining work cell. Preserve this reachability
through later population, and keep return/teaching routes legal. There is no second-node distance rule.

## 4. Exact quote, physical failure and genuine written conflicts

rules. Do not reveal hidden coordinates or unrelated sources. Bind commits that same frozen page, seed,

If the full one-source promise cannot fit because no permitted physical host, route or budget exists,

Do not silently retain pressure only, omit the source, place it too close/far, expand the budget, or relax

Only a **genuine conflict between supported direct written guarantees** uses the already-settled rule:
approved transformation first, otherwise stronger resolved support, then stable frozen-receipt tie-break.

[Winning declaration] takes priority.”** A full map, random frozen terrain, a failed search or lack of
budget is not itself a competing direct declaration. Do not invent a new competitor, priority or global
compatibility engine. If the actual conflict cannot yet be resolved and disclosed by the supported owner,
hold that quote without spending; do not pretend compatibility is implemented.
