# Handmade art ownership — current boundary

**Status:** settled project authority · 11 August 2026  
**Owner:** Aimee owns final handmade art; Game Design owns gameplay meaning; Engineering owns native
integration; Asset Lead owns functional presentation and conformance within the boundary below.

## Final handmade-art authority

Aimee is the final visual author for:

- characters and travellers;
- buildings, stations and authored sites;
- weapons, armour, tools, inventory items and resources;
- sigils, writing marks and authored iconography;
- combat-node and technique glyph art.

A design identity brief specifies what an object or glyph must communicate, what it must not be
confused with, and which accessibility distinctions must survive. It does **not** transfer visual
authorship or approve generated proof art as the final asset.

## Asset Lead boundary

For the handmade categories above, the Asset Lead may create only plainly provisional functional
placeholders needed to prove:

- dimensions, occupancy, cropping and responsive layout;
- state layering, selection, focus, labels and hit targets;
- color-independent readability and literal-grayscale behavior;
- stable-ID wiring, manifests, adapter conformance and fallbacks.

The Asset Lead does not aesthetically iterate those placeholders, propose a competing final
pictogram language, or treat acceptance of a layout/conformance sheet as approval of its pictured
art. Every proof and manifest must label such imagery **placeholder** until Aimee supplies or
approves the final handmade pack.

The Asset Lead may continue autonomous work on functional UI, accessibility, layout, conformance
and generated-world color support. Generated-world terrain/flora systems remain governed by their
own current camera, disclosure and world-diversity contracts rather than this authored-art list.

## Cross-lead handoff

1. Game Design authors semantic requirements and collision boundaries without drawing the final
   solution by proxy.
2. Aimee supplies or approves an immutable versioned handmade asset pack/mapping.
3. Engineering consumes stable IDs and preserves a labelled functional fallback when an authored
   asset is absent; it never substitutes a catalogue SF Symbol or generated candidate as final.
4. AssetLab may validate the exact pack in all required contexts, but validation cannot revise its
   art.

No roadmap entry may say that the Asset Lead owns final silhouettes, item identities, station art
or glyph execution. Historical logs may retain earlier wording as provenance, but current sources
must route through this document.
