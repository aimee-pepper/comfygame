# Write Iron into the ground: first callable resource guarantee

> **5 September resource-occupancy clarification:** Aimee requires one tile, one node/resource across every resource pass, including guarantees and reservations. This contract never grants a per-pass stacking allowance. Preserve required quantities, legal hosts, protected content and reachability using distinct resource coordinates. See `field-tool-gesture-and-resource-occupancy-2026-09-05.md`. Implementation correction remains pending.

**Decided intended binding · 4 September 2026.** This closes only the existing Iron→Substrate vocabulary for newly generated early worlds. It implements the accepted common-resource cluster, budget credit, reachable distances and truthful pre-Bind quote in the structural/host packets. Coal, Clay and Resin do not gain callable pressure-source rows. No general declaration grammar, vocabulary acquisition, Page-space or ink-cost redesign is introduced.

## 1. Trigger and quantity

Read authored sigils from the normal canonical book expansion, including authored compound components, **before adding unwritten rolls**. A component qualifies iff its canonical source is Iron, its explicitly bound target is Substrate, its intensity is not Absent, and that component does not negate Substrate. Iron merely contributing incidentally to Substrate while bound elsewhere does not qualify. A negated component suppresses its own promise, not another independent positive component: preserve the existing local denial semantics. An Absent component likewise does not silently negate another positive declaration.

One or more qualifying components produce exactly **one two-node Iron cluster per world**, deduplicated by canonical resource identity. Direct and compound statements share that identity. Repetition, Count, Scale and Intensity do not multiply the guaranteed nodes, alter their harvest tier/yield or create bonus clusters; their existing pressure, world-size, cost and grammar effects remain unchanged. Unwritten Iron remains ordinary. Legacy books without the new guarantee policy retain their pressure-only interpretation; do not retrofit resources into saved worlds. Counterfactual removal of authored Iron also removes this guarantee while preserving unrelated frozen rolls and their selected policies.

Each node is the existing blocking Iron producer: Pick 1, three successful hits, two Iron per hit. Two nodes mean twelve finite Iron in total. Both count against the ordinary mineral budget; they satisfy its one-Iron ordinary reservation, rather than adding two more after that reservation. Other required reservations and optional minerals spend only the remaining budget. A matching already-resolved written Iron placement counts once; no duplicate credit or duplicated body.

## 2. Permitted host preparation

First use existing eligible exposed dry stone/rubble faces in one compatible resolved region. Mark that region's formation iron-bearing through the existing written-formation binding. Preserve compatible Granite, Sandstone or Basalt; Iron does not demand a particular one or reroll unrelated geology.

If sufficient exposed faces are absent, the Iron guarantee may create the **minimum local exposed outcrop, at most the two node-anchor cells**, from otherwise unoccupied, dry, unfrozen ordinary soil (including its Loam/Clay realization and ordinary grass/groundcover surface). Those cells become exposed stone over their compatible existing bedrock. Do not create extra connector rock cells or repaint the whole region. The two finished anchors must belong to the same final compatible region; for a new isolated two-cell outcrop, use cardinally adjoining anchors with compatible elevation/root-region facts. An existing exposed region may supply either or both faces.

Exposure happens before final regional host/fact and population allocation, so all affected roots, capacity, legal sites and routes are recalculated from the resulting physical surface. A converted cell no longer counts as ordinary growing soil. Persist its parent geology/formation consistently through final region resolution; do not infer a new random bedrock from a changed region anchor. Unrelated region/formation rolls stay frozen. The change is a local exposed-face operation, not excavation gameplay or a whole-map substrate conversion.

Never drain or freeze water, thaw ice, change elevation, fill a chasm, alter a slope, erase a standing object/source, delete a Clay deposit, overwrite a portal/arrival/teaching/traveller/writing reservation, or contradict a winning explicit ground/liquid guarantee to make Iron fit. Do not expose a frozen or submerged mineral face. The resulting two blocking nodes must preserve the required return and teaching routes and all other retained guarantees. Optional population is placed afterward; it cannot steal the reserved outcrop or working positions. If no allowed complete two-node assignment exists, use the preflight disposition below, not broader terrain surgery.

## 3. Distances mean the route to the work

Measure graph distances using the final traversable layout with both Iron nodes and all retained blocking sources present. Choose and retain a legal adjacent work position for each node. The first work position is **3–8 ordinary movement steps from entry**; the second is **at most four movement steps from the first work position**, through the same entry-connected graph. A shared legal work position is allowed. These are shortest-path step counts, not screen, Manhattan-through-obstacles or mining-turn distances. Keep each source's existing height/access and Pick rules. Both nodes share one final compatible region even though the walking route may cross a regional boundary. Never count the blocking node's own cell as the operator position.

## 4. Preflight, conflicts and spend

Before spending for Bind, prepare and validate the exact deterministic world plan and complete two-node assignment. Quote **“Iron: 2 reachable deposits · Pick 1”** when that full promise is feasible; show tool wording only through the existing knowledge/disclosure rules. Do not disclose hidden coordinates or unrelated sources. Bind commits the same seed, policy, plan and quoted resolution atomically; a changed page/seed/plan invalidates the old quote and needs recomputation before spending. Failed generation or save spends nothing and does not grant stock or a partial world.

If physical capacity, protected content or route constraints prevent the two-node promise **without a competing direct written guarantee**, refuse pre-Bind as infeasible. Suggested copy: **“This page cannot place two reachable Iron deposits. Change the page before Binding.”** Do not automatically reroll, offer one node as equivalent, silently retain pressure only, or charge for discovering the failure after entry.

If **two direct written guarantees cannot coexist**, retain the already-settled compatibility rule: authorized transformation first, then greater resolved support, then the stable frozen-receipt tie-break. The quote names the winner and which declaration remains an influence before the player's normal Bind action. If Iron loses, say **“Iron influences the ground; deposits are not guaranteed. [Winning declaration] takes priority.”** There is no promised reduced one-node cluster. Do not reject a valid resolved world merely because its raw declarations conflicted, invent a new priority to make Iron win, or call random terrain/protected content a competing direct declaration. Where that exact conflict resolution is not implemented, do not pretend it exists: hold the affected quote before spend until it can truthfully name the resolved promise. This Iron-first binding does not introduce new callable declarations.
