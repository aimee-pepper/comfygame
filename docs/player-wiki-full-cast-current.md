# The travellers of Bookbinder

This is publishable Player Wiki copy for the current 29-person cast. It follows campaign order without
exposing hidden people before the game does.

## Spoiler and publication boundaries

- Show a traveller's name, calling and meeting context after the Library names them as someone to seek.
- Show each world hint only after its matching diary page has been read.
- Show a service after recruitment and its Home destination becomes visible.
- Show a teaching, schematic, pattern or site only after that exact page has been read.
- A relationship page may name its subject only after the page reveals that person.
- File presence, search indexing or an unrecovered portrait never reveals hidden campaign content.
- Page titles below use the Library's visible categories. The recovered page remains the source for its
  full authored passage.

## Progression summary

- **Opening:** trade, salvage, foundational gear, surveying and sites turn early materials into choices.
- **Middle:** writing hands, fieldcraft, formation, preparation, flexible gear, reach and Library
  cross-reference broaden how worlds are read and survived.
- **Specialists:** advanced armour, bows, weapons, natural history and mature formation reward deliberate
  late-campaign books.
- **Late infrastructure:** Menagerie, Deep Works, Channelworks, Distillery and Anchorage give difficult
  resources and recovered worlds lasting uses.
- **Endgame:** Ashe, Perren and Nine ask what bodily cost, interpretation and continuity mean after the
  player understands the ordinary systems.

No diary is required to recruit its author. Location pages and relationship clues help find people;
completing a book rewards attention after discovery rather than withholding the person.
