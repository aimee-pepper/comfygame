# Current Design — Five-Condition Traveller Signatures

**Status:** authored-direction profiles with playtest thresholds. This band covers Bracken, Fen, Wren,
Kestrel and Maud. All are writable from earlier/starter/research vocabulary and avoid their owner's
exclusive reward.

## Bracken — what remains protective under strain

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 60 | “Most exposed surfaces resist a tool before they reveal a mark.” | Granite |
| Thermal peak | ≥ 68 | “At the hottest interval, fittings expand far enough to change their seat.” | Sun combined with Ice |
| Thermal floor | ≤ 32 | “At the coldest interval, the same fittings draw tight and brittle.” | Ice combined with Sun |
| Relief verticality | ≥ 30 | “Weight rests differently at every ledge; nothing can be protected from one direction alone.” | Great Granite or Chasm |
| Atmosphere peak | ≥ 62 | “The air presses into every join that was left imperfect.” | Cloud |

**Identity:** hard material is tested by heat, cold, load direction and environmental pressure. This
is a world about protection under changing strain, not merely “lots of armour material.”

**Reward dependency:** never requires Bone, Bracken's diary focus.

## Fen — distance held in tension

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relief openness | ≥ 80 | “A released thing can travel until distance, rather than terrain, finally spends it.” | Sea or Sand |
| Atmosphere motion | ≥ 55 | “Every long line bows before it settles, even when both ends are fixed.” | Wind |
| Vitality dispersion | ≤ 35 | “Growth gathers in separate stands with clear space between them.” | Bloom |
| Hydrology dispersion | ≤ 30 | “Water collects into broad obstacles instead of following every path.” | Sea or Lake |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 82 | “The same conditions return often enough to compare one release with the next.” | Orrery |

**Identity:** open distance, crosswind, separated living stands, concentrated water and repeatable
conditions create a world where tension and release can be observed rather than romanticised.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Silk, Fen's diary focus.

## Wren — motion that preserves exits

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relief openness | ≥ 72 | “There is usually another line to take before the first one closes.” | Sand, Sea or Wind |
| Atmosphere motion | ≥ 60 | “The air never finishes one movement before beginning the next.” | Wind |
| Hydrology dispersion | ≥ 72 | “Shallow water interrupts nearly every route without becoming one impassable body.” | Rain |
| Vitality peak | ≥ 65 | “Enough lives here that clear space becomes occupied again almost immediately.” | Great Bloom or stacked producers |
| Cycle amplitude | ≥ 62 | “Quiet stretches give way to bursts large enough to rearrange the whole route.” | Tide |

**Identity:** a crowded, shifting world where mobility is not speed for its own sake; it is the work
of keeping another option available.

**Reward dependency:** never requires `Foes present ≥ 3`, Wren's diary teaching.

## Kestrel — enough evidence to support a claim

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Vitality produced | ≥ 40 | “Fresh growth appears beneath the older feeding marks.” | Root + Bloom |
| Vitality peak | ≥ 65 | “No single set of tracks accounts for everything using this ground.” | Producers plus Herd |
| Vitality trophic depth | ≥ 50 | “Marks overlap: grazing, pursuit, scavenging, and smaller traces inside all three.” | Producer-supported Herd |
| Relief openness | ≤ 45 | “Evidence disappears behind folds of ground before the thing that made it must.” | Granite/Ruin |
| Atmosphere clarity | ≥ 76 | “Fine disturbance remains visible at a distance instead of dissolving into haze.” | Hush raises clarity; avoid Mist/Miasma |

**Identity:** a deep living system leaves abundant but incomplete evidence. Kestrel is found by reading
relationships among traces, not by generating a guaranteed rare species.

**Reward dependency:** never requires `Foe: unrecorded species`, Kestrel's diary teaching.

## Maud — material fitted to a changing body

| Condition | Threshold | Honest sensory passage | Available route |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Substrate hard form | ≥ 40 | “Part of the ground holds an edge instead of folding under it.” | Granite |
| Substrate ductile form | ≥ 35 | “Another part bends before it breaks and keeps the shape it was given.” | Iron or Gold ore alongside Granite |
| Relief verticality | ≥ 30 | “Balance changes from ledge to ledge; the same length is wrong in a different stance.” | Great Granite/Chasm |
| Atmosphere motion | ≤ 20 | “Loose measures stay where they are laid while the body moves around them.” | Hush |
| Cycle regularity | ≥ 85 | “The interval returns precisely enough to test whether an adjustment truly helped.” | Orrery |

**Identity:** hard and ductile materials coexist under changing stance but repeatable conditions. The
world supports Maud's attention to fit without introducing a fitted-equipment subsystem.

**Reward dependency:** never requires Maud's fitting-pattern teaching.

## Validation notes

- Bracken's heat and cold conditions intentionally create a large thermal range. They do not require
  a negate qualifier and therefore are not automatically a contradiction.
- Fen and Wren both use openness and motion for opposite purposes: Fen studies a repeatable release
  across distance; Wren preserves options inside continual change.
- Kestrel uses `produced` as well as headline Vitality so consumers cannot imply a food web with no
  base. No condition guarantees a particular creature.
- Maud's two form shares require at least one hard and one ductile source. Thresholds allow a mixed
  substrate rather than demanding two simultaneous majorities.
